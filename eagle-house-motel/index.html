Charming Accommodations in the Heart of Rockport

Eagle House Motel offers comfortable, newly renovated rooms just steps from Main Street and a short walk to historic Bearskin Neck. Under new management as of 2025, we provide the same great level of service guests have come to expect, with continuous improvements based on your feedback.

Great Location

On a quiet side street in downtown Rockport. Front Beach is a 500-foot walk. Less than 1/4 mile to Bearskin Neck shops, galleries, and restaurants.

Comfortable Rooms

Traditional motel rooms, apartment-style guest rooms, and a three-bedroom guest house. All rooms have private bathrooms, daily housekeeping, and on-site staff.

Seasonal Property

2026 season: April 3–November 14. Office hours 9 AM–5 PM. Phone available 9 AM–8 PM. Reservations accepted year-round.

Rooms & Accommodations

From cozy motel rooms to spacious apartments and a private guest house, we have the perfect stay waiting for you.

Standard Rooms

Two double beds, 3/4 bath, cable TV, air-conditioner, mini-fridge, microwave, coffee maker, free WiFi. 260 square feet. Shared balcony with two chairs. Four rooms available.

King Rooms

One king bed, 3/4 bath, small table with two chairs, cable TV, air-conditioner, mini-fridge, microwave, coffee maker, free WiFi. 260 square feet. Shared balcony. Four rooms available.

Petite Queen Rooms

Compact and comfortable at 160 square feet. Accommodates two adults. 3/4 bath, cable TV, air-conditioner, mini-fridge, microwave, coffee maker, free WiFi. Shared balcony. Two rooms available.

Three-Bedroom Guest House

Beautifully renovated house next door to the motel. 3 bedrooms, 2 baths, kitchen, living room, large patio, driveway parking for 2 cars. Rented weekly in season; nightly off-season.

Check-in & Parking

Check-in: 3:00 PM · Check-out: 11:00 AM

We offer Express Check-in. We'll charge your card 24 hours before arrival and text you when your room is ready with your door code. Keyless entry means you can go straight to your room—no need to stop at the office.

Parking: Complimentary but limited, first-come first-served. One vehicle per room. Overnight street parking available on Cleaves and Hale Streets.

Explore Rockport & Cape Ann

Bearskin Neck

Less than a 1/4 mile walk. Art galleries, local shops, restaurants, ice cream, and The Hideaway—our oceanfront cocktail bar with sunset views.

Front Beach

Just 500 feet from the motel. Enjoy a day on the ocean without beach passes. Pack up and walk—it's that easy.

Shalin Liu Performance Center

Steps from the motel. Impressive music venue with shows rivaled only by the stunning ocean views.

Gloucester

10-minute drive to America's oldest seaport. Fishing, whale watches, restaurants, and beaches like Wingaersheek, Good Harbor, and Long Beach. Beach passes required.

Essex & Ipswich

Known for beaches, antique shops, craft breweries, and a meadery. Don't forget to try the fried clams!
