Explore Anacortes & Beyond

Your gateway to the San Juan Islands and Pacific Northwest adventures

Perfectly Positioned

The Ship Harbor Inn sits at the top of the hill above the Washington State ferry terminal, providing the perfect base for exploring the San Juan Islands, Vancouver Island, and the greater Anacortes area.

San Juan Islands

Take the ferry to Friday Harbor, Orcas Island, Lopez Island, and more. World-class whale watching, kayaking, cycling, and island charm await.

Downtown Anacortes

Historic downtown is just minutes away. Explore independent shops, art galleries, waterfront restaurants, and the maritime heritage of this classic Pacific Northwest town.

Outdoor Recreation

Hiking and biking trails, parks, beach access, and scenic viewpoints are all nearby. Deception Pass State Park is a short drive with dramatic bridge views and coastal trails.
