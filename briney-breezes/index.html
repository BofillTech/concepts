Welcome

A family-run Montauk beach resort by the ocean.

Welcome to Hartman's Briney Breezes, a Montauk beach hotel on Old Montauk Highway, overlooking the Atlantic Ocean and just steps from the beach.

Our bright Montauk studios and suites offer ocean views, spacious decks, and in-room kitchens, with an outdoor pool, BBQ and picnic areas, and plenty of space to relax. And with downtown Montauk just minutes away, you're close to restaurants, shops and everything you need for a Montauk getaway.
