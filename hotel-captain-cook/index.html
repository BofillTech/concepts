557 rooms and suites offering stunning views of Cook Inlet and the Chugach Mountains in the heart of downtown Alaska

Better Than Home

Located in the heart of downtown Anchorage, Alaska, every one of the Hotel Captain Cook's 557 rooms and suites offer stunning views of the neighboring Cook Inlet or Chugach Mountains. As Anchorage's only Preferred Hotel, we deliver exceptional service and unmatched amenities.

Elegantly Appointed Accommodations

Deluxe Rooms

Available with two double beds or one king, all deluxe rooms offer gorgeous views of downtown Anchorage, Cook Inlet, or the Chugach Mountains.

  • Air conditioning and ceiling fan
  • Windows that open
  • In-room safe and complimentary Wi-Fi
  • Access to Athletic Clubs

Junior Suites

Separate sleeping and living areas furnished with a sofa, desk and two televisions. Spread out and enjoy the views with extra space.

  • Separate living area
  • Two televisions
  • Blackout drapes
  • Complimentary Wi-Fi

Crow's Nest Suites

Perched atop Tower III, our signature suites offer spacious accommodations with large parlors and sweeping views of downtown, Cook Inlet, and the mountains.

  • One- and two-bedroom options
  • Large parlors with sweeping views
  • Individually appointed
  • Secured floor access

Distinctive Dining

Our five distinct restaurants offer something for everyone, from casual breakfast to fine dining with panoramic views at the Crow's Nest. Experience Alaska's finest culinary offerings without leaving the hotel.
