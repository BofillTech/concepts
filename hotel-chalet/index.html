A Legacy on the Rails

Hospitality has graced the grounds of the Chattanooga Choo Choo since 1974, when the historic Terminal Station first opened its doors to guests. Today, The Hotel Chalet honors this rich heritage, paying homage to the golden age of rail travel while delivering modern boutique luxury.

Nestled between the Tennessee River and the foothills of Appalachia, we are the anchor to the iconic Chattanooga Choo Choo—a Michelin Guide One Key Hotel where every detail celebrates the spirit and romance of the rails.

Alpine Pool

Dive into our year-round heated Alpine pool, framed by vintage globe lampposts and the iconic Terminal Station dome. Open daily from 7 AM to 10 PM, the pool deck offers green-striped loungers, umbrellas, and a sun-drenched escape in the heart of Chattanooga.

Modern Comforts

Every stay includes complimentary coffee, Bellino Italian linens, The Botanist & The Chemist bath amenities, Waterworks fixtures, 55" smart TV with casting, Bluetooth speakers, USB-C charging, in-room safe, and refrigerator. Our 24/7 boutique fitness center features Echelon equipment, while the Lobby Cafe serves local pastries from 7 AM to noon.

The Caboose

Gather around the fire pit in The Caboose, our courtyard bar where the vintage red caboose sets the scene for evening cocktails under the stars. With cushioned outdoor sofas and string lights, it's the perfect spot to unwind after exploring Chattanooga.

Events & Gatherings

Host unforgettable events on our garden lawn with the Terminal Station dome as your backdrop. The Beverly Ballroom, The Arbor, and our outdoor spaces accommodate weddings, meetings, and celebrations. Lawn games and a board game library add playful touches to any gathering.

Elsie's Daughter

Our Appalachian bistro honors Donn Barber—architect of Terminal Station—and his wife Elsie through three generations of namesakes. Elsie's Daughter serves dinner daily and weekend brunch, blending French-Belgian influences with Appalachian terroir. Join us for two daily happy hours at the curved wood bar beneath pendant lights.

Bistro Dining

Settle into our intimate banquettes for a meal that celebrates regional ingredients with European technique. Patterned upholstery, marble tables, and natural light create an atmosphere where every dish tells a story of place and craft.
