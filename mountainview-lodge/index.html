Family-Owned Montana Hospitality

Mountain View Lodge is a locally family owned hotel based in the heart of Bozeman Montana. Our promise to you is exceptional service, clean rooms, and a family friendly atmosphere with great amenities. We strive for the best service and value and look forward to meeting you.

Centrally located, close to the airport and within easy driving distance to many local restaurants, bars and shops. Just 20 minutes from world-class skiing at Big Sky and Bridger Bowl, and a scenic drive to Yellowstone National Park.
