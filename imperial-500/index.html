Family Owned & Operated

The same families come back, year after year.

The Imperial 500 has been family owned and operated for over 30 years, serving generations of happy families. What sets it apart, in the owners’ own words: a personal touch, friendliness, and most importantly — cleanliness.

It shows in the guest book. The motel is ranked #2 of 63 hotels in Wildwood Crest on Tripadvisor, with 354 of its 388 reviews rating the stay Excellent. Guests write about spotless rooms and grounds, owners who treat them like family, oldies playing on the deck, and traditions running twenty and thirty summers deep.
