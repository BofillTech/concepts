What we manufacture

We don’t order your boxes from
somewhere else. We make them.

Hudson Paper started as an industrial paper company and turned to retail packaging in the 1970s. The corporate office, the factory and the warehouse are all the same address in Stratford — production on site, with an extensive printing infrastructure that runs from ink-based to hot press.

1908

Four generations

The Wilk family opened this company
the year Ford built the Model T.

  1. 1908

    The Wilk family opens Hudson Paper as an industrial paper company.

  2. 1970s

    The focus turns to retail packaging — boxes, bags, wrap, ribbon and tissue for the people selling the gift, not just shipping it.

  3. Today

    Fourth-generation Brian Wilk is president. Corporate office, factory and warehouse in Stratford, Connecticut, with a permanent showroom in Dallas and customers across North America.
