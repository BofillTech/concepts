Grants Pass, Oregon

Your basecamp for Rogue River adventure

Hellgate Jetboat Excursions leave from one block away. Book a white-water float on the Wild and Scenic Rogue, cast for salmon and steelhead with a local guide, or sip your way through the Applegate Valley wine trail.

Crater Lake, the Oregon Caves and the ancient redwoods are all within a scenic day trip.

Local tip: skip the car. Downtown is a half-mile riverside walk.
