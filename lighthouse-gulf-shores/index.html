Resort Amenities

A day at The Lighthouse.

Morning

Straight from your balcony to the sand.

Every boardwalk leads to 635 feet of private beach — with outdoor showers and hoses waiting to rinse off the sugar-white sand on the way back.

  • 635-foot private Gulf beach
  • Boardwalks with outdoor showers
  • Private balconies on every unit
Afternoon

Three pools, two hot tubs, zero arguments.

Two private Gulf-front pools — one a zero-entry pool with four waterspouts where kids walk right in at ground level — plus a heated indoor pool for year-round swims. Restrooms, changing rooms, and lockers sit right at the pool decks.

  • Two Gulf-front pools, one zero-entry
  • Heated indoor pool, open year-round
  • Two huge outdoor hot tubs
Evening

Grill out, work out, or head into town.

Fire up the charcoal BBQ grills, hit the Gulf-view fitness room with sauna and panoramic waterfront views, or explore Gulf Shores — golf, fishing, kayaking, outlet malls, amusement parks, and restaurants from casual seafood to fine dining, all nearby.

  • Gulf-view fitness room with sauna
  • Two charcoal BBQ grills
  • Three-story covered parking · four elevators
