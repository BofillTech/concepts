The hub of the activities

The FairBridge Inn – Coeur d'Alene provides affordable hotel stays at the hub of the activities. Conveniently located on Appleway Drive at the intersection of Interstate 90 and Highway 95, we'll suit your needs with a comfortable room, free internet and a free breakfast buffet.

Whether you're visiting for work or play, the small city by the big lake has it all — magnificent sunrises and sunsets, mountains to hike or bike, golf, eagle watch cruises, fishing and boating, plus great shopping, microbrews and eateries.

Silverwood & Boulder Beach

The largest amusement park in the Pacific Northwest — 70+ rides plus a water park. Ask the front desk about discounted tickets.

Downtown

McEuen Park

22.5 acres beside the lake with the city's largest splash pad, courts, a dog park and the Tubbs Hill trailhead.

Ride out

The Centennial Trail

A 24-mile paved path along the lake and the Spokane River — plus the 72-mile Trail of the Coeur d'Alenes.

On the lake

Cruises & Boating

Eagle watch cruises in winter; pontoons, kayaks and charter fishing all summer long.
