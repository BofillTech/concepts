Before you step aboard.

In the price

  • Rods, reels & tackle — light or heavy, plus lures
  • Your fishing license — nothing to buy beforehand
  • Catch cleaning & filleting — you keep what you catch
  • A working first mate — a 20% cash tip is customary

Good to know

  • Trips are open to the whole family — bring the kids, and ask ahead about child-sized life vests
  • Bring bottled water, sunglasses and non-spray sunblock. Snacks help — nobody enjoys an empty stomach at hour six
  • Beer and wine in moderation are fine; no hard liquor and no glass bottles
  • Some species have seasons or size limits — the Captain will keep you inside the regulations
