Tucked away just steps from Amagansett Village, The Reform Club offers a timeless retreat where luxury and leisure intertwine. Known as one of the best hotels in the Hamptons, our resort offers a seaside retreat with boutique accommodations all within walking distance of Main Street's restaurants, shops and cafés.

Amagansett Awaits

The Reform Club is just off Montauk Highway in the hamlet of Amagansett, a little further down Long Island from East Hampton. The hotel is perfectly situated on Windmill Lane between hidden farm stands brimming with local treasures, buzzy restaurants and bars and iconic surf spots.
