An Oceanfront Escape in Historic Cape May

Ocean Club Hotel blends boutique elegance with Cape May's coastal charm. Our eco-friendly property sits directly across from the beach on Beach Avenue, where every guest room opens to a private balcony or cabana with full or partial ocean views.

Recently renovated with serene cream, coral, and teal interiors, our rooms feature scalloped headboards and thoughtful details that invite relaxation. Whether you're lounging by our signature cobalt pool, savoring fresh seafood at SeaSalt Restaurant, or strolling Cape May's Victorian streets, you'll find the perfect balance of comfort and coastal adventure.
