## Story / positioning (from site)
- Oceanfront boutique hotel in historic Cape May, "where Cape May luxury meets the waves." Every room has a private balcony or an attached private cabana, with a full or side (partial) ocean view.
- The site describes it as an eco-friendly boutique experience: conservation and recycling programs and "Evolve wireless energy systems."
- Rooms were renovated recently: the 2025 photo shoot shows new cream, coral and teal decor with scalloped headboards.

## Room types (from site)
- **Penthouse Floor** (6th floor, quiet floor, adults 18+ only): suites with one king bed and a separate living room with sofa bed, Double Queen Full Ocean View, Double Queen Side Ocean View. All have private balconies.
- **Cabana Rooms** (pool and sundeck level, walled terraces): king + sofa bed with full ocean view and sundeck access; two queens with full ocean view and sundeck access (an ADA version is available); king + sofa bed with a private garden terrace; two queens with a private garden terrace. Max 4 guests.
- **Full Ocean View** rooms · **Side Ocean View** rooms · **ADA-compliant** rooms (roll-in showers, grab bars, ADA-height beds, Braille signage) · a few "no-view" rooms by request.

## Amenities (from site)
31,000-gallon heated, deep-cobalt pool (open 9 AM to dusk in season; deep end and shallow end) · poolside chaises, umbrellas and towels · Tiki Ten35 poolside bar and restaurant (sometimes has live entertainment; open to the public) · SeaSalt Restaurant (seafood fine dining "without the fuss": breakfast 8-11:30, lunch 12-4, dinner 5 to close, in season; chef's table / private dining) · food and drink service at the pool, sundeck and beach · complimentary beach chairs, umbrellas, towels and beach tags (subject to availability) · free sand pails and shovels for kids · free self-parking behind the hotel (1 car per reservation) · smoke-free · no pets · family-friendly except the penthouse floor.

## Nearby (from site)
Cape May Lighthouse · Emlen Physick Estate · Cape May Historic District (Victorian homes) · Nature Center of Cape May · Washington Street Mall · whale watching and other tours.
