Other cleaning drones are flown.
SkySprayer flies.

Every other drone on the market puts an operator on the sticks for the whole job. SkySprayer turns the job into a mission: it builds a model of the facade, plans the spray path, and executes it — while your pilot supervises with a finger on pause.

STEP 01

Scan

Stereo vision and mmWave radar sweep the facade and build a working model of the surface — glass, stone, stucco, EIFS. Onboard AI detects every window and panel, even in glare radar-only sensors miss.

STEP 02

Plan

SkySprayer lays out a coverage path over the model and assigns a spray mode to each surface, so glass and masonry each get the right treatment in a single flight.

FULL-SURFACE WINDOW-ONLY WINDOW-AVOID
STEP 03

Clean

The drone flies the path on its own, holding a fixed standoff from the wall by radar the entire time. Your pilot supervises and can pause or take over instantly. Every pass is logged.

Built for the jobsite, not the showroom.

Heavy-lift hexacopter airframe, corrosion-treated hardware, and the details that decide whether a machine earns its keep on a wash crew.

Batteries that hot-swap

Dual 12S smart packs with ideal-diode power management. Swap one pack while the other carries the load and get back in the air in seconds — no pairing rules, no balance anxiety, no bench time between flights.

Radar that trusts nothing

mmWave radar holds a fixed standoff from the wall at all times — including against specular glass and mirrored curtain walls where cameras and lidar get fooled. The drone physically cannot drift into the building.

Your chemicals, your pump

Standard downstream injection and cam-and-groove fittings. Run the soaps and sealants you already buy through the soft-wash pump you already own. No proprietary solution, no captive consumables.

Tether-ready

Add the ground-power tether for effectively unlimited flight time on big commercial jobs — or fly on packs and hot-swap your way through the day. Your call, per job.

Every flight becomes a report.

The SkySprayer app syncs with the drone's flight log, so proof of work writes itself. Quote the job, fly the job, send the report — from one place.
