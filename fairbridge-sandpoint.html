A WARM NORTHWEST WELCOME

Traditional Pacific Northwest comfort, at a price you can afford

The FairBridge Inn & Suites wants you to experience the joy of this quaint town in traditional Pacific Northwest comfort. We provide the rooms and amenities you need — come visit Sandpoint and Ponderay, Idaho, and take in the splendor.

From the moment you first drive across the mile-long “Longbridge,” you know you're somewhere magnificent.

Sandpoint was recently voted “The Most Beautiful Small Town in America” by Rand McNally & USA Today — and every room here carries a bit of that place with it, in handcrafted custom log furniture built for a mountain-and-lake kind of trip.

EXPLORE SANDPOINT

Sandpoint has options for any season

Lake Pend Oreille

43 miles long and 1,150 feet deep in places — the fifth deepest lake in the country. City Beach downtown is free, and boat, jet ski, and pontoon rentals are easy to find.

Schweitzer Mountain

2,900 acres of lift-served terrain and a 2,400-foot vertical drop. Ski Magazine called it "the best kept secret in North America" — in summer it's mountain biking and huckleberries.

Silverwood Theme Park

Over 65 rides and attractions, four roller coasters, and Boulder Beach Water Park — with new features most years, and Scarywood in October.

The Panida & downtown

A 1927 vaudeville house turned community cultural center, surrounded by the small-town shops and eateries downtown Sandpoint is known for.
