Southern Oregon coast hospitality

A home base for the wild, scenic coast.

Gold Beach sits where the Wild & Scenic Rogue River meets the Pacific and the Siskiyou Mountains, 35 miles north of the California border. Salmon fishing, hiking, rafting and jet boat tours start right here.

Come back to a porch rocker and the sunset, a soak in the hot tub, and the coast's only oceanfront outdoor theater lighting up the grounds after dark.

Every night at dusk

The Pacific Reef Adventure Theater & Light Show

The coast's first oceanfront outdoor theater. An opening film tours the parks, beaches and folklore of the southern Oregon coast, followed by a feature presentation that changes with the tides.

Watch from your patio or balcony, from the outdoor amphitheater seating, or from the bird's-eye view in our gathering room.

Free for all guests
