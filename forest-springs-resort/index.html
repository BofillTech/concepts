The Wellness Ritual

Sauna · Cold Plunge · Forest · Repeat

Nordic Sauna

A mirrored glass sauna reflecting the Ozark canopy. Open throughout your stay, whenever the forest calls you back.

Cold Plunge & Pool

Step from the sauna's heat into the cold plunge, then drift into the forest-view pool. An outdoor thermal circuit under the trees.

Red Light Therapy

Warm, soothing, restorative. No effort required. Sink into the glow and let it work.

In the Trees, Minutes from Town

Set on ten wooded acres, a five-minute drive from downtown Eureka Springs. Go into town for dining, galleries, and boutiques, then come back to the pool, the sauna, and the fire.

Once the historic Traveler's Inn, Forest Springs is a boutique forest retreat reimagined for couples who need a real break. The trolley stops right out front, too.
