The House

The porch is the lobby.

Guests relax and mingle on the large front porch, lined with rockers and a porch swing. Out back there's a new back porch, a ground-level deck, and a second-floor sun deck — and two oversized outdoor showers with changing rooms, especially inviting after a long day at the beach.

Renovated in great detail

Purchased in 2013 by owners Frank and Garrett, the property has been renovated throughout to preserve and enhance the character of the original Victorian house — gay owned and operated, and made to appeal to every generation.

  • Rooms painted in warm colors with plantation blinds
  • 32″ flat-screen TVs and room-size refrigerators
  • Daily room cleaning · free Wi-Fi throughout
  • Bike rack and beach-chair rack on the side yard
  • Guest kitchenette with ice machine & microwave, 24 hours

Breakfast · 8:30–10:00

Wake up to the chef's kitchen.

Each morning the staff puts together a hot buffet breakfast with all the amenities of a continental spread, served from the exquisite chef's kitchen. Take it at the round family-size dining table, out on the front porch, in the sun room, or up on the second-floor sun deck.

Coffee is available 24 hours a day — and the guest kitchenette, with its ice machine and microwave, never closes.
