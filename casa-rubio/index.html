Beachfront Resort · Private Gardens · Bird Watching Sanctuary

Casa Rubio is more than lodging—it's an experience of the California coast at its most pristine. Our property features a private beach, extensive gardens, and a designated bird-watching sanctuary where you can observe coastal and migratory species.

Private Beach

Your own secluded stretch of Pacific coastline. Explore tide pools, collect driftwood, watch the sunset, and listen to the waves beneath the stars.

Lush Gardens

Meandering paths through gardens designed to complement the coastal landscape. Native plants, seasonal blooms, and ocean breezes create a serene atmosphere.

Bird Watching Sanctuary

A designated sanctuary for coastal and migratory birds. Bring your binoculars and observe species in their natural habitat along the Pacific Flyway.

Explore the Coast

Tide Pooling

Discover sea stars, anemones, and hermit crabs in rocky tide pools at low tide.

Beach Walks

Miles of coastline to explore, from sandy shores to dramatic rocky outcrops.

Whale Watching

Gray whales migrate past our coast spring and fall. Watch from your deck or the beach.

Sunset Views

Unobstructed Pacific sunsets every evening, painting the sky and ocean in brilliant colors.

Smith River, California

Casa Rubio sits on the California coast near the Oregon border, where the Smith River meets the Pacific. This is one of the most beautiful and least crowded stretches of the West Coast.

Nearby

  • Smith River – California's last major undammed river, pristine and beautiful
  • Redwood National Park – Ancient groves of the world's tallest trees
  • Oregon Coast – Brookings and the southern Oregon coast just minutes north
  • Jedediah Smith Redwoods – Spectacular old-growth forest along the Smith River
  • Crescent City – Harbor town with dining, shopping, and services
  • Battery Point Lighthouse – Historic lighthouse accessible at low tide

Getting Here

Casa Rubio is located on Crissy Road off Highway 101, between Crescent City, California and Brookings, Oregon. Crescent City is about 10 minutes south, Brookings is about 15 minutes north. Both have groceries, restaurants, and services.
