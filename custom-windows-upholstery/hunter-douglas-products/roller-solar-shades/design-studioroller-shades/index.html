Design Studio™ roller shades

Design Studio™ Roller Shades offer an exclusive fabric collection in opaques and semi-opaques from NY designer Rebecca Atwood.

Book a free consultationCall (203) 225-9066

Showcase your on-trend simplicity with style

The beauty of a roller shade is its minimalistic, modern profile, making it the perfect design option for any décor aesthetic that calls for simple yet on-trend styling at the window. Our Design Studio™ Roller Shades—part of our exclusive Design Studio suite of products—are the ideal canvas for showcasing the whimsical patterns and fresh color palette of our unique fabric collection from NY designer Rebecca Atwood.

Key features

  • Exclusive Fabrics

    A collaboration with NY designer Rebecca Atwood brings her unique watercolor technique and fresh, modern color palette to eight eye-catching fabrics.

  • Styles

    A regular roll is standard, but a reverse roll is always an option.

  • Light Control

    All roller-shade fabrics are available in your choice of two opacities, opaque or semi-opaque.

  • Finish the Shade

    Headrail choices include either a small or large square cassette. You can also choose between a fabric-wrapped or unwrapped bottom rail.
