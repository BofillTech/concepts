Alustra® Woven Textures®

A design-forward option for those interested in elevating their space. Our Alustra® Woven Textures® artfully blend unique fibers sourced from around the globe.

Book a free consultationCall (203) 225-9066

Refined textures and style—our most luxurious window treatments

An innovative alternative to woven woods and solar screens, The Alustra® Collection of Woven Textures® is inspired by design. Fabrics—from French linen to lustrous charmeuse—are globally-sourced to create a truly elevated, premium window treatment. Alustra Woven Textures are available in Roller Shades, Roman Shades and Skyline® Gliding Window Panels.

Book a free consultation

Key features

  • Exclusive Fabrics

    Textiles in the Alustra Collection are made with unique fiber and texture combinations. Like a soft, lustrous silk or a nubby tweed, the materials offer depth and visual interest.

  • Roller Shades

    Alustra Roller Shades are available with a dual roller option that pairs any Woven Textures fabric with a rear roller shade, which can be operated as an independent liner.

  • Roman Shades

    Alustra Roman Shades are two shades in one. Offering total light control and privacy, our independent liner option provides versatility and functionality with the clean look of a single headrail.

  • Skyline Gliding Window Panels

    Alustra Skyline Gliding Window Panels are a patented twist on traditional panel track blinds. These contemporary panels are ideal for covering large windows or function as room dividers.
