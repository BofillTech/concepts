Solutions for bay, bow and corner windows

Bay and bow windows are a beautiful architectural feature that can make a typical flat wall become a picturesque element. Other benefits include added square footage for seating or decoration and allowing more natural light to enter the room. A bay window consists of three windows that are positioned to each other in an angled shape, whereas a bow window consists of four or more windows arranged in a curved shape. Window treatments for bay and bow windows are usually standard rectangular installations that are positioned to mimic the angled or curved shape of these types of windows. The individual windows and window coverings are independent of one another. The same applies to corner windows: They are usually standard rectangular installations that are positioned to meet in a corner. The individual windows and window coverings are independent of one another.
