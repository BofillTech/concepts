How a project works

One team from the first visit to the final adjustment.

  1. In-home consultation

    A decorator visits at a day and time that suits you, with samples, and talks through light, privacy and style.

  2. Professional measuring

    We measure every window and give you a written quote.

  3. Ordered and delivered

    Your treatments are ordered and delivered straight to your home.

  4. Certified installation

    Our installers fit everything, set up any automation, and show you how it works.
