Questions we hear often

What drapery styles can you make?

We design and create soft window solutions in a variety of styles, from a basic pinch pleat to a sophisticated top treatment, and we carry a large selection of fine fabrics.

Do you measure and install drapery?

Yes. We take care of measuring and installation. Our experienced installers are fully trained and will ensure a perfect fit for your new drapery, and they show you how everything works before they leave.

What areas do you serve?

We are based at 100 Beard Sawmill Rd, Suite 225 in Shelton, CT, and have served Fairfield County, the New Haven County shoreline, the New York metro area and beyond for over 25 years.
