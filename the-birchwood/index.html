An Experience Like No Other

Welcome to The Birchwood, a historic boutique inn in downtown St. Petersburg, Florida, located on iconic Beach Drive. Our beautifully restored 18-room hotel offers a unique luxury stay experience just steps from the St. Pete Pier, waterfront parks, museums, shopping, and the city's vibrant arts, dining, and nightlife scene.
