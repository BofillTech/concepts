In season, hour by hour

A Madison day

Most of what makes a stay here easy is already handled before you walk outside. Swipe through a summer day.

  1. 9am

    Your spot is waiting

    Beach attendants set up chairs and an umbrella every day from 9 to 5, at no charge with your stay.

  2. 10am

    Pick a pool

    The Royal Pool runs 2′ to 11′ with a shallow kiddie area. The Ocean Pool has a 1′ in-water lounge. Both are heated, with a hot tub.

  3. 11am

    Lunch comes to you

    Light bites, pizza and smoothies delivered to your chair at the pool or on the beach, 11 to 5 through Labor Day.

  4. 1pm

    Claim a cabana

    Full-day cabanas line the Ocean Pool, 9 to 8, for up to six guests, with food from Madison Rose and Vinyl during their hours.

  5. 4pm

    Cereal on your sundae

    Twist & Spoon opens Friday and Saturday evenings with frozen treats and a bottomless cereal wall.

  6. 8pm

    Firepits & the Ferris wheel

    Bring your own to the Ocean Tower firepits under bistro lights, or head up to the rooftop for a straight view of the Ferris wheel.

Pools, beach service and outdoor dining are seasonal. Ask the front desk for this year’s dates.

Four places to eat, no car needed

Now playing at Madison

Good to know before you pack

Already taken care of

  • Beach chairs & umbrella

    Set up daily, 9 am to 5 pm, through September. No charge with your stay.

  • Towel Card

    Swap for a fresh pool or beach towel at either pool tower, as often as you like.

  • Complimentary shuttle

    Around Wildwood and Wildwood Crest, plus rides to Harry’s Ocean Bar & Grille at our sister resort in Cape May.

  • Free valet

    For Ocean Tower guests, May 15 to September 15. Self-parking the rest of the year.

  • Fitness center

    Cardio and strength equipment on the first floor of the Ocean Tower.

  • Rooftop lounge

    Tables, seating and panoramic beach views. Bring your own drinks.
