Artisanal Desert Lodge

Welcome to The Classic Desert Aire Hotel, a local artisanal lodge nestled in the foothills of the Tularosa Basin. Our hotel is your home base for all of Alamogordo's nearby attractions, including the stunning White Sands National Park, just minutes away.

Experience authentic New Mexico hospitality with modern comfort. Start your day at The Classic Cafe with vibrant Southwestern flavors, browse authentic local art and jewelry at the White Sands Boutique, and relax in thoughtfully designed rooms that blend desert charm with contemporary amenities.

Whether you're exploring White Sands, hiking, skiing, or wine tasting, The Classic Desert Aire is your restful retreat where you can relax and recharge from the day's adventures.

The Classic Cafe

Discover the Taste of New Mexico at The Classic Cafe, our on-site restaurant open daily for breakfast and lunch. Savor the vibrant flavors of the Southwest with our authentic New Mexican cuisine, or start the day off right with our classic breakfast offerings.

Open weekdays and weekends from 6:30 AM to 11:00 AM. With dishes crafted specifically with your little ones in mind, it's safe to say we have something for the entire family.
