Safety isn't a selling point. It's the standard.

Fully insured and licensed in New York State and for interstate travel. Decades of experience. One standard of service.

  • Drivers hold commercial driver's licenses with passenger endorsements.
  • Qualified drivers meet New York State Article 19-A requirements for school transportation.
  • Vehicles are inspected regularly under New York State Department of Transportation requirements.
  • A full-service maintenance operation handles preventive maintenance, repairs and vehicle safety.
  • We send your driver's name and cell number before every trip, so you're never left wondering.

Questions groups ask us

How do I get a charter bus quote?

Use the Get a Quote form or call 914-788-1000, Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 4 PM. Share your date, passenger count, pickup, destination and itinerary, and our office will check availability and send a clear quote.

What size vehicles do you have?

Our fleet includes 56-passenger motor coaches (Prevost H3-45 and MCI J4500), 40-passenger Temsa TS35 midsize coaches, 24-passenger minibuses, 14-passenger Mercedes-Benz Sprinters and 13-passenger Mercedes-Benz Party Sprinters.

Where do you travel?

We serve Westchester, the Hudson Valley, New York City and the tri-state area, and we are licensed and insured to operate in all 48 contiguous states.

Are your drivers qualified for school trips?

Yes. Our qualified drivers meet New York State Article 19-A requirements for school-related transportation, and our motor coach drivers hold commercial licenses with passenger endorsements.

Is Hudson Valley Charter under new ownership?

Yes. Hudson Valley Charter is under new ownership and has merged with Accredited Limousine Service. The phone number and the commitment to service stay the same.
