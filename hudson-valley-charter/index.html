Safety isn't a selling point. It's the standard.

Fully insured and licensed in New York State and for interstate travel. Decades of experience. One standard of service.

  • Drivers hold commercial driver's licenses with passenger endorsements.
  • Qualified drivers meet New York State Article 19-A requirements for school transportation.
  • Vehicles are inspected regularly under New York State Department of Transportation rules.
  • Our own full-service maintenance team handles preventive maintenance, repairs and vehicle safety.
  • We send your driver's name and cell number before every trip, so you're never left wondering.
