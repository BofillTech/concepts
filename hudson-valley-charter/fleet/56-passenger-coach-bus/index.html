56-Passenger Motor Coach

Our flagship coaches for big groups, long hauls and days when everyone has luggage.

Get a Quote Call 914-788-1000

Overview

The 56-passenger motor coach is the workhorse of our fleet. We run Prevost H3-45 coaches (2020 and newer) and MCI J4500 coaches (2018 and newer), each with a white exterior and blue interior.

With a restroom on board, WiFi, monitors and large under-floor luggage bays, a full-size coach is built for trips where comfort matters for hours, not minutes: a weekend tournament upstate, a wedding with guests spread across hotels, or a multi-day tour to Niagara Falls or Boston.

Need more than 56 seats? We send several coaches and coordinate them as one move, with every driver working from the same itinerary.
