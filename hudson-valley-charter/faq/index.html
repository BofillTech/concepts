Booking and quotes

How do I get a charter bus quote?

Use the Get a Quote form or call 914-788-1000, Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 4 PM. Share your date, passenger count, pickup, destination and itinerary, and our office will check availability and send a clear quote.

How far ahead should I book?

As early as you can. Weekends in wedding, prom and school-trip season fill first. If your date is close, call us at 914-788-1000 to check availability.

What details do you need for a quote?

The date and times, passenger count, pickup address, destination, any stops, and whether the trip is one-way or round-trip. Notes about luggage, equipment or accessibility help us choose the right vehicle.

What are your office hours?

Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 4 PM. The office is closed Saturday and Sunday.

Can I add a vehicle after I book?

Usually, yes. Call us as soon as your passenger count changes so we can check availability.

How is pricing calculated?

Every quote is built around your trip: the date, hours, distance, vehicle and itinerary. Send the details through Get a Quote or call us, and our office will send a clear price.

Vehicles and amenities

What size vehicles do you have?

Our fleet includes 56-passenger motor coaches (Prevost H3-45 and MCI J4500), 40-passenger Temsa TS35 midsize coaches, 24-passenger minibuses, 14-passenger Mercedes-Benz Sprinters and 13-passenger Mercedes-Benz Party Sprinters.

Do your coaches have restrooms and WiFi?

Our 56-passenger motor coaches include a lavatory, WiFi, a DVD player and monitors, AM/FM radio, a PA system, reclining seats, reading lights and large luggage bays. Ask us about amenities on other vehicles.

Which vehicle is right for my group?

Up to 14 passengers usually fits a Mercedes-Benz Sprinter, up to 24 a minibus, up to 40 a midsize coach and up to 56 a full-size coach. For bigger groups we send several vehicles.

Can you move hundreds of people?

Yes. We send several coaches and coordinate them as one trip.

What happens in bad weather or traffic?

Our drivers and office stay in touch with your trip leader and adjust the route when weather or traffic changes the plan. Customers have written about drivers rerouting around accidents and storms to arrive on time.

Safety and credentials

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We carry the insurance and licensing required to operate in New York State and all 48 contiguous states.

Are your drivers qualified for school trips?

Yes. Our qualified drivers meet New York State Article 19-A requirements for school-related transportation, and our motor coach drivers hold commercial licenses with passenger endorsements.

How are your vehicles maintained?

Our vehicles undergo regular inspections under New York State Department of Transportation requirements, supported by a full-service maintenance operation focused on preventive maintenance, repairs and vehicle safety.

Will I know who my driver is?

Yes. Before departure we send your driver's name and cell number, so you are never left wondering.
