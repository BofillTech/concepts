Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know about our one-day Regents and AP review courses at Farmingdale State College.

Each course is a single, focused day — 9:30 AM to 3:00 PM, with a 30-minute lunch break. Students review the major topics, work real practice questions, and learn test-taking strategy, all in one sitting.

Every course is taught by an experienced, New York State-certified public-school teacher. Long Island Regents Prep is teacher-owned and operated — it's the whole reason we exist.

On the campus of Farmingdale State College, in Gleeson Hall. It's a professional, distraction-free setting with free parking that's easy to reach from anywhere in Nassau and Suffolk County. See the map and directions.

Just something to write with, and any calculator or reference materials allowed on their exam. We provide the review packet and practice materials to keep.

Register online through our course pages. We accept all major credit cards, and we're expanding to popular options like PayPal, Venmo, and Apple Pay. Questions? Call us at 516-847-1265.

Cancellations are accepted up to two weeks before the class date. Because seating is limited, we can't accommodate changes or cancellations inside that two-week window.

Yes. June 2026 is the transition year for the new NYS Science Learning Standards, so we offer reviews for both the New and Traditional versions of Biology, Earth Science, Chemistry, and Physics — check the class list for your exact exam.

Our one-day classes are an affordable flat price per class — currently $130 — updated each season. That covers the full-day review and the materials you take home. See the registration pages for current pricing and any early-bird dates.

Please pack your own lunch and a drink. On-campus food service is closed on weekends, and for safety students are not permitted to leave campus during the class day. We take a 30-minute lunch break midday.

Yes. In addition to Farmingdale State College's own campus police, we hire NYS-certified school security guards to supplement security throughout every class day.

Classes are held in Gleeson Hall — modern, air-conditioned classrooms with plenty of desk space and multimedia technology.
