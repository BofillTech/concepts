Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know about our one-day Regents and AP review courses at Farmingdale State College.

Each course is a single, focused day — about a six-hour session. Students review the major topics, work real practice questions, and learn test-taking strategy, all in one sitting.

Every course is taught by an experienced, New York State-certified public-school teacher. Long Island Regents Prep is teacher-owned and operated — it's the whole reason we exist.

On the campus of Farmingdale State College, in Gleeson Hall. It's a professional, distraction-free setting that's easy to reach from anywhere in Nassau and Suffolk County. See the map and directions.
