Teachers who wanted to do test prep right.

Long Island Regents Prep is teacher-owned and operated — built by educators who prepare students for these exams every single day.

Long Island Regents Prep was founded in 2009 by Michael Graziosi, Brad Seidman, and Dennis Urban — full-time social studies teachers in the Bellmore-Merrick Central High School District. After preparing thousands of their own students for New York State Regents and AP exams, they saw that students often needed focused review beyond what the school year allowed.

So they created a better option: one-day intensive Regents and AP review courses at Farmingdale State College. In each course, students learn the major topics, work through real practice questions, and pick up the test-taking strategies that only experienced, NYS-certified teachers can share — all in a focused session from 9:30 AM to 3:00 PM.

Built on word of mouth

Since 2009, more than 7,000 students from Suffolk County, Nassau County, and New York City have taken our courses — and we keep growing, largely on the strength of recommendations from families who've been through it. We're proud members of the Farmingdale Chamber of Commerce, and every year we donate thousands of dollars in free classes to local schools and organizations.

An affordable alternative

We started this company because so many test-prep programs are run by business people, not educators. We offer an affordable alternative to private tutoring that never compromises the quality of instruction — and every one of our instructors is a New York State-certified classroom teacher.

Recognized educators

Award-winning classroom teachers.

Our founders have earned Teacher of the Year honors, and Dr. Dennis Urban was named the 2018 New York State History Teacher of the Year.
