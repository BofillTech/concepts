Terms & Conditions.

Booking with Paradise Vacation Suites means agreeing to a few common-sense things. Here they are.

Last updated June 2026. By making a reservation with Paradise Vacation Suites you agree to these terms.

Booking & Payment

A reservation is confirmed when payment is received. A 50% deposit is due at the time of booking, and the remaining 50% is due the day before arrival. We accept major credit cards through our secure booking platform.

Rates are quoted in U.S. dollars and exclude applicable Florida state and local taxes, which are added at checkout.

Check-In & Check-Out

Check-in begins at 3:00 PM. Check-out is by 10:00 AM. Self check-in is available — your unique door code is sent ahead of arrival.

Early check-in or late check-out can occasionally be arranged depending on the cleaning schedule. Please contact us in advance to inquire.

Cancellation Policy

Cancellations made more than 30 days before arrival receive a full refund, less a $50 administrative fee.

Cancellations made 14–30 days before arrival receive a 50% refund.

Cancellations made within 14 days of arrival are non-refundable.

We strongly recommend travel insurance for unforeseen circumstances.

House Rules

  • The property is age-restricted to guests twenty-one and older.
  • Smoking is not permitted in any suite or anywhere on the property grounds.
  • Small dogs are welcome in Suite 2112, Suite 2120, and Suite 2124 only. A pet fee applies. Other suites are pet-free.
  • No parties or events. Maximum overnight occupancy is limited to the number of guests on the reservation.
  • Quiet hours are from 10:00 PM to 8:00 AM. We are a small, residential property.

Damages & Security

Guests are responsible for any damage caused during their stay. A damage waiver is included in your nightly rate covering accidental damage up to $500. Damage exceeding this amount will be billed to the credit card on file.

Liability

Paradise Vacation Suites is not responsible for loss, theft, or damage of personal belongings during your stay, or for any injury sustained on the property. The pool is unsupervised — swim at your own risk.

Force Majeure

Should circumstances beyond our reasonable control (hurricane, natural disaster, government order) prevent your stay, we will work with you on rescheduling or refund based on the specific situation.

Contact
