Common questions

Frequently asked questions.

Everything people usually ask before starting a build. Don’t see your question — just reach out.

  • We build large-scale models, props, sculptures, brand activations, advertising props, architectural elements, event and exhibit pieces, prototypes and retail installations. If it needs to be designed, engineered and fabricated at scale, it’s the kind of project we’re set up for.

  • Most of our work comes from creative agencies, producers, brands and retailers, but we take on individual commissions when the scope is a good fit for the shop. Reach out and describe the build — we’ll tell you honestly if it’s something we’re set up to do well.

  • It depends entirely on scale, materials, detail and timeline — a small prototype and a full-scale sculpture aren’t priced the same way. Tell us about the project on our quote request form and we’ll get back with next steps.

  • Timelines vary with scope and complexity. Share your target date on the quote form and we’ll let you know upfront whether it’s realistic for the build you have in mind.

  • Foam, resin, fiberglass, wood and metal are the core of most builds, finished with automotive-grade paint and coatings. Which material fits depends on whether the piece needs to travel, mount outdoors, carry weight, or hold fine detail — see our full capabilities for the complete list.

  • Both. Design, engineering and fabrication happen under one roof — CAD modeling and structural engineering turn a concept into buildable geometry before anything is cut, so nothing gets handed off to a separate shop mid-project.

  • Either works. We can develop a concept from references and sketches, or start directly from a 3D model or scan if you already have one. Our in-house 3D scanning capability also lets us capture an existing object as the starting point for a reproduction.

  • Yes. We build to travel — crated for shipping or delivered and installed on-site, depending on the project. Let us know the destination when you request a quote and we’ll factor it into the plan.

  • Not a hard minimum, but our shop is set up for large-scale and detail-heavy work, so very small or purely decorative orders may not be the best fit. If you’re not sure, ask — we’ll point you in the right direction either way.

  • Yes — that’s exactly what our 3D scanning and pattern & mold fabrication capabilities are for. We can capture an existing piece and reproduce it at the same scale or larger with the detail intact.

  • We’re based at 3000 Paseo Mercado, Ste 107, Oxnard, CA 93036 — see our contact page for a map and directions.

  • Fill out the quote request form with as much detail as you can — scale, timeline, references — or call us directly at 805-500-8059. We’ll follow up with next steps.
