Our story

A full-service fabrication studio in Oxnard.

Mondo Creations has been designing and building large-scale models, props, sculptures and custom installations out of Oxnard, California since 2016. We work alongside creative agencies, producers and brands who already have a strong vision — and need a fabrication partner who can bring it to life without friction.

From the first CAD model to final paint and delivery, your project stays clean, controlled and on schedule. Whether it’s a towering sculpture, a sleek retail installation, or a high-concept film set, we bring artistry, speed and precision to every build.

Design, engineering and fabrication all happen under one roof — CAD/CAM workstations, a large-format 5-axis CNC mill, casting and finishing all in-house, so nothing is handed off to a third shop mid-build.
