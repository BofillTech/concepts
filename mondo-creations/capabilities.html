In-house capabilities

One shop, end to end.

Design, engineering and fabrication under one roof — powerful workstations running robust CAD/CAM, modern machines, and skilled craftsmanship for the work no machine can do alone.

  • 01

    3D Scanning

    We capture existing objects, sets and environments as precise digital models — the starting point for reproductions, molds and structural fits.

  • 02

    3D Printing

    Rapid prototyping and production printing for parts, maquettes and detail work that would be slow to sculpt by hand.

  • 03

    Large-Scale 5-Axis CNC Machining

    Our large-format 5-axis mill cuts complex geometry directly from foam, wood or tooling board at full production scale.

  • 04

    Pattern & Mold Fabrication

    Master patterns and production molds built for repeatable castings, from a single hero prop to a full run of retail units.

  • 05

    Resin Casting

    Cast finishes in durable resin for parts that need to hold detail, weight and finish under repeated handling.

  • 06

    Vacuum Forming

    Thin-gauge plastic forming for shells, signage faces and lightweight production parts.

  • 07

    Fiberglass Layups

    Structural fiberglass over foam or tooling cores for pieces that need to travel, mount outdoors, or carry real load.

  • 08

    Foam Sculpture

    Hand- and CNC-shaped foam is the backbone of most large-scale builds — fast to rough in, light to install.

  • 09

    Woodwork & Metalwork

    In-house carpentry and welding for armatures, bases, crates and structural support.

  • 10

    Paint & Finishing

    Every piece leaves the shop fully finished — faux textures, coatings, weathering and color-matching to spec.

How a build comes together

From concept to install.

  1. 01

    Concept & Scope

    We start with your vision, references and constraints — budget, timeline, venue — and scope the build.

  2. 02

    Design & Engineering

    CAD modeling and structural engineering turn the concept into buildable geometry before anything is cut.

  3. 03

    Fabrication

    Work moves through our shop — CNC, casting, sculpting, welding — under one roof, one schedule.

  4. 04

    Delivery & Install

    Finished pieces are packed, delivered and installed on-site, or crated for pickup and shipping.
