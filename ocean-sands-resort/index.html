Beach Life at the Ocean Sands Resort

Location, Location, Location

Ocean Sands Resort is located on the boardwalk looking directly over the beach to the ocean. Shopping and fine dining are only steps away, with oceanfront shows, festivals, and free concerts throughout the season.

The newly refurbished Ocean Sands Resort features fully stocked kitchens and private balconies in every suite. Our PURE™ Allergy Friendly suites ensure exceptional air quality for your comfort.

Your Oceanfront Escape

Wake up to the sound of waves from your private balcony. Step directly onto the boardwalk and feel the sand between your toes. Ocean Sands Resort offers unmatched access to everything that makes Virginia Beach special.
