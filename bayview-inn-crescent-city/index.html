Area Attractions

What our local area has to offer. Crescent City and the North Coast are full of natural beauty and outdoor activities.

Crescent City Harbor

Walk to the harbor to see fishing boats, enjoy fresh seafood at local restaurants, and watch for sea lions and seals.

Battery Point Lighthouse

Historic lighthouse accessible at low tide. Tours available. One of California's most photographed lighthouses.

Redwood National Park

Towering ancient redwoods just minutes away. Hiking trails, scenic drives, and wildlife viewing opportunities.

More to Explore

  • Ocean World – Aquarium and marine life exhibits, just a short walk away
  • Del Norte Coast Redwoods State Park – Pristine forest and rugged coastline
  • Jedediah Smith Redwoods State Park – Some of the world's tallest trees
  • Smith River – Fishing, kayaking, and scenic beauty
  • Oregon Border – Quick drive to Brookings, Oregon and more coastal attractions
  • Downtown Crescent City – Shopping, dining, and local services within walking distance
