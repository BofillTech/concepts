About

No frills. No premium resort prices. Just a bed near everything.

Hotel Pigeon Forge sits directly on the Parkway, in the middle of Tennessee's busiest mountain-vacation strip — close enough to walk to some attractions, close enough to drive to the rest in minutes. It's a simple, clean place built for travelers who'd rather spend their money at Dollywood than on their hotel room.

Rooms are non-smoking with air conditioning, a flat-screen TV, mini refrigerator, microwave, and coffee maker. The front desk is staffed 24 hours, housekeeping comes daily, and there's an outdoor pool and BBQ area for whenever the group wants to slow down for an afternoon.
