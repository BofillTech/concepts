Our Story

The Demutis Family

Since 1941, the Demutis family has considered Cape May a second home — vacationing here, and eventually building a life around it. Their personal and professional ties to the community run deep: recognized in Pennsylvania as leading redevelopment and revitalization real estate developers, the third generation of Demutis builders now carries on the family's hospitality here at the Jetty.

Their work has been featured in the Philadelphia Inquirer, the Philadelphia Business Journal, the Cape May Star & Wave, and the Atlantic City Press — but at the Jetty, it's still a small, family-run motel at the end of the road, next to the beach.
