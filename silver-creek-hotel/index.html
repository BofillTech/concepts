Your Gateway to Sun Valley

Minutes from the world famous Sun Valley ski resort, fly fishing, camping, world class dining, hiking, music festivals, art galleries and more. Built in 2017, our hotel offers modern comforts with a commitment to sustainability through solar-heated water and pool.

Bring the fur babies too—our pet-friendly rooms feature sliding glass doors straight outside, making your four-legged companions feel right at home.

Comfortable Rooms & Suites

Five distinct room types designed for your comfort, each thoughtfully appointed with modern amenities.

Double Queen

First-floor rooms feature 10-ft ceilings and sliding doors; floors 2-3 have 9-ft ceilings and larger-than-average windows. Walk-in showers with solar-heated water and oversize showerheads.

  • Two queen beds
  • Walk-in shower
  • Sliding doors (1st floor)

Single King

Same features as our Double Queen, plus a dedicated work station—perfect for business travelers or extended stays in the valley.

  • King bed
  • Work station
  • Walk-in shower

Queen Garden Tub Suite

Relax in style with two queen beds, a Lazy Boy recliner, garden tub, and Keurig coffee maker. Located on floors 2-3.

  • Two queen beds
  • Garden tub
  • Lazy Boy recliner

King Garden Tub Suite

One king bed, Lazy Boy recliner, garden tub, Keurig, and kitchenette. Perfect for longer valley stays.

  • King bed
  • Kitchenette
  • Garden tub

Executive King Suite

Our premier third-floor suite features a private bedroom, living room with sleeper sofa, Keurig, soaker tub, walk-in shower, dual vanity, and kitchenette. Ideal for extended stays.

  • Private bedroom + living room
  • Kitchenette
  • Soaker tub & walk-in shower

Modern Amenities & Thoughtful Touches

Everything you need for a memorable stay in the Wood River Valley.

Solar-Heated Pool & Hot Tub

Swimmable year-round in the sun or snow. Our outdoor pool and hot tub offer stunning mountain views. Open daily 6:30 AM to 10:30 PM.

Complimentary Breakfast

Start your day with our free continental breakfast, served 6-9 AM. Upgrade to our $10 made-to-order omelette bar for a mountain-fueled morning.

Pet-Friendly Rooms

Bring the fur babies! Dogs are welcome in select rooms with sliding glass doors straight outside. Just $10 per pet per night.

Sustainable Practices

Tesla/EV charging stations, solar-heated pool, and solar-heated water in rooms. Ample free parking for all guests.

Guest Services

24-hour front desk, fitness center, meeting rooms, picnic area, elevator access, and food & beverage services. Plus a free ice cream from Bumble Bee Boba with every reservation!

Comfortable Common Spaces

Relax in our inviting lobby lounge with mountain art and comfortable seating—a perfect place to plan your next valley adventure.
