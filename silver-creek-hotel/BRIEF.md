## Room types (from site)
- **Double Queen:** first-floor rooms have 10-ft ceilings and sliding doors; floors 2-3 have 9-ft ceilings and larger-than-average windows. Walk-in showers with solar-heated water and oversize showerheads.
- **Single King:** same as above, plus a work station.
- **Queen Garden Tub Suite:** two queens, Lazy Boy recliner, garden tub, Keurig. Floors 2-3.
- **King Garden Tub Suite:** one king, Lazy Boy recliner, garden tub, Keurig, kitchenette. Floors 2-3.
- **Executive King Suite:** third floor. Private bedroom, living room with sleeper sofa, Keurig, soaker tub, walk-in shower, dual vanity, kitchenette. Pitched for extended stays.
In-room (room pages): HD TV and free cable, coffee maker, A/C, microwave, refrigerator, free Wi-Fi, USB charging ports.
