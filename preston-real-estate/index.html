The area

Between the sea and the mountains, Boston and Portland.

The New Hampshire Seacoast is at the heart of a region filled with amusements, history and scenery, with some of the best vacation values you will find anywhere, for any budget.

Tax-free shopping
There is no sales tax in New Hampshire, and stores all along the Seacoast carry fashions, jewelry and locally made gifts.
Great dining
Seafood caught fresh every day, plus everything from Italian to pizza, chowder, ice cream and fried dough.
Hampton & Seabrook beaches
Family beaches, the Hampton River and harbor, and summer events on Hampton Beach.
