Nearby Attractions

  • Downtown Sister Bay – 10-minute walk to restaurants, shops, and waterfront
  • Sister Bay Beach – Beautiful beach and marina nearby
  • Sports Complex – Across the street for disc golf, pickleball, ice skating, and sledding
  • Peninsula State Park – Hiking, biking, and scenic overlooks
  • Ellison Bay – Charming town to the north
  • Fish Creek – Historic village and shopping

Perfect for All Seasons

Spring cherry blossoms, summer beaches and festivals, fall colors and harvest, winter snow sports and holiday lights. Door County is beautiful year-round, and Pheasant Park Resort is open all seasons.
