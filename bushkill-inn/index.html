A mountain retreat in every season

Set on 160 serene, wooded acres in one of the most picturesque corners of the Pocono Mountains, Bushkill Inn & Conference Center welcomes both leisure and corporate travelers. The newly renovated resort pairs well-appointed guest rooms and suites in the main lodge with secluded cottages and cabins tucked throughout the property, each with oversized decks for scenic viewing.

Walk the winding nature trails, watch the abundant wildlife, or take in a night sky full of stars. In every season there's adventure close at hand — Bushkill Falls just across the way, mountain hiking, fishing and horseback riding, spectacular golf courses nearby, and swimming in the resort's indoor and outdoor pools.
