The trip checklist

Here’s what most groups grab before a few days on the water. Print it, screenshot it, or just read it to us over the phone.

In the cooler

  • Cases of beer & seltzer
  • Canned cocktails
  • Canned wine

For camp

  • Boxed wine
  • A bottle for the fire
  • Mixers
  • Cups

Leave glass at home

Most river outfitters and many campgrounds don’t allow glass. Cans, boxed wine and plastic bottles pack lighter and won’t break in a dry bag. Check your trip’s rules before you shop.

Trip questions

Can I call ahead with my list?

Yes. Call (928) 774-8502 with what you need and we’ll let you know what’s in stock, so you’re not shopping in a hurry on launch day.

Do you sell ice?

Yes, bagged ice is in the freezer out front.

Can I load up at the drive-thru?

For a few items, sure. For a big trip order, it’s easier to park and come in so we can help you carry it out.

Should I bring cans or bottles?

Many river outfitters and campgrounds don’t allow glass, so check your trip’s rules. Cans, boxed wine and plastic bottles are the safe bet.
