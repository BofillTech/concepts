Good to know

Can I order online?

Not anymore. We’ve stopped online ordering, so come by the store or the drive-thru. If you want to check whether something is in stock, give us a call.

How does the drive-thru work?

Pull up to the window, tell us what you’re after and we’ll bring it to you. Have your ID ready for every adult in the car.

Do I need ID?

Yes. You must be 21 or older with a valid, unexpired photo ID to buy alcohol, at the counter or the window.

Can you check if you have a bottle?

Sure. Call (928) 774-8502 and we’ll look.
