Our story

About the beaver

Beaver Street Liquor is a family-owned bottle shop on Beaver Street, just north of historic downtown Flagstaff. Drive through or walk in, and look up when you do. There’s a canoe on the ceiling.

A neighborhood store with a sense of humor

We’re the orange building with the drive-thru window and the buck-toothed beaver on the sign. Locals stop on the way home, and plenty of folks stop on the way out of town for the lake, the river or a cabin weekend.

Inside you’ll find a wall of cold coolers, rows of wooden wine racks, a spirits wall, and a forest mural under a real wooden canoe. It’s a liquor store, but we like to think it’s a fun one.
