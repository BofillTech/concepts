Brewed up the road

We keep plenty of Flagstaff and Arizona craft beer cold, from Mother Road on Route 66 to whatever the local breweries just canned. Arizona wines get their own tagged spot on the racks too.

Not sure what to try? Ask whoever’s at the counter. We drink this stuff.

Feeling lucky? Grab a mystery bag.

Brown paper bags, packed by us, folded shut. What’s inside is a surprise until you open it. They make a great gift for the friend who has everything, or a fun way to find your next favorite.
