Mystery bags

Brown paper bags, packed by us, folded shut. What’s inside stays a surprise until you open it. A great gift for the friend who has everything, or a fun way to find your next favorite.

How it works

  1. Grab

    Pick a bag off the shelf by the register. Bigger bags hold more surprises.

  2. Pay

    Bring it to the counter. We’ll check your ID like any other purchase.

  3. Open

    Rip it open at home, at the party or at the gift exchange. No peeking in the parking lot.

Good for…

  • White elephant
  • Birthdays
  • Housewarming
  • Just because

Mystery bag questions

Can I see what’s inside before I buy?

Nope, that’s the fun of it. Every bag is packed by us and folded shut.

Do I need to be 21?

Yes. Mystery bags contain alcohol, so we check ID just like any other purchase.

What sizes do you have?

We usually have a few sizes on the shelf by the register. Ask at the counter what’s in today.

Can I order one ahead of time?

Give us a call. If you need a few for a party or gift exchange, we’ll let you know what we can put together.

Want a few for a party?

Call the store and ask what sizes we have today.
