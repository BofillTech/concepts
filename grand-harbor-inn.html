Welcome

A Tuscan-villa retreat, set at the water's edge

Perched on one of Maine's most scenic harbors, the Grand Harbor Inn is a small and distinctive boutique hotel — the only luxury, Four-Diamond waterfront property Camden has to offer.

Ten guest suites, each with a gas fireplace and a view toward Camden Harbor and the islands of Penobscot Bay, take their warmth from the villas of Tuscany: exposed timber, wrought iron, and stone underfoot, paired with the quiet comforts of a boutique inn. Downtown Camden's shops and harborwalk are steps from the front door.

Camden, Maine

The harbor is forty feet away

Bay View Landing puts you within walking distance of downtown Camden's shops, galleries, and harborwalk, with Camden Hills State Park and Mount Battie a short drive beyond.

  • On the water Camden Harbor sits across the landing — unobstructed views from harborview suites, water's edge in under two minutes on foot.
  • Camden Hills Hiking trails and the Mount Battie overlook are minutes from the Inn.
  • Schooner Appledore II Book a daysail on Penobscot Bay past lighthouses and island preserves.
  • Rockport & Rockland A quieter harbor town five miles south, and the Farnsworth Art Museum fifteen miles beyond.
  • Acadia National Park About 45 minutes by car — Jordan Pond and Park Loop Road for a day trip.
