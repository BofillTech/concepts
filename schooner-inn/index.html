Your Beachfront Escape

The Schooner Inn has something to offer everyone. Located on the Oceanfront in Virginia Beach, our hotel features an ideal location on the south-end of the oceanfront with spectacular views of the Boardwalk, the extra wide beach out front, Rudee Inlet to the south and west, and the Atlantic Ocean stretching to the horizon.

This hotel is truly a unique destination, highlighted in the Mid Atlantic Living section of Southern Living Magazine. Whether you're watching sunrise over the Atlantic or strolling the boardwalk at sunset, you'll discover why guests return year after year.

With comfortable rooms, oceanfront amenities, and a prime location near Virginia Beach's best attractions—all with no resort fees—the Schooner Inn offers exceptional value for your beach getaway.
