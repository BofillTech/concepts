The Inn

Where luxury meets the laid-back lifestyle of the Forgotten Coast.

Nestled in the heart of historic Apalachicola — a picturesque fishing village established in 1831 — Coombs Inn & Suites comprises two historic Victorian mansions along with a couple of beautiful standalone buildings, an elegant retreat far from Florida's crowded tourist scenes.

For nearly three decades, guests have chosen Coombs for its tranquil, romantic and historic ambiance.

Each suite showcases European antiques, original oil paintings and oriental carpets atop gleaming hardwood floors, with private bathrooms, high-speed internet, in-room coffee and flat-screen cable TVs. Select suites add kitchenettes and private verandas — ideal for family vacations, bridal parties and corporate retreats.

Life at the Inn

Mornings on the veranda, afternoons by bicycle.

Morning

Coffee brews in your room while the village wakes. Double-paned windows keep the mansion hushed; the porch keeps the breeze.

Afternoon

Take the inn's complimentary bikes through boutique shops, museums, art galleries and waterfront cafés — and the world-renowned Apalachicola oysters.

Evening

Return through streets of live oaks to a room of European antiques and gleaming hardwood — the quiet luxury Coombs is known for.

Apalachicola

A fishing village since 1831.

Apalachicola sits on the pristine shores of what's affectionately known as Florida's Forgotten Coast — a scenic two-hour drive from Panama City Beach or Tallahassee, with easy access to the white sands of St. George Island, Cape San Blas, Mexico Beach and WindMark Beach.
