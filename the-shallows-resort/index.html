Welcome to The Shallows

Slow summer days, the way Door County does them.

A canopy of trees welcomes you to charming waterfront and wooded hideaways. Start the day with tennis, launch a kayak or canoe from our dock and try your luck at fishing.

In the afternoon, lounge by the pool or bike the rural roads of the peninsula. Then gather for a sunset over the bay, followed by a bonfire on the beach.
