Crystal Coast, NC

Twelve miles of coastline, and we're on the best part.

Emerald Isle sits at the western end of Bogue Bank, and the Islander is the only hotel on the island with the ocean at its doorstep. Fall asleep to the sound of the surf, walk straight from your room to the sand, and let the rest of the day sort itself out — whether that means the pool, the beach, or nothing at all.

Whether you're here for a long weekend, a family reunion, a conference, or a wedding by the water, the resort has the room type — and the room — to match.

Private Beach Access

As Emerald Isle's only oceanfront hotel, the Islander has the best spot on the island for a beach getaway — just steps from the ocean, with complimentary beach chairs while they last.

Oceanfront Pool & Pool Bar

A seasonal poolside bar and an expansive oceanfront pool with cabana — the easy alternative when you want the ocean view without the sand. (Coolers and outside beverages stay at the room.)

Bikes & Beach Chairs, On Us

Complimentary bike rentals and beach chairs are ready at the front desk — ride Emerald Isle's 11-mile bike path, or set up on the sand for the day. Need a board or a kayak? Hot Wax Surf Shop is close by.

Rest Easy in the Hammocks

Ocean-side hammocks made for sunrise, sunset, or the middle of the afternoon — plus a 24-hour business center and marketplace on-site when the day still needs a little work in it.

Ballroom & Weddings

4,000 square feet of flexible space by the sea.

The newly renovated grand ballroom comes with a full-service bar, cocktail service, a stage, and banquet tables — built for weddings, holiday parties, conferences, banquets, and everything in between. An outdoor patio with a fire pit and an oceanfront event lawn extend the party outside.

All-inclusive wedding packages are available, and the Islander's team handles the coordination — including, guests say, keeping a wedding on track through the edge of a hurricane watch.
