Moments by the lake

Mornings start on your balcony. The rest of the day follows the shoreline.

Terrace Bay Hotel sits right at the water's edge on Little Bay de Noc. Wake to sunrise over the bay, ride the waterside trail on a complimentary e-bike, paddle out before lunch, then settle in at Freshwater Tavern as the light goes gold.

Modern rooms, attentive personal service, and the Upper Peninsula just outside: it's an easy place to slow down.

Gather around something delicious

Freshwater Tavern

Scratch-made modern American cooking with panoramic views of Lake Michigan: hand-cut steaks, regional harvests, fresh seafood, craft cocktails, and a rotating list of Upper Peninsula beers.
