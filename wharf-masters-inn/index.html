Welcome to Wharf Master's Inn

Perched on the bluff overlooking Point Arena Cove with breathtaking views of the Pacific Ocean, Wharf Master's Inn is a historic landmark offering the perfect coastal retreat. Our oceanfront boutique hotel combines Victorian elegance with modern comfort.

Enjoy cozy eco-friendly fireplaces, comfy pillow-top beds, oversize jetted spa tubs, and private balconies with gorgeous views of the ocean, hillsides, or our tranquil courtyard garden. Every detail is designed for your relaxation and renewal.

Located near the historic Point Arena Lighthouse with a private trailhead to Stornetta Public Lands, our inn offers the perfect blend of peaceful seclusion and California coastal adventure.
