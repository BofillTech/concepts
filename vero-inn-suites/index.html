Welcome

Comfort, without the coastal markup.

Vero Beach Inn & Suites sits a few minutes inland from the shoreline — close enough to catch the ocean breeze, far enough to skip beachfront pricing. Just off I-95 at Exit 147, the hotel puts the Jackie Robinson Training Complex, the Vero Beach Outlets, and a wide stretch of Treasure Coast restaurants and shops within easy reach.

Every stay includes free parking, free high-speed fiber Wi-Fi, and a room that's been freshly updated with new furniture and a new mattress — the basics done well, at a rate that leaves room for the rest of the trip.

Resort Life

Small hotel, resort-size perks.

Pool & tiki huts

A resort-style outdoor pool wrapped in tropical landscaping, shaded loungers, and tiki huts — a spot to sit still for a while before the next stop on the trip.

Hot breakfast, every morning

A free hot breakfast buffet with a waffle bar, eggs, bacon, sausage, and potatoes, plus fresh fruit, cereal, and pastries — served daily, no extra charge.

Pet-friendly, genuinely

Dogs and cats are welcome, so the whole family can travel together — no separate pet hotel, no leaving anyone behind.
