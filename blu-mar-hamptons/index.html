Where History Meets Hamptons Luxury

Originally built in 1648, Harpoon House offers a timeless escape with refined design and coastal charm

Nestled in the heart of Southampton, Harpoon House stands among the village's oldest and most iconic properties. Our boutique hotel blends historic charm with modern coastal sophistication, offering nine beautifully appointed guest rooms, world-class Mediterranean dining at Blu Mar, and the perfect setting to experience the best of the Hamptons.

Guest Rooms & Suites

Jr. King Guestroom

Our Jr. King rooms feature luxurious beds with fine linens and beautifully appointed décor. Each room includes a coffee machine, kettle for tea, writing desk, flat-screen TV, plush sofa, robes, and slippers — all designed for comfort and relaxation.

Experience Harpoon House

Blu Mar Mediterranean Seafood

Celebrated Chef Rocco DiSpirito brings his award-winning culinary expertise to Southampton with the Blu Mar Pop-Up, an exclusive seasonal dining experience located within Harpoon House. This Mediterranean-inspired restaurant showcases the finest local seafood, farm-fresh ingredients, and vibrant coastal flavors.

Open May through October, the Blu Mar Pop-Up offers a sophisticated yet relaxed dining atmosphere, perfect for long summer evenings and unforgettable gatherings.

Hours: Monday-Thursday 5pm-10pm, Friday 5pm-11pm, Saturday 12pm-11pm, Sunday 12pm-10pm
