Welcome to Avondale by the Sea

Located directly across from the beach on Cape May's historic promenade, Avondale by the Sea offers immaculate beachfront accommodations with warm hospitality. Our upper-floor rooms feature private balconies overlooking the spectacular Atlantic Ocean.

Walk to Washington Street Mall, Convention Hall, and Cape May's finest restaurants and shops. Enjoy complimentary coffee, tea, bagels, and beach passes with your stay. Our outdoor pool with separate kiddie pool is perfect for families.

We take pride in our attentive service and spotless housekeeping. Many of our guests return year after year to experience Cape May's charm from our perfect beachfront location.
