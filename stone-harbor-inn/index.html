Pool first, then the beach

Start the day in the heated pool, or ease into it in the spa. The sun deck is there for the hours in between.

When you're ready for the ocean, the beach tags are on us. Leave a $40 cash deposit for each guest 12 and up, and bring them back at checkout.

Back in the room, there are all-new Serta 16" mattresses and fresh linens, air conditioning for July and heat for the cool nights of spring and fall.

Good to know before you book

Check-in and check-out
Check in from 4 PM, check out by 11 AM.
Minimum stay
Rates are quoted for a four-day minimum. Weekends, holidays and special events carry a higher rate and minimum.
Family groups
Guests must be 25 or older, and rentals are made to family groups only.
Smoke-free, no pets
No smoking in rooms or anywhere on the property. Sorry, no pets.
Cancellations
Cancel 30 or more days before arrival for a full refund less a $50 service fee.
Parking
Parking is subject to availability and not guaranteed.
