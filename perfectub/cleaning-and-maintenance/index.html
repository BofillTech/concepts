How To Clean an Acrylic Bathtub

If you want to know how to clean an acrylic bathtub effectively, a simple rule is to protect the surface while constantly removing the buildup. Acrylic is actually easier to maintain compared to older bathtub materials, but it still needs the right kind of care.

Unlike other resources you might find on how to deep clean a bathtub, never use harsh scrubbing, steel wool, powdered cleaners, bleach, ammonia, or acetone, as these will dull the finish of acrylic bathtubs over time.

The better approach is gentle and regular. Let’s discuss these practical steps.

Routine Cleaning

For best results, rinse the bathtub after use to remove soap residue and body oils.

For regular cleaning:

  • Wet the bathtub surface with warm water.
  • Apply a mild, non-abrasive cleaner that is suitable for acrylic surfaces.
  • Clean using a soft cloth, microfiber cloth, or non-abrasive sponge.
  • Rinse thoroughly with clean water.
  • Wipe dry with a soft cloth to help prevent water spots and mineral buildup.

Use Gentle Products

A mild cleaner is usually the better choice for acrylic. Even if you know how to clean an acrylic bathtub, avoid aggressive chemicals, excessive scrubbing, steel wool, powdered cleaners, bleach, ammonia, or acetone. These products will dull the finish of acrylic bathtubs over time.

  • Use warm water, dish soap, a soft sponge, and acrylic-safe cleaning products.
  • Use cleaners specifically labeled as safe for acrylic tubs and showers.
  • For tougher spots, household options like baking soda or diluted vinegar may help.
  • Always rinse the surface after using any cleaning products as best as you can.
  • Avoid abrasive pads and rough scrubbing tools to avoid scratches.

For Soap Scum or Mineral Buildup

  • For light soap scum or mineral deposits, apply a solution of equal parts white vinegar and warm water using a soft cloth or non-abrasive sponge.
  • Allow the solution to remain on the surface briefly, gently wipe the area, and rinse thoroughly with clean water.
  • Do not allow cleaning products to dry on the bathtub surface.

Protecting Your Perfectub®

  • Do not place hot objects directly on the acrylic surface.
  • Avoid dropping heavy or sharp objects into the bathtub.
  • Rinse away shampoo, soap, bath oils, hair coloring products, and other personal-care products after use rather than allowing residue to remain on the surface for extended periods.
  • Regular gentle cleaning is preferable to occasional aggressive cleaning.
