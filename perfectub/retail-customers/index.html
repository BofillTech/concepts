For Retail Customers

Will you be buying a new bathtub for your upcoming bathroom remodel? Do you need a deluxe bathtub for its potential impact on your home’s resale value? If so, choose the Perfectub, a high-quality bathtub designed by a builder. It features a host of patented enhancements that elevate the bather’s safety, comfort, and relaxation. If you’ve been longing for a rejuvenating deep soak, like to take ice-baths, or want a shower tub combo with minimal risk of slippage, opt for the Perfectub.

Order your new bathtub directly from us online or visit a showroom near you

The Benefits to Expect With the Perfectub

Here are the advantages the tub offers:

It’s More Comfortable

There are many reasons why the Perfectub is the best bathtub. However, the top requirement for most homeowners buying a new bathtub is to have an improved bathing experience, and the Perfectub offers precisely that.

Our tub features several patented improvements that make it more functional, safer, and comfortable than standard bathtubs. For example, it’s deeper and has about 30% more floor space, allowing users to be fully submerged, with both elbows in, for a truly relaxing and rejuvenating bath. Even larger bathers can fit comfortably. The tub is made of acrylic, good for heat retention and easy to clean.

It’ll Improve Your Home’s Resale Value

Most buyers looking to purchase a home in America hope to find one that has a bathtub. For some, not having one can be a deal breaker because they don’t want to deal with the costs of a bathroom remodel to install a bathtub. In particular, families with children and pets often specify their need for a bathtub to make bathing and showering easier. Consequently, a home with a bathtub is likely to sell faster. You can sweeten the deal by buying and installing a new top-quality bathtub with unique functionalities.

It Accommodates Your Family as Needs Change

The Perfectub is designed for the entire family. The 3” walls make it easy to bathe small children or wriggling pets. This makes it easier to reach them, and easier to lift them out when finished. The patented concave inner wall keeps splashes inside the tub, rather than on the bathroom floor. That means less risk of slippage. And, for older bathers, the wider, flat floor of the Perfectub allows a shower chair to sit level, without the wobbling that traditional bathtubs cause.

We Offer Excellent Customer Service

From the moment you first contact us through the form, we strive to provide helpful responses, support, and assistance. Our customer service representative will contact you to answer any questions, provide more information about the Perfectub, guide you to find a showroom near you, or even assist you in placing your bathtub order directly through us. Even after the purchase, we follow up to ensure that the bathtub has been shipped, that it’s in good condition, and has been installed correctly.
