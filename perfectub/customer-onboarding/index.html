New Customer Onboarding

Wholesale and dealer account setup for Perfectub by Brak Tub. Contact Rosemarie Torla, Office Manager — sales@braktub.com · (516) 217-4599, option 3.

Use this form to open a new wholesale or dealer account. It matches Rosemarie’s current Brak Application, including the 48-hour shipping-damage clause. After we receive your details, Rosemarie will confirm your account and payment setup. You can also download the fillable PDF application.
