How to Keep a Bathtub Looking New

By Jerry GouldMay 6, 2026

Key Takeaways:

  • Acrylic bathtubs have an easy-clean surface that makes them more resistant to soap scum, stains, mold, and mildew.
  • Knowing how to clean your acrylic bathtub properly means using gentle, non-abrasive cleaners, soft cloths, mild cleaners, and rinsing after use.
  • Harsh chemicals, steel wool, and abrasive tools damage the acrylic finish and cause dullness over time.
  • These bathtub cleaning tips will keep your tub looking new throughout its life.

With daily use, soap scum, hard water stains, bath products’ residue, and mold can quickly dull the appearance of your bathtub. But, the good news? With the right bathtub cleaning tips, keeping your tub looking fresh and new doesn’t require hours of scrubbing or harsh chemicals, especially with an easy-clean acrylic bathtub like the Perfectub.

We’ve put together some practical, effective cleaning tips to help you keep your bathtub spotless. Read on.

Bathtub Cleaning Tips: The Best Way to Clean an Acrylic Bathtub

Do the following to keep your bathtub looking new:

1. Create a Consistent Cleaning Schedule

The secret to a clean, spotless bathtub is consistent, regular cleaning. Don’t wait for stains from soap scum, body oils, or product residues to build up. After every bath, rinse your tub with clean water, then wipe it dry to remove water droplets and any remaining residue. Take more time wiping down areas that are more prone to residues, such as around the drain hole, tub walls, and fixtures. Once a week, do more cleaning with a non-abrasive cleaner.

The need for daily touchups is often overlooked, but this bathtub cleaning tip goes a long way in preventing the accumulation of stubborn grime in the tub and eventual discoloration.

2. Tackle Soap Scum and Stains as Soon as They Form

As you learn how to clean acrylic bathtubs, keep in mind that harsh chemicals, such as acetone, bleach, and ammonia, can damage the bathtub surface. They erode the protective surface and may cause discoloration. Therefore, stick to gentle cleaners made from natural ingredients, such as baking soda, vinegar, hydrogen peroxide, or essential oils.

Essentially, knowing the best way to clean an acrylic bathtub means choosing bathtub cleaning tips and products that clean effectively without sacrificing safety.

3. Prevent Damage Before it Starts

As you master how to properly clean acrylic bathtubs, be careful to also avoid damaging the tub. Do not place razors, metal containers, and sharp objects directly on the tub’s surface. Also, use a bath mat that’s specifically designed for acrylic tubs to reduce scratches.

Another often missed tip for cleaning your bathtub is to limit the use of heavily dyed products and bath bombs. Although they’re fun, they stain acrylic surfaces, particularly if the residue isn’t cleaned off promptly.

4. Ventilation is Critical

Proper ventilation is the last puzzle piece in your quest to keep your bathroom clean and your bathtub looking new. Even after mastering the best ways to clean an acrylic bathtub, without adequate ventilation, moisture trapped in the bathroom can encourage mold and mildew growth, especially along the caulking and in the drains.

Therefore, every time you take a bath, open a window or turn on the exhaust fan for at least 15 minutes to let the moisture out.
