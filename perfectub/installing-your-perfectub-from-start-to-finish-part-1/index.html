Installing Your Perfectub, From Start to Finish Part I

By Jerry GouldMay 28, 2026

The Rundown

  • Verify dimensions on-site before committing.
  • Check for Perfectub-specific pipe clearance in the tub envelope.
  • Confirm what’s included: drain + overflow tie-in parts.
  • Rough-in to the Perfectub system, not a generic tub kit
  • Dry-fit early to confirm the drain path and connections.
  • Stage carefully: use handles, keep corner guards, protect the base.
  • Note the slot overflow and plan valve/spout placement around it.

All builds always reach a point where the next step just isn’t fun, but it’s required for the project to proceed. That’s the exact situation the Stud Pack crew found themselves in during one of their builds.

In their YouTube video, they had been pushing through MEP work and had gotten their rough plumbing in, but couldn’t pass their inspection because important fixture decisions hadn’t been finalized yet.

The inspector wanted to see the tub valves selected and installed, and also wanted the tub set in the Jack-and-Jill bathroom. The Stud Pack team figured they’d just run down the street and grab a basic 30 x 60 tub and be done with it. We knew they could do better than that, though!

Why Basic-tub When You Can Perfectub?

Our team at Brak Tub reached out to Stud Pack and let them know about our product, the Perfectub. You’re welcome, Stud Pack!

Once we mentioned that this tub was designed by the son of one of the design leaders for the Apollo lunar landing module, they got very interested in what we could do with bathtub designs.

Beyond its impressive design origins, the Perfectub was also practical for this build because:

  • It fits in a standard 60 x 30 alcove.
  • It is shower-chair friendly.
  • It has a built-in solid bottom that requires fewer installation materials compared to a traditional bathtub.
  • Its interior is noticeably larger than traditional bathtubs due to our unique design.

Needless to say, the team at Stud Pack was sold!

The Installation Requirements and Early Must-Checks

Before you install a Perfectub, you need to lock in a few non-negotiables to avoid headaches. Let’s look at what Stud Pack was working through as a guide for your own build.

Confirm Your Dimensions

Plan for a standard 30 x 60 alcove, then verify it on-site before you commit. Stud Pack does this by measuring the Perfectub.

They also catch a key Perfectub-specific requirement where a pipe they assumed would hide behind a traditional tub’s sloped wall now sits in the usable tub envelope.

It barely clears (about an inch), so they pad the back wall out slightly instead of moving plumbing. If you’re still framing, keep pipes out of the tub space so the Perfectub drops in cleanly.

Confirm What Comes Included

The Stud Pack team was surprised to find that the Perfectub includes a drain assembly. This can change what you need to source and how you plan the rough-in.

They also notice that the drain has multiple connection points, with the drain body itself having two inlets (one for the tub drain and the other for the overflow connection).

This design choice helps them during alignment since the overflow uses a flexible connection, which means you won’t be stuck if things are slightly off.

Therefore, plan your rough-in around the Perfectub’s drain-and-overflow system, not a generic tub kit. This is because you’ll need to confirm that you have the clearance and positioning to connect both lines cleanly.

Dry-fit the tub and confirm the drain path early, so you don’t force fittings or reopen the wall at the set time.

Handling and Staging the Perfectub

Because the Perfectub is an acrylic finish piece, you want it protected from the first lift to avoid unnecessary scratches.

The Perfectub has molded carry handles (two on each side), heavy-duty corner protection, and the dotted cardboard cutouts that are meant to be used as a bottom protector while you move it around.

Using these handling and protection features keeps the tub install-ready.

Check the Overflow Style and Plan Your Valve Decisions Around It

With the Perfectub, don’t wait until the tub is in the alcove to think about overflow and fill height.

In the video, the Stud Pack team spots the slot-style overflow right away, and they flag it as part of the system they’ll need to tie in because it affects how the drain/overflow assembly comes together and how the tub will ultimately fill.

Therefore, confirm your tub valve and spout plan with the Perfectub’s overflow design in mind before you lock in the rough plumbing for inspection. That way, you’re not rethinking valve placement or fill behavior after the tub is already staged for install.

Check Your Drain Hand

Confirm that the drain hand matches your layout. As the Stud Pack points out, tubs come in two types: left vs right hand drains.

If you’ve got a layout with the rough-ins for this on the left, then make sure you order a tub with the left-hand configuration. This is important since it will determine whether the tub will even line up with your existing plumbing and alcove plan.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the Perfectub come in left-hand and right-hand drain options?

Yes. Choose the drain hand that matches your rough-in so the tub lines up without rework.

Does the Perfectub include a drain assembly?

Yes, it does: not because of its unique design but also to reduce the extra sourcing and help you plan your rough-in more accurately.
