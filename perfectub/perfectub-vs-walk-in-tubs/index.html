The Perfectub vs. Walk-in Tubs

People often spend a lot of time weighing the pros and cons of walk-in tubs before making a purchase, and they should! However, they might not bother to find out what the disadvantages of a walk-in tub really are.

On paper, these tubs seem like a good idea. You’d think that a door entry, or a bench to sit on, would seem like clever and considerate accommodations. But if you talk to other homeowners who have them, you might start hearing about problems that they didn’t expect.

That’s why it’s better to look at the full picture, not just the brochure promises, before you commit to one, especially when there is a wide selection of bathtub options you can consider.

To be fair, many features do work. But like anything, the advertised benefits don’t tell the whole story. The other half of the walk-in tub pros and cons is a list of frustrations that show up once the tub is in daily use.

Walk-In Tub Frustrations You Should Know

So, what are the disadvantages of a walk-in tub? There are more than most people realize:

  • Draining takes time. You can’t open the door until all the water has drained out, which leaves you sitting in cooling, dirty water.
  • Filling isn’t quick either. Because the walls are higher, you end up waiting for the tub to fill before you can even start getting clean.
  • A walk-in tub is a semi-permanent commitment that can limit the use of a bathtub long-term. While it may suit some family members, it will prevent others from taking showers easily and eliminate the ability to bathe pets or children. It also eliminates the ability to store a large volume of water for in during an emergency.
  • The inside feels smaller than it looks. Between the door and the bench, the actual bathing area is tight compared with wide bathtubs.
  • Installation is rarely simple: These tubs can need extra plumbing, structural reinforcement, and don’t fit in all bathrooms.
  • Walk-in tubs cost more than you’d think. Both the unit and the labor push the price higher than most alcove tubs.

Why These Cons Matter

The problem isn’t just convenience, it’s how these details creep into everyday life.

Compared with wide bathtubs that give you more room and fewer complications, the trade-offs become clearer. Taking some time to know what the advantages and disadvantages of walk-in tubs are helps you see what living with one actually looks like.
