Perfectub Features and Specifications

Take a look at our tub’s specifications to have a better understanding of its features and dimensions:

  • The Perfectub is 60 inches long and is available in 30” or 32” width, each in left-hand (LH) or right-hand (RH) drain.
  • The overall height of the 30″ tub is 17.33″, and the overall height of the 32″ tub is 19.33″ (per Brak Tub’s cut sheets)
  • It’s an alcove bathtub made from acrylic, a lightweight, durable material that is easy to clean and keeps hot baths insulated.
  • Perfectub comes with a linear overflow, in matching tub color
  • On a smooth and level subfloor, the Perfectub is self-supporting due to its built-in solid bottom, which usually means less prep during installation. The use of a wet mortar bed or foam bedding is not required, but highly recommended when the floor is uneven or older, in the case of most remodeling jobs.
  • Additionally, the reinforced back edge seamlessly integrates with the tile walls. To install, refer to the guide.
