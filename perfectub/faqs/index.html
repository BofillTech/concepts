Frequently Asked Questions

General Questions

What makes Perfectub different from a traditional alcove bathtub?

Perfectub combines a wide, flat bathing floor, a high-positioned linear overflow, a patented interior toe-kick and a streamlined rear rim in a standard 60-inch alcove format.

What is the toe-kick or undercut?

They are two names for the same feature: a recessed concave contour on the inside of the front apron wall. It gives the bather room to position a foot closer to the outer apron when stepping out.

Does the toe-kick help contain splashes?

The shape of the interior toe-kick is designed to help redirect splashing water back toward the bathtub. It does not eliminate the need for a properly installed shower curtain or enclosure.

Does Perfectub have 30% more floor space?

No. The proposed comparison is approximately 30% greater overall bathing volume, not 30% more floor area. That bathing-volume comparison is awaiting final verification before it is used as a published performance claim.

How does the linear overflow provide a deeper soak?

The slim linear overflow is positioned close to the upper rim, allowing a higher fill level than many conventional round overflow arrangements. The final depth measurement will be provided in the approved specifications.

What sizes are available?

Perfectub is offered in standard 60 × 30 and 60 × 32 alcove sizes. Review the model cut sheets for complete dimensions.

Do I need to install Perfectub in a wet mortar bed?

On a properly level subfloor, the solid underside is designed to allow installation without a separately applied wet mortar bed, when installed in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions.

What is the solid underside?

Perfectub's underside has an acrylic envelope filled with support material to form a solid base rather than relying on conventional support feet. This adds weight and provides a continuous support structure.

What comes with the bathtub?

Each Perfectub includes a six-piece installation kit intended to provide the components for a standard installation. Confirm the current kit inventory against the official packing list.

Is Perfectub ADA compliant?

The 32-inch model was designed with an accessibility-related rim-height consideration. Whether an installation meets ADA requirements depends on the applicable standard, confirmed product dimensions and the entire bathroom design.

What should I do when the bathtub is delivered?

Inspect the packaging and bathtub before signing the Proof of Delivery, and confirm that the six-piece installation kit is included. Record visible damage and any missing pallet on the delivery receipt. See the Delivery, Repair & Replacement Policy for detailed instructions.

What if I discover concealed damage?

Stop installation and contact your selling showroom or distributor, or Brak Tub if purchased directly, immediately. Report concealed damage within 24 hours of discovery under the current consumer policy.
