One Small Step for Mankind – Redesigned for Everyday Living

By Jerry GouldApril 21, 2026

One Small Step—Redesigned for Everyday Living

What does The Perfectub have in common with the Apollo Lunar Module?

More than you might expect.

The structural engineer behind the Lunar Module helped design one of the most iconic “small steps” in history. When Neil Armstrong descended the ladder in 1969, every detail—from stability to step height—was engineered for safety and precision.

That same focus on designing safer steps is just as relevant today—only now, it applies to something far more common: the everyday step into a bathtub.

The Overlooked Problem with Alcove Bathtubs

Traditional alcove bathtubs have remained largely unchanged for decades. While they are standard in residential and multi-family construction, their design presents a consistent challenge:

  • A high step-over height
  • A narrow, curved standing surface
  • Limited stability when entering or exiting

For many users, this is inconvenient. For others—especially in aging-in-place environments, senior housing, and multi-family developments —it becomes a real safety concern.

Search interest in terms like alcove bathtub safety, safe step-in bathtub, and ADA-friendly tubs continues to grow as awareness increases.

A Smarter Approach to Bathtub Safety

The Perfectub was designed to address this exact issue—without requiring a complete redesign of the bathroom.

Instead of changing everything, it improves the most important moment: the step into and out of the tub.

Key Design Improvements:

  • Lower step-over height Makes entry and exit easier and safer
  • Wider, flatter standing area Provides greater stability compared to traditional curved tub floors

These patented innovations transform a familiar fixture into a safer alcove bathtub solution.

A Legacy of Safer Design

The connection to the Lunar Module is more than symbolic.

It reflects a mindset—one that recognizes how small design details can have a major impact on safety.

That same thinking carried forward into residential construction. After years of working with homeowners, the inventor of The Perfectub saw a consistent issue: the step into a traditional bathtub was unnecessarily difficult and often unsafe.

The solution was simple in concept, but meaningful in execution: reduce the step and improve the surface where people stand.

Ideal for Aging in Place, Multi-Family, and Senior Living

While many projects look for ADA-friendly tubs, full ADA compliance isn’t always required—or practical.

The Perfectub offers a practical, cost-effective alternative that enhances safety in:

  • Multi-family housing developments
  • Senior and assisted living facilities
  • Aging-in-place home designs
  • Renovations using standard alcove layouts

It integrates easily into existing design plans while improving usability for a wide range of users.

Why a Small Step Makes a Big Difference

In bathroom design, small improvements can lead to meaningful outcomes.

By rethinking the step into the tub, you can:

  • Reduce the risk of slips and falls
  • Improve comfort and confidence for users
  • Extend the functional lifespan of the bathroom

It’s a subtle change—but one that makes a measurable difference every day.
