Alcove Tub Installation: Installing a Perfectub

If you’ve done alcove tub installations before, you know how fussy it can be. Leveling everything, making sure it drains right, mixing Structo-Lite just to set the base. The Perfectub cuts some of that out.

We designed this tub to make life easier on both ends, whether you’re installing it or using it. It’s a standard 60” x 30” or 60” x 32” alcove tub, but it’s got features most tubs don’t offer, and that makes it anything but standard!

Step One: Prep Your Space

Start with a flat, level subfloor. The tub has a solid bottom, so on a smooth, level subfloor it usually needs less prep than laying it over mortar. The use of Structo-Lite is not required, but highly recommended when the floor is uneven or older, in the case of most remodeling jobs.

Check your framing. Studs should be square and straight. While you don’t need to overbuild anything, sloppy framing will show up later in tile.

Pick your drain: we make this tub in both 60 x 30 and 60 x 32 models, with a linear drain and pop-up toe-touch drain. Make sure your rough-in matches. You don’t want to wrestle with offsets later.

Step Two: Setting the Tub

This part’s refreshingly simpler than other alcove tub installations. Drop the Perfectub into place. Structo-Lite is recommended under the tub when the floor is uneven or older. The tub’s base is solid; no feet. It’s got full support across the bottom, not just pads or legs. Once it’s in the alcove, check for level again and adjust if needed.

Secure the flanges to the studs. Use screws and washers, nothing fancy. The back flange is thin and flat, and fits flush to the wall so you don’t have to fight with tile lips or awkward overhangs. It’s one of our patented features, and it makes the finishing look cleaner and sleeker.

Step Three: Hookups and Finish

This is the last part of this alcove tub installation. Drain hookups are standard, whether you’re working with a left drain or a right drain. Attach the overflow and main drain like you would any acrylic tub. We include detailed instructions, but most plumbers won’t need them.

Once it’s hooked up, do a water test. Fill it, check for leaks, and test the overflow. If everything holds, you’re good to go.

Why It’s Easier

Brak Tub made this tub because we were tired of all the little frustrations that come with most alcove tub installations. Extra setting prep, shaky bottoms, clunky tile lips, it adds up. Perfectub skips the worst of it. No weird framing tricks, fewer delays waiting for mud setting, and the thing just fits where a normal tub should.

And for homeowners: you end up with a tub that’s bigger inside, easier to step into, and a whole lot nicer to use.
