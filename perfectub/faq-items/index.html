FAQs

How is the Perfectub different from a standard bathtub?

The Perfectub is designed to provide more usable space without requiring a larger footprint. Its patented geometry creates a wider, flatter floor while maintaining a standard alcove size. The result is a tub that feels more spacious and comfortable without requiring major changes to the bathroom layout.

Where can I see the Perfectub in person?

You can find the Perfectub in our partner showrooms across the Northeast, Mid-Atlantic, and Midwest. We also have some other locations in the Midwest and Texas. If you’re not sure where to find one in your area, reach out to us, and a founding member will point you to the closest display. If you’re looking to buy Perfectubs for multiple projects and you need to discuss distributor options, just call us. You’ll always be speaking with an experienced founding member of our team.

Is acrylic the best bathtub material?

For most homes and light-commercial installs, high-quality acrylic hits the sweet spot. This is because it retains heat well for longer soaks, resists everyday scuffs, and has a smooth, non-porous finish that’s easy to clean. It’s also lighter than many alternatives, which simplifies handling and installation. When it comes to the Perfectub, its acrylic construction is what makes it comfortable, durable, aesthetically pleasing, and easy to maintain.

Can the Perfectub be used as a shower?

Yes. The Perfectub is designed to work as both a bathtub and a shower, making it a practical choice for bathrooms that need to serve multiple purposes. Its wider floor and stable step-over also create a more comfortable showering experience than many standard tubs.

Does the Perfectub fit a standard alcove?

Yes. The Perfectub is designed to fit standard alcove spaces, making it possible to upgrade the bathing experience without completely reworking the bathroom layout. Its unique geometry creates more usable interior space within that familiar footprint.

Is the Perfectub easy to clean?

Yes. The smooth acrylic surface is non-porous and easy to wipe clean as part of your normal bathroom routine. The integrated design also minimizes areas where dirt and buildup can collect.

Is the Perfectub comfortable for showering?

The Perfectub’s wider, flatter floor provides more room to move while showering. Combined with its stable step-over, the design creates a more comfortable and secure-feeling showering experience.

How does the Perfectub make a bathtub feel more spacious?

The Perfectub uses a combination of tighter-radius corners and an undercut design to create more usable floor space. Instead of simply making the tub longer or wider, the geometry makes better use of the space already available.

Can I use a shower chair with the Perfectub?

The Perfectub’s wider, flatter floor provides additional room for a shower chair while maintaining a standard alcove footprint. This gives homeowners more flexibility when their bathing needs change over time.

Is the Perfectub suitable for remodeling projects?

Yes. Because the Perfectub is designed around a standard alcove footprint, it can be a practical option for bathtub replacement projects without requiring a complete bathroom remodel. Its design allows homeowners to improve comfort and usability while keeping the existing layout.

How deep is the Perfectub?

The Perfectub is designed to provide a deeper soaking experience while maintaining a standard alcove footprint. What is the advantage of the Perfectub’s linear drain?

A linear overflow drain reliably moves excess water out of the tub, while also allowing a larger volume of water to be retained for an enjoyable soak. Reliable drainage lets you keep a hot tap running slowly to keep a hot bath hot, without overflowing. It also keeps your bathing area dry and more stable underfoot. Paired with Perfectub’s clean, integrated lines, the result is a sleeker look and a bathing surface that feels more secure and comfortable day-to-day.

Does the undercut make a big difference?

Absolutely. The undercut allows a smaller, more stable step when getting into or out of the tub. It also widens the floor space and, with the tighter radius corners, gives you a wider, flatter floor. That means that you get a more spacious interior within the same standard alcove space. That extra floor width allows better movement when rinsing off, bathing kids, or setting a shower chair in place. It’s a small geometric change with a big ergonomic payoff, because you get more comfort, less reach, and a cleaner interior layout that installers appreciate for its precision fit.
