A Better Bathtub by Design

A bathtub should make everyday life easier without sacrificing comfort or style. Perfectub brings together a collection of practical design innovations in a familiar 60-inch alcove size.

Why You Should Choose The Perfectub

Convenience and Safety

Brak Tub’s Perfectub features multiple modifications that make it a safer tub to bathe in, and the perfect replacement for your current bathtub. Standard bathtubs have curved interiors, with slick arcs from the sides into the center of the tub. To enter or exit the bath, one foot ends up positioned in the center of the tub while the other foot must extend over the side of the tub. The distance between each foot is wide, off-setting your center of gravity and making it easy to lose balance.

The Perfectub has a concave interior wall, an undercut apron, that allows the occupant to place the interior foot right up against the interior apron wall. This keeps the feet about three inches apart while they exit the tub while maintaining their center of gravity. The result is easy entry and exit, and a safer way to bathe.

In addition, the widening at the top serves as a splash guard redirecting water and soap suds back into the tub (rather than on the floor). Bathe your good dogs, unhappy cats, or giggling children without massive puddles and suds on the floor, making it safer for everyone.

Design Benefits

The ergonomic shape, size, and depth of the Perfectub make it big enough to stretch out in, even for tall and wide users. As a flat bottom alcove tub, it gives you a wide, level floor to stand, sit or stretch out on. In this deep soaking tub, you can submerge more completely and fully enjoy a soothing hot bath, or ice bath. Perfectub’s sturdy, thick acrylic material retains warmth for a longer, more comfortable bath. Choose the 32" tub with a bathing depth of 15" to the overflow, or the 30" tub with a bathing depth of 13" to the overflow. The 60 x 30 deep soaking alcove bathtub matches standard alcove depth, front to back.

Cost Effective

Perfectub is your best choice for the ultimate alcove tub shower combo. Although the tub appears larger than regular tubs, it fits in a standard 60-inch alcove. The built-in solid bottom enables installation without wet mortar bed if your floors are level. So no need to buy the mud or wait for it to dry. Less installation materials, fewer hours of labor, and you end up with a more economical bathroom remodel. In addition, every Perfectub comes with a complete installation kit with a retail value of about $150. Because it fits a standard 60-inch alcove, Perfectub is a straightforward alcove bathtub replacement, and a standard size bathtub replacement that needs no reframing or new plumbing.

Take a look at our cut sheet or installation instructions. Then, look forward to a stylish bathroom and a relaxing soak. The Perfectub is competitively priced but has more volume for water than a Jacuzzi spa tub. And besides, how many times have you actually used the jets in your spa tub?

Patented Design for Unique Designers

Perfectub is unique in the market. Its patented features and benefits make it stand out, and for good reason. It’s easy to install and easy to enjoy. Its patented 1¼" back edge, with the steel rod reinforcement, also creates a sleek, modern appearance. If you add a ¼" shim on the back wall, the back ledge is eliminated and the tile wall flows right into the tub basin, for a clean tile to basin design.

Special Features

Whether your bathroom renovation is focused on accessibility, design, comfort or deep soaking, Perfectub is… well, perfect. Easy access for people with disabilities, older adults, larger users, or anyone who requires special accessibility and mobility modifications (or just wants a delicious, relaxing bath). It is a bathtub for older adults, a bathtub for people with disabilities, and an accessible tub shower combination the whole family can use. If you use a shower chair, or know anyone who does, you know how important it is to sit without wobbling, making it the best alcove tub shower combo for older adults and individuals with disabilities to take showers, as well as baths.

For individuals with compromised ‘depth-perception’ the top lip and bottom of the tub can be done in a different color to make entering and exiting safer.
