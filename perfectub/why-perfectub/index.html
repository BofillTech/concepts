Why Choose The Perfectub by BRAK Tub

Step In and Find Out!

Eventually, all bathrooms need remodelling. Are you looking for a replacement for your bathtub – or are you designing the perfect bathroom from scratch? Are you wondering what the best bathtub on the market is today? Take a look at Perfectub. It’s a revolutionary bathtub that addresses the functionality, accessibility, maintenance, and aesthetic challenges you may be experiencing with your current tub.

Perfectub, by Brak Tub, holds one design patent and eight utility patents [TBC], making it the most unique and specialized deep soaking tub. It’s both stylish and accommodating for users who require safety and ease of use. And that’s all of us.

Why You Should Choose The Perfectub

Here’s why it’s the ideal bathtub replacement if you’re remodelling or looking to install a new tub:

Convenience and Safety

Brak Tub’s Perfectub features multiple modifications that make it a safer tub option and the perfect replacement for your current bathtub. For example, standard bathtubs have curved interiors, with slick arcs from the sides into the center of the tub. To enter or exit the bath, one foot ends up positioned in the center of the tub while the other foot must extend over the side of the tub. The distance between each foot is wide, off-setting your center of gravity and making it easy to lose balance. However, the Perfectub has concave, thin side walls that make it easier to exit the tub or reach active pets and children when bathing them.

An additional safety feature that makes Perfectub the ideal bathtub is similar to the ‘toe-kick’ feature under the cabinets in your kitchen. About 30% more floor space makes everything easier. For example, you can get closer to where you need to be – to wash whatever, or whoever, needs it!

The Perfectub’s side wall widening at the top edge serves as a splash guard, redirecting water and soap suds back into the tub (rather than onto you or your floor). Bathing good dogs, unhappy cats, or giggling children will no longer leave puddles and suds on the floor, making it safer for everyone.

Design Benefits

Cost Effective

Perfectub is your best choice for the ultimate alcove tub shower combo. Although the tub appears larger than regular tubs, it still fits in a standard 60-inch alcove. Due to its built-in solid bottom, you’ll require fewer installation materials, fewer hours of labor, and end up with a more economical bathroom remodel. Take a look at our cut sheet or installation instructions. Then, look forward to a stylish bathroom and a relaxing soak. The Perfectub is competitively priced but has more volume for water than a Jacuzzi spa tub. And besides, how many times have you actually used the jets in your spa tub?

Patented Design

Perfectub is unique in the market. Its patented features and benefits make it stand out, and for good reason. It’s easy to install and easy to enjoy. Its patented back edge lip makes for a sleek, modern appearance. Its specialized internal structure makes it stable and safe, and its larger volume lets larger folks stretch out (and get out) easily.

Special Features

If your bathroom renovations are focused on accessibility, Perfectub is… well, perfect. It’s easier to access for people with disabilities, older adults, larger users, or anyone who requires special accessibility and mobility modifications (or just wants a delicious, relaxing bath). The concave walls and flat floor make a real difference. If you use a shower chair, or know anyone who does, you know how important it is to sit without wobbling, making it the best alcove tub shower combo for older adults and individuals with disabilities to take showers, as well as baths.

For individuals with compromised ‘depth-perception’ the top lip and bottom of the tub can be done in a different color to make entering and exiting safer.
