The Perfectub: An Easy Access Bathtub Shower

By Bruce AhmesNovember 12, 2025

An easy-access bathtub shower is the problem most remodels try to solve without giving up comfort or style. With the Perfectub, Brak Tub designed a modern tub that behaves like a shower pan yet still lets you soak when you want that option.

If you care about bathtub accessibility but don’t want the normal tubs, or you just don’t find them safe, the Perfectub will give you much better options that are not just aesthetic choices but design decisions that make the bathtub safer and more accessible.

Why The Perfectub

When families weigh convenience, a bathtub shower for their elderly relatives often tops the list, but what’s even better is an easy-access bathtub shower like the Perfectub. Here are some reasons why:

  • You get a floor space of over 25 inches [VERIFY], which might not mean much until you want options like a chair-friendly design or a larger tub volume that your loved ones can soak in comfortably.
  • The patented back edge gets rid of that bulky rear ledge, leading to a much safer, larger volume, and cost-effective design.
  • The thin side walls and patented undercut, significantly different from the normal bathtubs are also great because they give you more space for your baths as they have a larger volume, but also, these easy-access bath showers are safer than the normal options when getting in and out of the bathtub, since they reduce slip.
  • You don’t even need a large space for it. The entire bathtub shower fits perfectly in a standard 60 x 30 alcove, so it’s not a problem, even in small spaces. It also comes in a 60 x 32 version.

With the Perfectub, bathtub accessibility is not a sticker claim. The choices we’ve made are deliberate, and you’ll feel how much easier they make your shower experience. Therefore, if you’ve thought about ADA accessible showers or how to make a shower safe for the elderly, you’ll greatly appreciate the Perfectub as a bathtub shower for your elderly loved ones.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can grab bars be added to the Perfectub, and where should they go?

While this bathtub makes accessibility much easier and safer, it also has reinforced walls that accept standard bathroom grab-bars when installed to studs. Most homeowners choose one vertical bar near the entry and a horizontal bar close to the wall. Your installer should confirm stud locations and follow local code and manufacturer torque specs.

What’s different about installing an easy-access bathtub shower like the Perfectub compared to a typical alcove tub?

The rigid, built-in bottom usually means less underlayment, which speeds up the set time, reduces materials, and helps prevent squeaks or shifts later. A wet mortar bed or foam bedding is recommended on uneven or older floors.

We’re torn between a walk-in shower and a tub-shower. When is Perfectub the smarter pick?

If you need fast daily showers and occasional soaks, prefer bathtubs to showers, or want a safe and accessible bathtub shower for your elderly folk, the Perfectub is your best option.

How does the patented back edge help with water control and tiling?

Our easy-access bathtub shower integrates cleanly with tile, which expands interior space. This, however, creates less of an overhang, which leads to fewer awkward caulk lines and fewer spots where water can stall and mildew can grow.
