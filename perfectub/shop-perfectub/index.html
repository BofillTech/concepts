Reasons for Choosing the Perfectub Acrylic Alcove Tub

HIGH GLOSS ACRYLIC SURFACE

Brak Tub ’s quality bathtubs are made out of acrylic, one of the best bathtub materials on the market today. Tubs were first made of heavy cast iron and then subsequently of steel. The problem with cast iron and steel is that when you fill a tub with hot water, the materials are heated up first, and all of the heat is taken out of the water. Today, bathtubs are made out of acrylic, which is a pliable plastic that is very strong reinforced with fiberglass and resin. Acrylic with its fiberglass backing is an insulator, so when you put hot water in your tub, it will stay hot.

Despite being very smooth, the acrylic surface is non-slip, which, along with the tub’s flat bottom, minimizes the risk of slips and falls. In contrast, standard baths curve towards the center, and their thick walls mean that the distance between one leg and the other when climbing in and out is wide. This makes it hard to maintain proper balance, and the bather can fall.

It’s a Perfect Fit

Perfectub is easy to install, and it saves space. Even if you’re working with a small bathroom, the 60-inch alcove Perfectub bathtub will fit snugly into your standard bathtub clearance. Its back edge seamlessly integrates with the tile and secures precisely into steel or wood studs. And, eliminating the need for a back ledge means there’s more space for the bather, and a sleek, modern look.

It’s More Convenient

If you’re comparing bathtubs, Perfectub is great for clients with disabilities, larger bathers, or older adults who need better accessibility. Besides being easier to get in and out of, it has about 30% more floor space than a standard alcove tub. Thanks to its flat, 26” wide [VERIFY] floor surface, it can hold a shower chair without it wobbling. For those who need to bathe small children or animals, the thinner walls allow easier access, making the child or pet closer and easier to bathe.
