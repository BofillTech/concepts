Testimonials and Reviews

At Brak Tub, we reimagined the standard alcove bathtub to solve the pain points of traditional designs. By narrowing the side walls and adding our patented undercut, we’ve created a tub with about 30% more floor space and a flat, non-slip floor that provides a safer, more luxurious soak.

Therefore, whether you’re a homeowner enjoying a more accessible bath or a contractor who appreciates the easier installation, we want to hear from you.

We Value Your Feedback

Your reviews help us continue to innovate and assist other families in finding the right solution for their bathroom remodels. Did the Perfectub make bathing safer for an elderly relative? Did our reinforced, solid-bottom design save you time on a professional installation?

Some things we would love to know include:

  • Telling us about the setup process. Did the built-in base simplify your project?
  • Letting us know how the thin side walls and undercut changed your entry and exit experience.
  • You could describe the difference in bathing volume compared to your previous standard tub.
  • Sharing what you didn’t like about the perfectub or possible areas of improvement.

What Other Customers Are Saying

First of all, thank you SO MUCH for being the only person with some sense to design a tub without alllll that wasted space. I live in South Carolina and I’ve been looking for a tub like this everywhere.
