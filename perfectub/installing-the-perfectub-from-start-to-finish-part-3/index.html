Installing The Perfectub, From Start to Finish Part III

By Jerry GouldJune 25, 2026

Key Takeaways

  • Spread the thinset under every base support pad before the final set.
  • Use shims if needed to completely level the tub.
  • Glue the last PVC joints and set the tub in one controlled move.
  • Let the bed and glue cure, then fasten the flange and finish the drain.
  • Run water, check for leaks, and review the overflow, undercut, and step-out.

This part of our series deals with the commit phase, where the tub is placed down for real, the plumbing gets locked in, and testing is done so you can know whether or not you’re ready to move on to the finishes with confidence.

Use this section as an order-of-operations checklist. Keep it simple: support first, glue second, cure third, then test with water. Let’s go through these steps in a little more detail, as they were done in the video.

1. Prepare the Installation Surface

Start by removing the protective plastic and confirming the installation area is clean, level, and properly supported. Perfectub’s reinforced bottom usually means less prep than a traditional mortar bed in standard installations. The use of a wet mortar bed or foam bedding is not required, but highly recommended when the floor is uneven or older, in the case of most remodeling jobs.

If the floor is uneven, if needed, add one or two shims under the tub to ensure it’s completely level. Using a setting material such as thinset to help create proper contact beneath the tub base. Follow installation guidance and use only when conditions require additional leveling or support.

Once the tub is positioned, verify that it sits evenly before moving to the next steps of installation. A level tub ensures the water smoothly flows toward the drain when emptying.

2. Confirm You’re Ready for the One-Shot Set

Before making the final set, check again that the floor is level and the tub is positioned correctly. Once confirmed, glue up the primed PVC joints, lift the tub in a rabbit trap style, remove every temporary block or spacer, and set the tub straight down onto the connection in one smooth move.

If the tub isn’t fitting just right, stop and reset. Forcing fittings while the glue is wet could twist the traps and lead to misalignments.

3. Verify Level, Then Let It Cure

After confirming that the tub is exactly where it needs to be and is perfectly level, leave the thinset and the PVC glue to cure overnight. Don’t fill the tub with water immediately after setting. Wait for the bed to harden before you do this.

4. Fastening and the Drain Finish

When the curing is done, secure the flange based on the recommendations. The instructions are to drive a screw with a fender washer so it captures the flange without cracking it.

Next, they finish the drain installation. This drain uses a three-prong pattern, so you can use a drain tool that fits that style to remove it, apply plumber’s putty, and then tighten it down. The drain is hand-tightened, so this should be sufficient.

While tightening the drain, keep the PVC below from turning to avoid twisting the connection. When done, wipe away any putty that squeezes out.

Don’t be surprised if the drain looks slightly off-center when you first look at it. In the video, it’s explained as being caused by the tub’s internal slope, not a bad alignment.

5. Water Test

For the water test, fill the tub, drain it, and check underneath while the water is running. If there are any leaks, this will be seen easily by the water dropping on the concrete. Then confirm that the tub drains properly, even before the venting work is fully finished.

Frequently Asked Questions

Don’t I need a mortar bed or foam blocks like other acrylic tubs?

Not usually. Perfectub features a heavy triple-layered reinforcement under the tub that is designed to provide support on a smooth, level subfloor. The use of a wet mortar bed or foam bedding is not required, but highly recommended when the floor is uneven or older, in the case of most remodeling jobs.

How does a built-in linear overflow differ from a traditional one?

The built-in linear overflow eliminates the need for external hardware and allows the water level to be filled higher for a deeper soak.

Doesn’t the tub’s side bow under the weight of the floor tile?

The Perfectub has steel embedded within the acrylic/fiberglass to reinforce the side wall, ensuring that the weight of the tile doesn’t push the flange down.
