The Perfectub Just Got “Perfecter”

By Bruce AhmesAugust 28, 2025

New Models, Greater Depth, Linear Overflow, and Unmatched Value Now Available

Northport, NY — July 23, 2025 — Brak Tub, the makers of Perfectub, have announced that a new shipment of their signature bathtub is now available. The latest shipment of this breakthrough bathtub now contains several innovative new additions, while still boasting the many patented features that make Perfectub unique.

Perfectub is proud to announce the inclusion of a linear drain on all models. The new linear overflow now makes the Perfectub more perfect and allows customers to save over $200. The Perfectub’s linear overflow gives it a sleek, modern look, and a deeper bathing depth. The Perfectub also now features a discreet white toe-touch pop-up for easy filling and draining.

Bathers can look forward to the same safe and stable exit and entrance thanks to the carefully designed height and the wide, flat surface on the bottom. However, they’ll now also be able to enjoy an additional 2.5” of depth [VERIFY] for increased submersion and a more luxurious bathing experience, due to the linear overflow. So we’ve added a no-cost linear overflow, while keeping the same 60” x 30” dimensions that make the Perfectub such a simple addition to standard bathtub alcove spaces.

Brak Tub has also announced that Perfectubs are now available in a 60” x 32” model. This new addition offers even more comfort, accessibility, and spa-like design for modern bathrooms. It adds more flat surface space, along with extended height. Our 32-inch tub meets the apron height requirements for an ADA bathroom installation. Like the 60” x 30” model, this 32” version still features the signature easy step-in and step-out and signature undercut for comfort and splash-control.

About The Perfectub

The Perfectub by Brak Tub is a leading innovator in bathtub design, combining modern aesthetics with accessibility and convenience. With a focus on thoughtful engineering and customer-first value, it makes everyday bathing feel like a spa experience, without complicated installations or expensive upgrades.

About Bruce Ahmes

Bruce Ahmes is the inventor of the Perfectub, as well as a home builder, contractor, real estate developer, and proud father and grandfather. He has built beautiful homes on Long Island for over 50 years.

His father, Albert Ahmes, was a head structural engineer at Grumman, leading the development of the Apollo Mission’s Lunar Excursion Module. This inventive spirit and experience gave Bruce exclusive insights into the possibilities for a better bathtub and to develop the many unique, patented features that define the Perfectub. Now, more people than ever can enjoy a safer, more comfortable, and more accessible luxury bath.
