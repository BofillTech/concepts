What Features To Look For in the Best Bathtubs for Kids

By Bruce AhmesFebruary 27, 2026

Rundown

  • Families need tubs that make bathing small children easier to manage.
  • Tub layout and floor space directly affect stability and control.
  • Certain designs and materials extend usage, reduce slipping, awkward reach, and unnecessary adjustments.
  • Perfectub offers these practical advantages in a standard 60×30 as well as a 60×32 footprint.

Families deserve the best bathtubs for their kids, even in cases where walk-in showers might seem more practical. A well-designed bathtub helps parents stay drier, and children have soothing fun while getting clean.

And, materials matter: cast iron tubs are rock hard and unforgiving, fiberglass tubs are so fragile that a five-year-old could actually break the surface. The acrylic of Perfectub gives the perfect balance of a durable material and toughness to withstand abuse.

We will explore more therapeutic benefits in this article while also discussing the important precautions you need to understand and consider when it comes to bathtub safety for kids.

Warm Water in The Tub Helps Them Wind Down

Sitting in warm water has a different effect on the body than showering. The tub creates a calming environment, and children become more cooperative during the routine. It’s a great way to end a busy day and ease into bedtime.

The best bathtubs for kids retain heat reliably, allowing parents to complete this routine without rushing and without worrying about bathwater getting cold, and children catching a chill.

Why Tub Design Matters When Bathing Young Children

A lot of parents don’t really study a tub until they’ve gone through a few baths, and something keeps getting in the way. Maybe the child keeps slipping into one area, or the space just doesn’t feel right during baths.

Bathtub safety and comfort for kids (and parents) are important. To the parent, it becomes clear after a few baths that layout matters more than people assume. The best bathtubs for kids utilize innovative design features like narrower side walls, an undercut, and no sloping sides to make baths safer.

When all the elements of a safe and comfortable design work together, parents can move along the tub more comfortably and maintain steady supervision without dealing with awkward angles or constant adjustments.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does tub design play into bathtub safety for kids?

A well-designed bathtub lets a parent have easy access to the child in the tub. There’s enough room to move around the child, but it still keeps them within quick reach. The routine feels safer, because it is. Flat bottomed tubs prevents your child from slipping away from your reach.

Is acrylic a good material for family tubs?

Some of the best bathtubs for kids use acrylic, and parents like it because it stays warm, doesn’t feel flimsy, and is easy to clean. An acrylic bathtub is not as hard and unforgiving as cast iron, and is much more durable than fiberglass.

Why are concave walls important in a bathtub?

Perfectub’s narrow walls with featured undercut put you closer to the child in a bathtub, which makes scrubbing and rinsing them easier and more comfortable, since you’re not stretching over the edge the whole time. An undercut, like the one the Perfectub has, reduces the amount of water that gets splashed over the edge of the tub. This reduces the chances of slipping once bath time is over.

Is a standard 60×30 tub enough for most families?

This is a standard alcove bathtub size. It fits the usual bathroom layout, and has plenty of room for at least two small children and their favorite bath time toys! For renovations that have a 60×32 tub space already built out, we also have a 60×32 tub.
