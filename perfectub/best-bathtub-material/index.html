Best Bathtub Material

By Bruce AhmesNovember 24, 2025

Key Takeaways

  • The material allows comfort, cleaning, durability, and practicality of the bathtub
  • Acrylic and fiberglass are light and budget-friendly
  • Cast iron is heavy, strong, and long-lasting, but it affects water temperature
  • Perfectub from Brak Tub is acrylic with more space, easier exit and entry, easy installation, and more convenience. Fiberglass is applied to the outside for increased durability and insulation. As a result, bathwater stays warmer than in a cast-iron tub.

When people are thinking about remodeling their bathrooms, the attention is often placed on tiles, lighting, drainage, the color of the walls, and so on. But the bathtub is an important consideration. Learn about the best bathtub materials, and more about the differences like acrylic vs. fiberglass bathtub s and acrylic vs cast iron bathtub s.

Your tub’s material will influence the feel of your bathroom. The acrylic surface of Perfectub is shiny and appealing; the bottom has a bubble texture for gripping, but is pleasing to the eye. It will also affect the comfort of a soak, how easy it is to clean, how long the temperature of your bathwater remains comfortable, and even how long the tub will last.

When choosing a bathtub material, don’t just focus on appearance. Make sure that your bathroom works for you, and that your bathtub remains an enjoyable investment.

Why Bathtub Materials Matter

Many popular bathtub materials have advantages and disadvantages, but the best bathtub material depends on your needs and what you’ll find convenient and durable.

The options are many, but the best way to know what your priorities are is to think about your daily routines, who’s bathing on a regular basis, long-term care, your housing situation, what kind of bathing experience you want, your design preferences, and so on.

We’ve spent time considering every aspect of the Perfectub’s design. The shape, angles, design, and materials have all been chosen for the best possible bathing experience and long-term value.

The Most Common Bathtub Choices

For many owners, this decision comes down to three familiar materials: acrylic, fiberglass, and cast iron. Comparing acrylic vs. fiberglass bathtub s and acrylic vs cast iron bathtub s should be an important part of your decision-making.

Acrylic and fiberglass options are popular for those who want a light tub with excellent value. Cast-iron is a bathtub material that can be a solid option with antique flair; however, it’s prone to chipping and draws heat out of bathwater. However, this isn’t always feasible for all constructions or budgets.

Other Options on the Market

These are common materials, but there are others like steel, stone, resin, and copper tubs. Each serves unique needs, but they remind us that the best bathtub material is not one single answer. It’s about what fits your home, your style, and your expectations.
