Advantages of An Alcove Tub

  • An alcove soaking tub delivers an indulgent deep soak while maximizing space use.
  • When in a hurry, it also provides a safer, accessible, and comfortable shower for when you don’t have time for a bath.
  • An alcove tub’s benefits include saving on space, greater comfort, easy maintenance, and cost-effective installation, among others.
  • The Perfectub stands out as the best alcove tub and one of the most practical solutions for small bathrooms or even extensive layouts. It delivers the ultimate, luxurious, and relaxing bathing experience.

That’s the Perfectub – a thoughtfully designed tub that blends comfort with an innovative, space-saving design.

The Perfectub strikes the perfect balance between everyday practicality and elevated comfort, earning its place among the top bathtubs. Let’s explore the benefits of alcove tubs to better understand what sets the Perfectub apart.

What is an Alcove Bathtub?

Before we dive into the benefits, let’s first define what an alcove soaking tub is. Simply, it’s a

type of tub installed in a recessed space, typically measuring 60×30, between three walls, with one side open, facing the bathroom. This layout makes it ideal for compact bathrooms because it maximizes wall space, while still providing a comfortable full-size bathing experience.

Unlike traditional tubs that require extra floor space for freestanding placement, an alcove design, like that of the Perfectub, fits seamlessly into corners and tight nooks. It’s the perfect solution for small bathrooms. Find a Showroom to see the tub for yourself and weigh how well a Perfectub will fit in your alcove.

The Benefits of an Alcove Soaking Tub

Here are some of the advantages the Perfectub brags about:

1. It Uses Available Space Efficiently

Among the top alcove tub benefits is its ability to fit snugly into a recessed alcove, maximizing available floor space without compromising the full soaking experience.

For homeowners looking for the perfect tub for small bathrooms, Perfectub’s design lets you enjoy full-volume bathing without having to expand the room’s layout, but if you have the room to spare, consider the 32-inch option.

2. It Provides Superior Comfort and Relaxation

The true luxury of an alcove soaking tub lies in its depth, and the Perfectub tub does this well. It’s designed with generous depth and width, providing about 30% more floor space than other alcove tubs, with plenty of room for your arms to lie idly on each side. A deeper water level helps soothe tired muscles, eases stress, and leaves you feeling fully rejuvenated.

3. Its Installation is Cost-Effective

Another of the alcove tub benefits is its affordability. Compared to drop-in or freestanding tubs, alcove soaking tubs are generally easier and less expensive to install. Since the tub fits perfectly between walls, the installation materials and plumbing modifications needed are minimal. This cost-efficiency is especially sought after by homeowners looking for smart solutions for small bathrooms, particularly those working on a budget.

4. It’s Easy to Clean and Maintain

With the three sides of an alcove soaking tub enclosed by walls, there are fewer exposed surfaces where dust, soap scum, or water can accumulate. So, the tub is easier to clean.

Also, like the Perfectub, some models are made from the best bathroom material, which is stain-resistant, simple to maintain, and still one of the strongest materials available.

This makes an alcove soaking tub a practical choice for families, rental properties, and other users looking for low-maintenance bathroom fixtures.

Make the Most of Your Bathroom Space

An alcove soaking tub isn’t just functional; it’s a stylish and comfortable way to make the most of your bathroom spa. It’s perfect for all types of users, and especially for homeowners looking for bathing solutions for their small bathrooms. The tub also delivers deep soaking comfort and is easy to maintain. Its features make its installation cost-effective.

FAQs

Are alcove soaking tubs expensive?

Alcove tubs are generally more affordable than freestanding tubs. Plus, they offer excellent value for their layout and comfort.

Can I install a shower together with the alcove tub?

Yes. Perfectub gives you the best showering platform with the safest access possible. You can have a shower-tub combo, adding versatility to your bathing options in the same space.

Is an alcove soaking tub different from a standard alcove tub?

Yes. A soaking tub is deeper and lets you immerse more of your body, while standard tubs are typically shallower and intended for basic bathing or showering.
