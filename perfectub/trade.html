Questions people ask

Do you offer trade or volume pricing?

Yes. Call Jerry at (781) 912-3836 for pricing. We verify each business before sharing trade pricing.

Can I get cut sheets?

Yes. Request cut sheets and installation guides with the form, and see the published dimensions on the resources page.

How is the tub set?

Support the tub from below in a wet mortar bed or foam, as the installation guide directs. The subfloor must be smooth, level and supported.

Is it ADA compliant?

When the 60 x 32 is installed properly, the height of its apron is compliant with ADA (Americans with Disabilities Act) standards. Call to discuss a specific project's requirements.

What is the warranty on commercial installations?

Commercial installations are covered for one year. Residential installations carry a limited lifetime warranty for the original owner.
