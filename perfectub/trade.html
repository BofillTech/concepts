Questions people ask

Do you offer trade or volume pricing?

Yes. Trade pricing is provided on request. Use the form on this page or call Jerry at (781) 912-3836. We verify each business before sharing pricing.

Can I get cut sheets?

Yes. Request cut sheets and installation guides with the form, and see the published dimensions on the resources page.

How is the tub set?

The tub is self-supporting and must be installed on a smooth level sub-floor. The floor should be supported and level in BOTH DIRECTIONS and on each board (back, middle and front). The use of Structo-Lite is not required, but highly recommended when the floor is uneven or older, in the case of most remodeling jobs.

Can it be used in an ADA bathroom?

Our 32" tub meets the height requirements for an ADA bathroom installation. A full ADA (Americans with Disabilities Act) bathroom also needs other features, such as grab bars and a seat.

What is the warranty on commercial installations?

Commercial installations are covered for one year. Residential installations carry a limited lifetime warranty for the original owner.
