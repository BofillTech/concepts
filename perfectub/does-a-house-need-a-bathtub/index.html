Does a House Need a Bathtub?

By Bruce AhmesJuly 24, 2026

Key Takeaways

  • A house doesn’t always need a bathtub, but having one makes it more practical.
  • A bathtub can help with resale value, especially for buyers with young children and pets.
  • A well-designed bathtub can make bathing children and pets a lot easier.
  • A well-designed bathtub offers more versatility than a shower pan as your needs change.
  • The right bathtub can optimally use your bathroom space while adding comfort to your baths.

If you’re having questions like “ Does a house need a bathtub?” or “ Do I need a tub in my house?” You’re in the right place to help you decide whether a bathtub would be great for you.

Showers are convenient, so why install a bathtub? From taking up too much space to collecting soap scum, becoming slippery when wet, and being rarely used by most people, some bathtubs can be a messy addition to your bathroom.

But when done right, a bathtub addition makes your bathroom feel complete, due to the many advantages it comes with. Let’s discuss some of the benefits of having one.

Future Property Resale Value

A house does need a bathtub because properties without a single tub get very little buyer interest. Many buyers, especially families with young children, prefer at least one full bathroom with a tub.

Without one, the property feels less flexible. It may not ruin the resale value, but it will narrow the group of buyers who see the layout as a fit.

Making Family and Pet Bathing Easier

It can be tough to wash children and pets in a shower; a tub gives you a contained bathing area. That’s another reason to say yes to questions like “ Does a house need a bathtub?”

The innovative interior shaping of the Perfectub makes the process of bathing the kids and pets a lot easier. That means less reaching, less splashing of water, a reduced risk of slipping, and a more manageable routine.

Better Use of Alcove Space

Another reason why you should install a bathtub is that today, you don’t have to worry about the cramped, chilly alcoves that can’t fit the bulky standard setups.

Today, with a bathtub that can fit a standard 60×30 ft alcove, having one in your house can be a great use of that bathroom space. The patented Perfectub design expands the space and usability of the standard-sized alcove.

Standard setups use bulky, hollow wall shapes that are easier and cheaper to mold in the factory but limit the accessibility and comfort of the tub. If this is something that bothers you about bathtubs, there are options that use your alcove space optimally.

What To Consider When Picking a Bathtub

If you’re still asking, “ Does a house need a bathroom?” or “ Do you really need a bathtub in your house?” The short answer is yes. However, the type of bathtub you choose will make your experience worth it or messy. Here are some considerations to look out for.

  • Interior Space: Choose a tub that gives you more room to sit, soak, and move comfortably without needing a larger bathroom footprint.
  • Wall design: Look for a design that makes it easier to climb in and out, reach children or pets, and reduce awkward bending.
  • Material Quality: High-quality acrylic is a strong choice because it retains warmth, holds its color, and offers durable everyday performance.
  • Installation Needs: A built-in solid bottom can reduce the extra installation materials, while a clean back edge can help the tub fit more smoothly with tile walls.
  • Long-term Use: Pick a tub that works for today’s routines while still making sense for aging users, guests, families, and future buyers.
  • Curb Appeal: Choosing a nicely designed, attractive tub could never be wrong. Perhaps one fitted with a linear overflow, rather than the round hole, can add to the attractiveness of the tub.
