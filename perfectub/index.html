Reasons to Choose the Perfectub Alcove Tub

  • Greater ease when bathing others, including wiggly small children and pets! Narrower side walls allow you better reach, and their concave design keeps splashing water inside the tub.
  • Looking for a bathtub that is easy to get in and out of? You’ve found it. The thinner walls and flat bottom make it easier to enter and exit the tub without losing balance.
  • The Perfectub design adds about 30% more floor space. Even larger bathers can stretch out and be more submerged, for a warmer, more relaxing bath.
  • Perfectub’s high-quality, durable acrylic bathtub material is easy to clean and offers greater protection against slippage: a safer way to bathe.
  • The acrylic material does not absorb heat from the water. Instead, the heat is retained, and the water stays warm throughout the bath.
  • Our perfect alcove tub features a modern, stylish, and luxurious design that complements a wide range of bathroom designs. The patented thin back wall is reinforced with steel for durability. It also makes installation virtually seamless and regular cleaning easy.

Who Is The Perfectub Designed For?

The Brak Tub Perfectub is designed for all bathtub users. Everyone wants the perfect bathtub. And, they can have it! The Perfectub is a standard 60-inch alcove tub, making it easy to incorporate into most bathrooms.

Individuals with mobility challenges, such as disabilities, aging, and those with larger builds looking for a more accessible bathtub, will find that the Perfectub addresses the accessibility concerns they may have experienced with standard bathtubs.

A household with small children and pets will find the Perfectub a more convenient way to get everyone clean. And, those who want a luxurious bathing experience but are challenged by limited space can relax and enjoy a long soak in a safer and more efficient bathing space.

Don’t find yourself with much time for long leisurely baths? The Perfectub still shines. Its spacious flat floor makes it a great place to shower, and still gives you the ability to soak when the opportunity arises. And, you’ll be boosting the resale value of your home by including a bathtub as a feature.

Questions people ask

Will it fit my existing alcove?

Yes, if your alcove is the standard 60 inches long. Pick the 30 or 32 inch depth to match your space.

Can I use a shower chair in it?

Yes. The flat floor and narrow-radius corners leave room for a standard shower chair to sit level.

Is it a walk-in tub?

No. There's no door. It's a standard-size alcove tub shaped to be easier to get in and out of.

Is it deep enough for an ice bath?

Yes. The 60 x 32 holds up to 15 inches [VERIFY] of water to the overflow, and the 60 x 30 about 13 inches [VERIFY]. The linear overflow lets you fill higher than a standard overflow plate.

Does it keep water off the floor?

Yes. The undercut works as a built-in splash guard, so water stays in the tub instead of running onto the bathroom floor.

Is it good for bathing kids or dogs?

Yes. The undercut brings kids and dogs closer to you, and the flat floor gives them steady footing.

Is the floor non-skid, and is it comfortable to sit on?

The soft, comfortable bubbles on the tub floor interrupt the hydroplaning layer of water, which is the real reason surfaces get slippery. So the floor is non-skid, and still comfortable to sit on. Most bathroom slips and falls come from water on the floor outside the tub, and the undercut helps prevent that.

How can I adapt it in an ADA-compliant bathroom?

Our 32-inch tub meets the apron height requirements for an ADA bathroom installation.

What comes in the box?

The tub, a linear overflow and the installation kit, including the drain and toe-cap.

How is it shipped?

By freight on a pallet, with shipping included in the price anywhere in the U.S.

What is the warranty?

A limited lifetime warranty for the original owner, for a tub installed in a home and used and cared for normally. Commercial installations are covered for one year, and accessories for parts only, for one year.
