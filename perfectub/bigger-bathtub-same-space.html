Questions people ask

Will it fit my alcove exactly?

Yes, if your alcove is the standard 60 inches long. Measure front to back to choose the 30 or 32 inch model, and match the drain side to your plumbing.

How deep is it?

The 60 x 30 holds about 13 inches of water to the overflow, and the 60 x 32 up to 15 inches.

Can I shower in it too?

Yes. The flat, non-skid floor gives steady footing for showering, and the undercut works as a built-in splash guard.

Do I need to move walls or plumbing?

No. It's a standard 60-inch alcove tub and installs like one, with the waste and overflow kit in the box.

What does it cost?

$1,389 for the 60 x 30 and $1,496 for the 60 x 32, with shipping included.
