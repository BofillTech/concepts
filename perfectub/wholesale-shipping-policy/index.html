Wholesale Shipping, Inspection & Return Policy

For wholesale distributors receiving Perfectub shipments from Brak Tub Corp. Consumer deliveries use the Delivery, Repair & Replacement Policy.

Brak Tub Corp. takes great care in packaging and shipping Perfectub products to our wholesale distributors. Because wholesale orders may include multiple bathtubs delivered at one time, we recognize that it may not always be practical to completely unpack and inspect every bathtub while the carrier is present.

This Wholesale Shipping, Inspection & Damage Policy is intended to provide a reasonable inspection period while establishing clear procedures for reporting and resolving freight-related damage.

1. Inspection at Time of Delivery

Whenever reasonably possible, the distributor should inspect the shipment at the time of delivery before signing the carrier’s Proof of Delivery (“POD”) or delivery receipt.

At a minimum upon arrival of the merchandise, the recipient should:

  • Confirm the quantity of cartons, pallets, or units received against the shipping documentation.
  • Inspect the exterior packaging, pallets, cartons, and other visible portions of the shipment for signs of damage.
  • Take photographs of any visible damage before the shipment is moved or unpacked.
  • Take photographs of the overall condition of the shipment upon arrival whenever possible.

Perfectubs shipped individually or in palletized shipments leave Brak Tub Corp.’s warehouse secured on pallets. If a shipment that was designated as palletized arrives without the applicable pallet(s), this should be noted on the Proof of Delivery and photographed.

Signing a POD without noting visible damage may limit Brak Tub Corp.’s ability to pursue a freight claim and may affect the distributor’s eligibility for a replacement or credit for damage that was reasonably visible at the time of delivery.

2. Inspection Following Delivery

Brak Tub Corp. understands that wholesale shipments containing multiple tubs may require additional time for a complete inspection.

Distributors are therefore responsible for unpacking and inspecting all tubs promptly following delivery.

For shipments of 12 or more tubs, any concealed shipping damage must be reported to Brak Tub Corp. within five (5) business days of delivery.

Damage discovered during inspection should be reported as soon as it is found rather than waiting until the end of the applicable inspection period.

Claims submitted after the applicable inspection period may not be eligible for replacement, credit, or compensated repairs.

3. Reporting Damaged Tubs

For each tub claimed to have been damaged during shipping, the distributor should provide:

  • The applicable purchase order, invoice, or shipment number;
  • The model and quantity affected;
  • Photographs clearly showing the damaged area;
  • A photograph showing the entire tub;
  • Photographs of the carton and packaging associated with the damaged tub, when available;
  • Photographs of the pallet or shipment if the damage may be related to freight handling;
  • A copy of the carrier’s POD or delivery receipt; and
  • A brief description of the damage, when and how it was discovered.

Damaged tubs and their packaging should not be discarded until Brak Tub Corp. has completed its review or advised the distributor that disposal is permitted.

Brak Tub Corp. may request additional photographs or information reasonably necessary to evaluate the claim.

4. Full-Container Damage Allowance

Full-container orders include two (2) additional Perfectubs at no additional charge specifically as a shipping and handling damage allowance.

These additional tubs are intended to compensate the distributor for anticipated incidental damage that may occur.

Accordingly, for a full-container order, the distributor’s first two (2) damaged tubs are covered by the included damage allowance and will not qualify for replacement, refund, or credit from Brak Tub Corp.

5. Damage Exceeding the Full-Container Allowance

If more than two tubs in a full-container shipment are damaged, the distributor must notify Brak Tub Corp. within the seven (7) business-day inspection period and provide the required documentation for all damaged units.

Brak Tub Corp. will review documented damage exceeding the two-tub allowance on a case-by-case basis.

Where Brak Tub Corp. determines that an eligible claim exceeds the included damage allowance, Brak Tub Corp. may, at its discretion and subject to the terms of the applicable order, provide a replacement product or a product credit.

The inclusion of the two-tub damage allowance does not eliminate the distributor’s responsibility to inspect the shipment, document visible freight damage, or timely report concealed damage.

6. Damaged Products Must Not Be Installed

A tub that appears damaged or defective should not be installed, modified, drilled, cut, repaired, or otherwise altered before Brak Tub Corp. has reviewed the claim.

Installation or modification of a tub constitutes acceptance of its visible condition and may make the tub ineligible for replacement or credit for visible damage.

If there is any question as to whether a tub can be used, the distributor should contact Brak Tub Corp. before installation or resale.

7. Damage Occurring After Delivery

The distributor is responsible for protecting and properly storing the tubs after delivery.

This policy is intended to address damage attributable to transportation or delivery and does not cover damage occurring after delivery as a result of improper storage, warehouse handling, forklift or equipment damage, movement between locations, installation, job-site handling, misuse, or other circumstances occurring after the shipment has been delivered.

8. Returns of Undamaged Wholesale Products

Wholesale purchases are not returnable.

No product should be returned to Brak Tub Corp. without receiving return authorization and shipping instructions.

Any authorized return must be unused, uninstalled, unmodified, and in resalable condition. Authorized returns may be subject to return freight charges, restocking fees, and other applicable costs as communicated by Brak Tub Corp. at the time the return is authorized.

Special orders, container orders, and other specially priced or negotiated wholesale purchases may be designated as final sale and non-returnable.

9. Our Goal

Our goal is to make receiving and inspecting a wholesale Perfectub shipment as straightforward as possible.

Prompt inspection and good documentation protect both the distributor and Brak Tub Corp. and allow us to address legitimate freight issues quickly and fairly.

When in doubt: photograph the shipment, note visible damage on the Proof of Delivery, retain the packaging, and contact Brak Tub Corp. promptly.
