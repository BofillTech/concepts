A Safer, Easy In-and-Out Bathtub

When safety is a priority, every design detail matters. The Perfectub uses a high-gloss acrylic surface that’s remarkably smooth but inherently non-slip. Along with its proprietary engineering and flat bottom, it stands out as a tub that offers a safer bathing experience. That means fewer worrying moments when getting out of the Perfectub.

Features and Benefits of Acrylic Bathtub Surfaces

A Bathtub Designed for Easy Access

Stepping in and out of bathtubs can be tricky, especially ones with far reaching curves and curved bottoms. The Perfectub has thin side walls with a patented undercut, and a wide 26½″ [VERIFY] flat floor that lets your feet be planted firmly. No more balancing act between one high step up and another across a wide front apron. This makes it the easiest bathtub to get in and out of, especially for older adults or those with mobility concerns. It’s a beautiful sleek tub, with an easy access design.

For those who need visual cues, the tub is available with a two-tone color contrast to help people with limited vision or depth perception see the surfaces more clearly.

A Bathtub That Reduces Splashes

If you have kids or pets that you give soapy baths, you’ll find this design decision delightful: the Perfectub has bell-shaped interior walls that repels water back into the tub, and off the bathroom floor. The slimmer exterior (apron) wall allows you to get closer to the bathtub while bathing kids or pets.

Warmth, Durability, and Traction

With enameled cast iron bathtubs, the metal conducts the heat, sucking the warmth out of your water. This is not the case with acrylic. This material acts as an insulator, retaining your bathwater’s warmth longer. This acrylic is tough, too, since it’s reinforced with fiberglass. So, in addition to this bathtub’s easy access, it also offers improved comfort and durability.

Professional Appeal, Consumer Confidence

For contractors or remodelers, recommending Perfectub means an easy installation and an instant bathroom upgrade. With a built-in solid base that usually means less prep (a wet mortar bed or foam bedding is recommended on uneven or older floors), thin side walls for more space, and a patented “invisible” back edge that installs cleanly under the tile, the Perfectub is a pleasure to work with. You can even present its easy in and out exit, as credentials for a unique value proposition during sales. Homeowners get more than a great-looking tub and a boost to their home’s value. They get peace of mind and safer bathing every day.
