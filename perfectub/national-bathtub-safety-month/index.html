National Bathtub Safety Month

By Bruce AhmesFebruary 25, 2026

January marks National Bath Safety Month, a time dedicated to raising awareness about preventing bathroom accidents like slips, falls, and drownings, especially for seniors and young children. It’s a reminder that one of the most common places for household accidents is also one of the most familiar: the bathtub. For Brak Tub, this month is an opportunity for us to highlight how simple bathtub safety upgrades, as in the Perfectub, can make a meaningful difference in bathroom safety.

The Need for Bathroom Safety Awareness

Bathtubs are designed for hygiene and relaxation, but water and soap on their smooth surfaces can rapidly turn slippery and hazardous. Slips, falls, and bruises from them are common. A reported 43,000 kids go to the emergency rooms every year, which is about 120 kids a day. That’s not mentioning the number of injuries and related deaths among adults aged 65 years and older. The number goes as high as 235,000 people injured in the bathroom each year.

Bathrooms are often high-risk areas, making it essential to take practical steps to improve safety for everyone. So, it’s not surprising that we wholeheartedly celebrate a National Bathtub Safety Month.

The Perfectub is Enhancing Bathroom Safety

Bathtub accidents typically happen during routine moments, such as when stepping into the tub, standing up, or adjusting footing while bathing. The Perfectub is designed with patented features to reduce these risks in the following ways:

  • The Perfectub is an easy-access tub. Its concave, slim sidewalls significantly narrow the distance between both feet, making it easier to exit the tub without losing balance. It’s also easy to reach children and pets when bathing them.
  • The Perfectub features a large tub volume and a flat floor, providing enough space to securely accommodate a shower chair without wobbling, thereby improving bathroom safety for elderly users and individuals with mobility challenges.
  • The Perfectub’s side walls widen at the top edge, serving as a splash guard that directs water and soap suds back into the tub, rather than onto your feet or floor, reducing the risk of slips.
  • The Perfectub can be customized to assist individuals with compromised depth perception, making it safer to enter or exit (custom order feature).

Bathroom Safety is a Priority

The National Bathtub Safety Month is a reminder that ensuring safety in the bathroom remains a priority for households. Bathrooms are one of the most frequently used spaces in any home, and also one of the most dangerous. However, by combining smart designs with a focus on real-life needs, Perfectub is enhancing bathroom safety for the elderly, physically-challenged individuals, children, pets, and everyone in the household.

This month, and all year long, is the perfect time to assess your bathtub safety and take steps toward a safer bathing experience. Fill out a contact form to learn more about the tub.
