What to Know Before Your Bathroom’s Bathtub Remodel

By Bruce AhmesDecember 15, 2025

The Rundown

  • Measure twice before ordering any tub. (Keep in mind that the wall materials in your current bathroom are covering parts of the tub.)
  • Stability and fit matter more than style.
  • Plan installation details early to avoid rework.
  • Interior shape affects comfort more than overall size.

If you’re going to change the look and feel of your bathroom with a remodel, a bathtub is one of the fixtures that makes the most difference and even affects how you use your bathroom in general.

But before you start looking at tile samples or picking faucets, the real work begins. That’s where you have to consider everything from how to choose a bathtub to how bathtubs are installed.

At Brak Tub, we know how important this is: we’ve designed an entire bathtub that’s safer, more accessible, practical, and useful. It’s also easy to install. So, let’s guide you through what you need to know before your bathroom remodel.

Start With Your Space, Not The Style

Every project starts with a footprint. In the U.S., where most homes use a 60-inch by 30 or 32-inch alcove, the replacements might seem straightforward. But with so many options, you’ll need to consider other factors to choose the best bathtub for a remodel.

Take measurements twice: once with walls open (stud-to-stud) and again after drywall or tile. A lot of remodel headaches start when those numbers don’t line up. Leave room for your wall board, tile thickness, and the flange of the tub itself. Even a quarter inch off can throw off a shower door install later.

When you start planning the bathroom’s bathtub remodel, remember to explore some possible alcove options. In most homes, keeping the original alcove layout ends up being simpler and cheaper, and it usually looks just as good once everything’s finished.

The Small Details that Make Big Differences

Knowing how to choose a tub involves looking beyond color and sheen. Check the back edge profile (the area where the tile meets the tub). A well-designed edge helps the wall system tie in cleanly, reducing the risk of water getting behind the tile. This is an important consideration because how these bathtubs are installed can greatly affect your experience.

Modern alcove tubs have a built-in tile flange for that joint. Not all tile flanges are equal, though. The Perfectub has sturdy, deep flanges for the best leak-proofing.

The shape of the tub floor is important in a bathroom bathtub remodel. A flatter, sturdier base gives you more standing room if you’re looking for a bathroom remodel with a tub shower combo. It also feels safer underfoot.

If you’ve ever stepped into a tub that flexes slightly when you shift weight, that’s what you want to avoid.

Interior Comfort Is More Than Length

Two tubs can measure the same outside but feel completely different inside. That’s because of how the walls curve and how deep the basin sits.

When taking on a bathroom bathtub remodel, it’s important to know that some modern alcove models manage to create about 30% more floor space in the same footprint by pushing the sides outward and flattening the floor. That’s the Perfectub.

When you shop, even if you think you know how to choose a tub, sit in the display units. Check out elbow space, shoulder angle, and whether your knees come above the overflow drain. Those small physical details can determine whether you’ll actually use the tub as intended or avoid it entirely.

Support, Leveling, and What’s Underneath

A tub is only as good as its foundation. How bathtubs are installed keeps changing in modern bathroom bathtub remodels. Traditionally, installers use a layer of bedding compound under the base to keep things level and stop squeaks.

Some newer tubs, like the Perfectub, come with a solid pre-leveled bottom, which saves both time and materials. It also makes movement, cracking, and creaking less likely.

If you’re hiring a plumber, ask whether the tub you choose needs additional bedding or not. It’s a small question that can prevent a very expensive redo.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical bathroom bathtub remodel take?

About one to two weeks, depending on the complexity of the job. A direct alcove replacement with pre-leveled tubs, like the Perfectub, can shorten that timeline significantly by eliminating bedding compounds and reducing alignment adjustments.

Do acrylic tubs last as long as cast-iron ones?

Acrylics hold up really well when made of reinforced resin backing. While cast iron can last many a lifetime, modern acrylic keeps heat longer, resists chipping, and is far easier to install. This is why they’re preferred for many bathroom bathtub remodels.

Does the Perfectub support grab bar or handrail installation?

Grab bars can be added to the walls of the bathroom, or anywhere that is needed surrounding the Perfectub.

How is the Perfectub different from other tubs?

The Perfectub combines installation simplicity with structural precision. Its pre-leveled base usually means fewer bedding materials (a wet mortar bed or foam bedding is recommended on uneven or older floors), the patented back-edge lip integrates seamlessly with wall tile, and its wider interior floor improves standing comfort and water containment. All these, and more, combine to form a safer and practical tub for everyday use.
