Installation and Warranty

Before installing a tub from Brak Tub, it’s important to review the installation instructions in full and prepare the alcove, subfloor, drain area, and surrounding framing correctly. Here, we’ll discuss the BKTB-100-LH, BKTB-100-RH, (60x30s) and BKTB-300’s (60x32s) models.

Here, you’ll find a clear on-site guide to the main installation and warranty information. For project-specific questions, installers should confirm code requirements and review the full installation before the work begins.

Before Installation Begins

Good preparation helps prevent avoidable installation problems. Before setting the tub, the installer should confirm that the alcove, subfloor, drain area, and surrounding framing are ready.

Therefore, before the work begins, check that:

  • Local plumbing and building code requirements have been reviewed.
  • The tub has been inspected before installation begins to avoid ‘voiding’ the warranty.
  • Selected fittings and drain components are ready.
  • The installation area is clear and accessible.
  • The acrylic surface is protected from scratches.
  • Non-abrasive cloths are used when handling or cleaning the bath.
  • Nails or screws can be driven without damaging the tub.

Our tub comes with a complete installation kit, including the linear overflow drain assembly and the connecting toe-kick for the drain. We also include an adapter and 2 pieces of PVC in the size needed to complete the installation. Most tubs come with nothing but the two holes for the overflow and the drain.

Tools and materials required include:

  • Level
  • Square
  • Tape measure
  • Pipe wrench
  • Slip joint pliers
  • Screwdriver
  • Standard woodworking tools
  • Personal safety equipment
  • Caulking gun
  • Nails
  • Plumber’s putty
  • Silicone sealant
  • Drop cloth
  • Wet mortar bed or foam bedding, when needed

Installation Steps

Before starting, remember to cut out the top of the box, which is designed to protect the bottom of the tub while the bathroom remodelling is in progress. It protects against things like dropping tools, tiles, and grout while the tiling and other framing work is completed. (usually after the tub has been installed)

Next, remove the small box attached inside the box that contains the full assembly package included with the Perfectub. The Perfectub comes with a complete installation package including the overflow drain assembly, toe-kick drain, and precisely sized PCV pieces.

1. Installation and Framing Instructions

  • Locate the studs as required. Ensure that roughing-in dimensions are proper, plumb, and square. We strongly recommend providing an additional opening to access the drain components.
  • Install components in the bath following the manufacturer’s instructions. See the roughing-in drawing for suggested opening size and location dimensions.

2. Subfloor and Support Requirements

The tub is self-supporting, but it must still sit on the right surface. The support needs to come from below the tub, not from the rim or the wall system.

The subfloor must be:

  • Smooth
  • Level
  • Supported in both directions
  • Supported across the back, middle, and front of the tub area.

The use of a wet mortar bed or foam bedding is not required, but highly recommended when the floor is uneven or older, in the case of most remodeling jobs.

Before setting the tub:

  • Check the floor from front to back.
  • Check the floor from side to side.
  • Shim the full floor area under the tub if needed.
  • Do not allow the bath rim to support the tub’s weight.

This is one of the most important installation points. A tub that is not supported and correctly levelled can create problems with wall alignment and drainage.

Securing the Tub to the Studs

After the tub is positioned and levelled, it can be secured to the framing. The correct method depends on whether the installation uses wood or steel studs.

For wood stud construction:

  • Use drywall screws with flat washers or roofing nails.
  • Place fasteners immediately above the flag flange.
  • Avoid driving fasteners into the tub itself.

For steel stud construction:

  • Use drywall screws with flat washers.
  • Secure the tub according to the flange mounting detail.
  • Take extra care to avoid damaging the bath during fastening.

Fasteners should hold the tub in place without placing stress on the acrylic surface.

Wall, Tile, and Flooring Notes

The surrounding finish materials should be installed in a way that does not overload the tub deck.

Important finish material notes:

  • The tile and wallboard must be self-supporting if they contact the bath deck.
  • Wall materials should not rely on the tub deck for support.
  • Flooring should be brought against the bottom of the tub apron.
  • The flooring helps hold the tub in place once the installation is complete.

This helps create a cleaner finish around the tub while keeping the bath properly supported.

3. Drain Connection

The drain should be connected only after the recommended opening and waste line location have been confirmed.

Drain connection steps include:

  • Use the recommended drain opening.
  • Connect the bath drain to the waste line.
  • Tighten all joints securely.
  • Check the connection before closing the installation area.

A secure drain connection helps prevent leaks and avoids unnecessary strain on the waste line.

Warranty Coverage

Brak Tub provides a limited lifetime warranty to the original residential purchaser when the tub is used and maintained under normal residential conditions.

Residential Warranty Coverage

  • Applies to the original consumer purchaser.
  • Applies under normal residential use and maintenance.
  • Allows Brak Tub to repair or replace the tub if a covered defect is found upon delivery.
  • Remains tied to the original purchaser.

Commercial Warranty Coverage

  • Commercial installations are covered for one year.
  • The limited lifetime warranty does not apply to commercial installations.

What the Warranty Does Not Cover

The warranty has important exclusions, especially for installation-related problems and surrounding materials.

The warranty does not cover:

  • Improper installation
  • Alterations
  • Accidents
  • Misuse
  • Lack of proper maintenance or cleaning
  • Normal wear and tear
  • Non-genuine Brak Tub replacement parts
  • Previously repaired or replaced tubs and accessories
  • Commercial use under the residential lifetime warranty
  • Shipping charges for replacement products
  • Labor costs
  • Product disposal or reinstallation costs
  • Tile, marble, or other surrounding installation materials
  • Local code compliance responsibility
  • Consequential or incidental damages, where excluded by law

Accessories from Brak Tub, such as aprons, drains, grab bags, heaters, and trim kits, are covered for parts only for one year from the date of purchase by the original owner.

Requesting Warranty Service

Warranty service should usually begin with the contractor or dealer from whom the product was purchased.

A warranty request should include:

  • A product model
  • Description of the defect
  • Purchase or dealer information, when available
  • Original owner information
  • Any relevant installation details
