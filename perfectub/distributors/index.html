Bathtub Distributors

If you’re a contractor, showroom rep, or someone in the remodeling trade, you already know that most standard tubs leave a lot to be desired. Perfectub fixes that. It’s easier to install, better to use, and solves so many problems you run into with standard alcove bathtubs. We’re Brak Tub, the bathtub manufacturer behind Perfectub, and we’re actively expanding our network of bathtub distributors.

We want to work with bathtub showrooms and distributors who care about quality and want to bring a unique bathtub option to customers looking for better solutions.

How is Perfectub Different?

This isn’t your average acrylic tub. Every part of Perfectub was rethought, starting with the shape. The inner tub walls have an undercut. They curve slightly outward, which gives users more space without taking up extra room in the bathroom.

This keeps splashing water in the tub and off the floor, making bathing kids or pets a less soggy experience.

The flat bottom makes it great for shower chairs. This design choice also allows the tub to hold more water for people who want a real soak. These are all great options that bathtub distributors can present as unique benefits to customers.

Furthermore, we also hold multiple patents on this design, including one for the slim back edge, which fits cleanly under the tile. It just looks better and works better.

Here’s a detail pros really appreciate: the solid base. That usually means less prep and less messing around with setting beds, and a wet mortar bed or foam bedding is recommended on uneven or older floors. That’s less work for you or your installer, which means less room for error.

Is Perfectub Right for Your Customers?

Perfectub was designed for the people who actually use the tub, not for bathtub showrooms or just the ones who install it.

Bathtub distributors will find that its features solve everyday problems and are powerful selling points.

  • Older homeowners appreciate the narrower side walls that make stepping in and out easier.
  • Families love the wider floor because it gives them more space when bathing kids and pets.
  • People with larger body frames can soak comfortably with both elbows submerged because of the tub’s extra volume.
  • If you’re a remodelling contractor, this is a tub that adds real value and sets your work apart.
  • Plumbing wholesalers and bathtub distributors benefit from offering a high-quality, patent-backed product that’s unlike anything else on the market.
  • For developers and architects, it’s a standout upgrade that still fits within standard alcove dimensions with no custom framing required.
