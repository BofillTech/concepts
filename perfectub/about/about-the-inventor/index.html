About the Inventor

The Ahmes’ put us on the moon in 1969, and will take you over the moon in 2022, and beyond with the Perfectub from the BRAK Tub Company.

My Dad Albert Ahmes, an inventor, was Grumman’s head structural Engineer for the Apollo 11 mission (L.E.M.) that put the first man on the moon. My Dad designed the landing gear, the fuel line and the window. When Neil Armstrong climbed down that ladder to step on the moon, he was on my father’s ladder.

My patented Perfectub takes people over the moon when they step into a bath like no other. Its space-age design is light years ahead of the first bathtub invented, and until Perfectub’s launch, unchanged for 120 years. Lucky owners of the Perfectub bathe in luxury with about 30% more floor space all packed in a standard 30″ x 60″ footprint. The Perfectub by BRAK Tub design is 100% easier to enter and exit with its thin side walls and toe-kick which doubles as an in-depth splash guard.

I am proud to be a curious inventor like my Dad. He got as much of a thrill taking us to the moon as I do, providing an out-of-this-world bathing experience with my Perfectub.
