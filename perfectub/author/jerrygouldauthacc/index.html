About Jerry Gould

Jerry Gould is V.P. of Marketing and Sales of Brak Tub and President of Gould Marketing Services. He has been a respected plumbing industry professional for over 40 years. Under his professional name, Jerome N. Gould, Jerry has written articles published in several nationally distributed magazines, including House Beautiful, New England Monthly, Contractor, and Computer Sources.

After learning about the Perfectub, he joined Brak Tub in 2024 to help build the brand and sales network of this important, life-enhancing, and innovative product.
