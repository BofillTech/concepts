The Perfect Bathtub and Shower Combo

When you’re remodeling a bathroom, one of the first questions is whether to dedicate the space to a shower or to a tub. With Perfectub, you don’t have to choose. At Brak Tub, we designed these bathtubs and shower combos to work like a modern shower pan but still give you the comfort of a soak.

In comparisons between the walk-in shower vs the tub shower, that balance is what makes it practical, especially for families and builders who want flexibility without compromise.

Why This Combo Stands Out

After installing a bathtub shower combo like Perfectub, you are probably going to be using that shower more than you’ll use the bathtub. But of course, if you want to take a bath, then you’ll still have that bathtub there. And that’s where Brak Tub’s bathtubs and shower combos really optimize for both use cases.

First of all, the Perfectub has the widest footprint you’ll find because of its patented back edge and the absence of an extra lip on the wall side of the tub; both combine to give it over 25 inches [VERIFY] of foot space. This is a lot, even in bathtub showers for small bathrooms.

With such a footprint, you can also use a shower chair, which can even be great for aging people or those who need a shower chair when bathing.

Easier for Installers

It’s not just about the ease and safety you get with Brak Tub’s bathtubs and shower combos because installing these tub showers normally takes so many materials, and this can get frustrating for installers.

Perfectub simplifies the job with a solid built-in bottom that removes the need for extra structural support. It saves time and money for the contractors, which gets passed down to the users as savings on getting a more efficient and safer bathtub.

Wholesalers understand the value of this kind of innovation. When it comes to walk-in shower vs tub shower, builders want tubs that install quickly, and distributors can point to the features of Brak Tub’s bathtubs and shower combos as clear selling points. Installing a bathtub shower combo like Perfectub benefits everyone involved!
