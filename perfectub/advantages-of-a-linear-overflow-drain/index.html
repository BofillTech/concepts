Advantages of a Linear Overflow Drain

By Jerry GouldJune 11, 2026

Key Takeaways

  • A linear overflow drain is a horizontal drain commonly found in deep-soak tubs like the Perfectub.
  • It allows for a higher, yet safe, water level compared to traditional round designs, making bathing a more relaxing, immersive experience, even for people with mobility issues.
  • The linear design improves water flow efficiency, reduces noise, and makes it easier to clean and maintain due to its smooth, elongated modern design.

When designing or upgrading a bathroom, even the smallest details can elevate performance and visual appeal. Today’s modern spaces are increasingly defined by sleek, simple additions that combine beauty and practicality, such as linear overflow drains.

Unlike traditional round tub overflow drains, this contemporary design allows for greater water depth and safety, and offers a more modern aesthetic. A closer look into the benefits of a linear vs round drain can help guide homeowners as they make informed purchasing decisions. Let’s get after it.

How Does a Bathtub Overflow Drain Work?

A bathtub overflow drain is a drain feature that prevents water from spilling over the edge of the tub. When water rises to the overflow opening, it flows into the drain channel and travels through the internal plumbing to the main drainpipe.

The Advantages of a Linear Overflow Drain

Here are the most significant perks

1. Has a modern, minimalist design

A linear drain has a sleek, streamlined appearance that complements contemporary bathroom styles, blending seamlessly with clean lines and modern finishes. In contrast, a round overflow drain can appear bulky and outdated.

2. Improved water flow efficiency

Due to its elongated shape, a linear drain can channel water efficiently because the wider opening allows it to flow smoothly.

3. It delivers a deeper soaking experience

Unlike the traditional round overflow drains, which often sit slightly lower and limit the height of the water level to the lowest point on the round overflow, linear overflow drains are optimally placed and allow a higher water level before excess water starts to drain.

4. Enhanced safety and accessibility

Since tub overflow drains are designed to prevent flooding, choosing a more effective system provides peace of mind. Hence, a horizontal overflow drain is more efficient because it maintains constant overflow protection while allowing a fuller bath. Even for individuals with mobility needs, this water level is both comfortable and secure.

5. Easier to Clean and Maintain

Standard round overflow drains often have exposed screws and tiny crevices where dirt and grime can accumulate. However, a linear overflow drain has a smooth surface and fewer hard-to-reach areas that make cleaning simpler.

FAQs

Do horizontal overflow drains require special maintenance?No, maintenance is generally simple. Clean linear drains occasionally to prevent buildup. It’s easier to wipe clean compared to round models.

Do linear overflow drains improve bathroom safety?Absolutely. By preventing accidental overfilling and ensuring safer access, the drain enhances bathroom safety, especially in tubs with higher water capacity, such as the Perfectub. Visit a showroom to see the tub for yourself before your purchase.

Do linear tub overflow drains affect water pressure in the main drain?No. The overflow simply redirects excess water safely. It does not impact the main drain’s pressure or normal tub drainage.
