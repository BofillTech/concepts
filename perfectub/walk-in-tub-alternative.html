How it works

The undercut does what the door does.

In a typical tub, the sloped front wall pushes your inside foot away from the edge, so you straddle the side and lean out to climb over. Perfectub's front wall angles in under the rim. Your inside foot steps forward until it's right against the apron, and you step over with your feet only about 3 inches apart, leaning about 5.5 degrees instead of about 17.

Questions people ask

Is Perfectub a walk-in tub?

No. It has no door. It's a standard-size alcove tub with a patented undercut in the front wall, so stepping in and out takes far less effort than with a typical tub.

Can I use a shower chair in it?

Yes. The flat floor keeps a standard shower chair level and steady.

Will it fit where my old tub is?

Yes, if your alcove is the standard 60 inches long. Choose the 30 or 32 inch depth, front to back, to match your space.

Is it ADA compliant?
