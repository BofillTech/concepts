From order to installation

How the purchase works, step by step

  1. Measure your alcove

    Confirm the alcove is the standard 60 inches long. Measure front to back to choose 30 or 32 inches.

    Then pick the drain side. Both sizes come in left-hand (LH) or right-hand (RH): it's the side of the tub the drain is on, chosen by where the plumbing is in your bathroom. If you're not sure which you need, call (516) 217-4599 before ordering.

  2. Place your order

    Online at braktub.com or through a showroom near you. Shipping is already in the price, and sales tax is added at checkout.

  3. Order confirmation and delivery scheduling

    You receive an order confirmation, and Brak Tub arranges freight delivery to your address.

    About 1 to 3 business days to ship Estimate

  4. Freight delivery on a pallet

    The tub arrives boxed on a pallet. Have someone there to receive it and room to store it until installation.

    • Inspect the tub before you sign for it and again before installation. The warranty requires inspection before the tub is installed.
    • Keep the carton. The top protects the tub during the remodel, and returns need the original packaging.
    See the delivery estimates below Estimate

  5. Installation

    A plumber or contractor installs it like a standard alcove tub, using the included kit.

    • Check that the framing is plumb and square, and plan an access opening for the drain if you can.
    • The tub is self-supporting and must be installed on a smooth level sub-floor. The floor should be supported and level in BOTH DIRECTIONS and on each board (back, middle and front). The use of a wet mortar bed or foam bedding is not required, but highly recommended when the floor is uneven or older, in the case of most remodeling jobs.
    • Secure the tub to the studs just above the flange, without driving fasteners into the tub.
    • Connect the drain, tighten every joint, and check for leaks before closing up.
    • Wall materials must support themselves, and the flooring butts against the bottom of the apron.

    Read the full installation guide

  6. Enjoy it, and keep your records

    Keep your receipt and the model number. The limited lifetime warranty stays with the original owner.

Warranty and returns

Covered for as long as you own it

Limited lifetime warranty

For the original owner, in a home, under normal use and care. Brak Tub repairs or replaces a tub with a covered defect.

  • Accessories: parts only, one year

What isn't covered

  • Improper installation, alterations, accidents or misuse
  • Labor, removal, reinstallation and disposal
  • Tile, marble and other surrounding materials
  • Shipping for replacement products
  • Damage from non-acrylic-safe cleaners

30-day returns

Return an unused tub within 30 days of receiving it, in its original packaging, with proof of purchase.

  • The refund goes back to the original payment method after inspection
  • Return shipping is paid by the buyer and isn't refundable
