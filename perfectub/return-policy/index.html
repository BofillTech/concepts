Return | Repair | Replacement — Consumer Policy

At Brak Tub Corp., we take great care to ensure that every Perfectub is properly packaged and shipped in good condition. Because bathtubs are heavy large items transported by freight carriers, it is extremely important that every shipment is inspected at the time of delivery.

Inspection at Time of Delivery

  • DO NOT sign for the tub until it has been fully inspected, especially if the box is damaged.
  • You have the option to decline a delivery before the driver leaves the drop-off location and the driver will take the tub back at no cost to you.
  • Verify that you received the full installation kit provided with every Perfectub. The installation kit is in a separate box inside the same box as the tub and should contain six(6) pieces.

Box is Damaged

  • If the tub arrives and the box is damaged, please take photos and instruct the driver to take photos, of the damaged box – as many sides or views needed to capture the full extent of the damage to the box.
  • Report the box damage on the Delivery Acceptance or Proof of Delivery (POD) receipt, even if you decide to accept the delivery.
  • All Perfectubs are delivered on a palette when they leave our warehouse. If the tub arrived and was not delivered on a palette, that needs to be noted on the ‘Delivery Acceptance’ or Proof of Delivery (POD) receipt.

Only Accept Delivery if the Tub is Acceptable

  • Remove the tub completely out of the box to examine it. Check for damage on the outside, underside and inside of the tub.
  • If there is no damage to the tub or the box, you can accept delivery and sign the Delivery Acceptance or Proof of Delivery (POD) receipt.

Choosing Not to Accept Delivery

  • The driver must note every reason that the tub was not acceptable on the Delivery Acceptance or Proof of Delivery (POD) receipt.
  • Please take photos of the damage. The driver is responsible for taking photos and reporting the reasons the tub was not accepted, back to the freight company.
  • The driver will take the tub back and it will be disposed of, at no cost to you.
  • If you purchased the tub through a showroom or distributor, contact the representative who sold you the tub, within 24 hours of declining a delivery for damage.
  • If you purchased the tub directly from Brak Tub, please email Sales@braktub.com within 24 hours after declining the delivery.

Concealed or Minor Damage

We understand that some shipping damage may not be visible until the outer wrapping on the bathtub is completely removed.

  • If concealed damage is discovered after delivery, stop installation and contact Brak Tub Corp. or your sale representative immediately.
  • Report the damage within 24 hours of discovery.
  • If the damage is minor or is in a place that will not be visible after the installation is complete, Brak Tub Co. will arrange for a repair.

Acrylic repairs extremely well. Repairing minor damage can be done quickly and cost effectively. Brak Tub will pay for and schedule the repair at a location and time determined by you.

To Request a Repair or Report Hidden Damage

  • If you purchased the tub through a showroom or distributor, contact the representative who sold you the tub.
  • If you purchased the tub directly from Brak Tub, please email Sales@braktub.com

Why the Proof of Delivery Is Important

The Proof of Delivery is an important part of any freight damage claim. Signing a delivery receipt indicating that the bathtub was received in good order may be considered confirmation that the shipment arrived without visible damage.

If a damaged bathtub is accepted and the delivery documentation states that it was received in good condition, Brak Tub Corp. may not be able to obtain a freight claim or provide a replacement bathtub at no charge.

For this reason, please do not allow a delivery driver to rush you through the inspection process.
