One of New Jersey's most romantic hotels

A family-run inn with a following that comes back every summer.

TripsToDiscover named the Periwinkle Inn one of the seven most romantic hotels in all of New Jersey, and Kayak has listed it among the best hotels in Cape May. Guests return year after year for the beachfront location, the lush garden grounds, and a staff that remembers their names. The inn has also picked up back-to-back AAA Best Housekeeping honors — the kind of award that only comes from guests who kept coming back.

Cape May itself is America's first seashore resort, a National Historic Landmark town of gingerbread Victorians a short walk from the inn's front steps — restaurants, the promenade, and the Convention Center all within reach on foot.

The courtyard is where the vacation actually happens.

A 5-star-rated outdoor heated pool and a separate baby pool anchor the garden, ringed by chaise lounges, a shuffleboard court, and BBQ grills guests are free to use any time. Kids gravitate to the life-sized dollhouse and sandbox while the trolley waits out front for a free ride into town each evening in season.

  • Outdoor heated pool + baby pool
  • Shuffleboard court & BBQ grills
  • Life-sized dollhouse & sandbox
  • Complimentary trolley service (in season)
  • Free Wi-Fi, free local calls & voicemail
  • On-site parking, one vehicle per unit
