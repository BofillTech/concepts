The Inn & the Farm

The farm came first. The inn came from the heart.

Louie Escobar never wanted to be anything but a dairy farmer. His father started the farm on Middle Road, Louie took it over in 1972, and — despite a tractor accident that left him a quadriplegic — he kept dairying until his passing in 2022. For Jane, a city girl, life on the farm was the fulfillment of a childhood dream. The two married in 1986 and spent their years together keeping the farm thriving.

In June of 2010, Jane opened Escobar's Farmhouse Inn across the street from the farm — a comfortable "home away from home" where guests aren't just welcome to look at farm life, they're invited into it.

On the Farm

The farm has its own calendar.

July 5 · 9:15 PM The Fireworks

Portsmouth celebrates Independence Day with the annual fireworks display put on by the Escobar family at their Middle Road farm (rain date July 6). The back of the inn's property is the perfect vantage point.

Sept – Nov The Corn Maze

Get lost in the corn maze, then take a picturesque hay-ride around the dairy farm. Call (401) 683-1444 or visit escobarfarm.com for maze information.

Sept – Nov The Pumpkin Patch

Pick out the best pumpkins for jack-o'-lanterns to decorate at home — priced $2 to $20 by size, with gourds for sale too.
