The best of both worlds

Beach on one side. Downtown on the other.

Spend the day on our own stretch of sand on West Grand Traverse Bay, then walk into town for dinner, shopping and Front Street’s galleries and bars — or let the complimentary shuttle take you there and bring you back.

Many of our 120 guest rooms open onto a private balcony or patio with the bay in front of them, and the suites add a fireplace and a two-person in-room spa. It is, in every sense, a resort you can walk out of in either direction.

The beach

The bay is the front yard.

Bayshore Resort sits directly on West Grand Traverse Bay, and the beach in front of it is ours. In the warmer months it is the whole point of the place — and in every other season the view out of it is reason enough.

  • Beach chairs and towels provided for guests through the summer season.
  • Aussie Watersports operates on the beach from Memorial Day to Labor Day, with jet-ski and boat rentals.
  • Bayfront rooms and suites look straight out over the sand and the water.

The location

Between two peninsulas.

Grand Traverse Bay splits in two around Old Mission Peninsula, with Leelanau on the far side of the west arm. Bayshore sits at the foot of it all — which makes it an unusually short drive to almost everything people come north for.

  • Old MissionVineyards and orchards straight up the middle of the bay.
  • LeelanauWineries, villages and shoreline on the west arm.
  • DowntownFront Street dining, shopping and nightlife, on foot or by shuttle.
