Natural Mineral Hot Springs

Come take a break to soak and relax in our rejuvenating, all-natural mineral hot springs. Open daily 7 AM–11 PM (6 AM for hotel guests).

Temperature-Controlled Pools

Our pools range from 94°F to 107°F. We also have a 44°F cold-plunge pool for contrast therapy.

Multiple Soaking Areas

Several pools including the Aztec pool out front and river pools out back. Scenic views of the Portneuf River.

Comfortable Amenities

Poolside seating with umbrellas, tables for refreshments, and a family-friendly atmosphere.

Hotel Guests

Complimentary access included with your stay. Wristbands provided at check-in. Pools open 6 AM for early morning soaks.

Public Day Passes

$15.00 per pass (adults, children, seniors)
$2.00 for infants under 2
Purchase at front desk. Come and go all day with wristband.

Pool Hours

Public: 7:00 AM – 11:00 PM daily
Hotel Guests: 6:00 AM – 11:00 PM daily
Open year-round!

Pool Guidelines

  • All soakers must wear provided wristbands in the water
  • No glass containers. Lidded plastic/metal water bottles only beside the water
  • No food or open drinks directly in or beside pools
  • Children must be supervised at all times
  • Pets must be leashed and are not allowed in water
  • No alcohol, smoking, or vaping on property
  • Respectful behavior required—no excessive noise, profanity, or harassment

Explore Lava Hot Springs

Natural Hot Springs

For thousands of years, travelers have come to Lava Hot Springs to experience the natural healing mineral waters. Our hot springs are fed by underground geothermal sources—100% natural and therapeutic.

Portneuf River

Our historic inn overlooks the beautiful Portneuf River. Enjoy river tubing in summer months, scenic views year-round, and the soothing sound of flowing water.

Olympic Swimming Complex

World-famous Olympic-size pool and waterslides just down the road. Perfect for families looking for more aquatic fun beyond the hot springs.

Downtown Lava

Small-town charm with shops, restaurants, ice cream parlors, and local art. Walk to everything from the inn—true small-town Idaho hospitality.

Outdoor Recreation

Hiking, fishing, ATVing, and wildlife viewing in the surrounding mountains and valleys. Southeast Idaho offers year-round outdoor adventures.

Pocatello & Idaho Falls

30-40 minutes to larger cities with shopping, dining, and Pocatello Regional Airport. Easy access while staying in peaceful Lava Hot Springs.
