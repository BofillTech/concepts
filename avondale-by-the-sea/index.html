Good to know before you come

Three floors, no elevator
Ground-floor rooms are easiest with luggage or little ones. Ask when you call.
Check-out by 11 a.m.
The beach is still across the street if you want one last swim.
Rates are for two
Additional guests of any age are $20–$30 each per night. We never exceed a room’s maximum occupancy.
Minimum stays
Three nights or more at peak times and on holiday weekends.
Non-smoking, no pets
All guest rooms, the lobby and the pool are strictly non-smoking. Sorry, no pets.
Deposits
One non-refundable night for stays of five nights or less; two nights for six or more.

Off-season midweek packages

April, May, October and November, for consecutive weeknights: stay five nights and the fifth is free; four nights and the fourth is half off; three nights and the third is 30% off.
