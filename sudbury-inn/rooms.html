Good to know

Breakfast
A cooked-to-order country breakfast is included when offered. Midweek breakfast is limited, so ask when you book.
Extra guests
Additional guests are welcome in the same room for a small nightly charge.
Carriage house
Rooms 30, 40, 50 and 60 are in our carriage house. Every other room is in the main inn.
Pets
We're no longer able to host pets. Sorry for any inconvenience.
Taxes
Maine lodging tax applies to all room bookings.
Open all year
Ski season, foliage season and everything in between.
