Sleep upstairs. Pub downstairs. Shuttle at the door.

An 1873 country inn in the heart of Bethel's Main Street. Rooms, family suites and a cooked-to-order breakfast, with Suds Pub one flight down and the free ski shuttle to Sunday River stopping out front.

Everything you came for, under one roof.

The Sudbury is the only bed and breakfast in Bethel with lodging and a lively pub in the same building. Our staff are mostly Mainers and long-time Bethel locals, so ask us where to hike, ski or eat. We know.

Main floor Breakfast & the porch

Mornings start slow here.

Breakfast is cooked to order: Maine blueberry pancakes, omelets your way, and more. It's served in the dining room. Then take your coffee out to a rocker on the front porch, or settle in by the lobby fireplace.

  • Country breakfastIncluded with your stay when offered. Midweek breakfast is limited, so ask when you book.
  • Front porch rockersA front-row seat on historic Main Street.

Downstairs Suds Pub

Dinner is one flight down.

Suds Pub is Bethel's spot for pizza, a big menu of pub grub and the largest selection of New England beers in Western Maine. Thursday is Hoot Night, an open mic that's said to be Maine's longest-running jam night.

Open Thursday through Monday, 4 to 9:30 pm. Pub line 207-824-6558. More about Suds.

Leave the car. Ski Sunday River.

During ski season the Mountain Explorer, a free shuttle, picks up right at our front door every half hour and runs to the South Ridge Base Lodge at Sunday River. No parking, no driving, no worries.
