Welcome!

The Sudbury Inn is a 1873 Bethel Maine Bed and Breakfast country inn located in the heart of the beautiful mountain village of Bethel, Maine. Popular with generations of skiers, hikers and tourists, we are the only B&B in town that offers lodging, breakfast and a lively restaurant and pub under one roof. The inn welcomes families, offering 6 two bedroom suites and a three bedroom apartment. The Sudbury Inn boasts excellent staff, many of whom are "Mainers" and long time residents of Bethel, and are experts on what to do and see in the area.

Main floor Breakfast & the porch

Mornings start slow here.

Breakfast is cooked to order: Maine blueberry pancakes, omelets your way, and more. It's served in the dining room. Then take your coffee out to a rocker on the front porch, or settle in by the lobby fireplace.

Downstairs Suds Pub

Dinner is one flight down.

Suds Pub is Bethel's spot for pizza, a big menu of pub grub and the largest selection of New England beers in Western Maine. Thursday is Hoot Night, an open mic that's said to be Maine's longest-running jam night.
