Gather at the Sudbury

Private events & groups

Our dining room and bistro are available for private parties and special events, with rooms and suites upstairs for everyone who comes to celebrate.

Plan an event Or call 207-824-2174

What people host here

  • Rehearsal dinners

    A warm, historic room on Main Street, with the wedding party sleeping upstairs.

  • Family reunions

    Book the apartment and a block of suites, and keep everyone under one roof.

  • Birthdays & anniversaries

    Intimate dinners and larger parties, planned with our team.

  • School & team weekends

    Gould Academy is within walking distance, and Sunday River is a short shuttle ride away.

  • Business retreats

    Meet in the dining room, then unwind by the lobby fireplace or downstairs at Suds.

  • Holiday gatherings

    Ask about special menus and theme evenings for your group.

How to plan it

  1. Tell us the basics. Your date, the size of your group and what you're celebrating.
  2. We'll suggest a setup. Dining room, bistro or both, plus a block of rooms for your guests.
  3. Settle the details. Menu, timing and rooms, all with one team in one building.
