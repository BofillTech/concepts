Every season, something to do

Winter

Downhill at Sunday River and Mount Abram, with the free Mountain Explorer shuttle stopping at our front door every half hour in ski season. Nordic skiers have nearby tracks at Carter's Cross Country Ski Center and Sunday River.

Summer

Day hikes in the Mahoosucs and Grafton Notch, canoeing and fishing on the Androscoggin, 18 holes up the street and bike rides that start at the porch.

Fall

Foliage drives through the notches, moose watching on scenic back roads, and a cold one at Suds when you get back.

Any day

Main Street shops, the movies, the library and a rocker on the front porch. We can also connect you with local outfitters for canoeing, dog sledding, fishing and snowmobiling.

Need a tip?

Maps are available at the front desk, and we're happy to point you to a trail, a river put-in or a quiet drive.
