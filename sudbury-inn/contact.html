Frequently asked questions

Where is the Sudbury Inn?

At 151 Main Street in Bethel Village, Maine, about 6 miles from Sunday River, within walking distance of shops, restaurants and Gould Academy.

How do I get to Sunday River without driving?

In ski season the free Mountain Explorer shuttle stops at the inn's front door every half hour and runs to the South Ridge Base Lodge at Sunday River.

Is there a restaurant or bar at the inn?

Yes. Suds Pub is downstairs, serving pizza, pub food and 29 beers on tap, Thursday through Monday from 4 to 9:30 pm. Take-out is available.

Is breakfast included?

A cooked-to-order country breakfast is included when offered. Breakfast is limited midweek, so ask when you book.

What are check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is at 2 pm and check-out is at 11 am.

Are pets allowed?

No. The inn is no longer able to host pets.

Do you have rooms for families and groups?

Yes. There are seven two-bedroom suites and a three-bedroom apartment with a stone hearth, full kitchen and private deck.

What do the rooms include?

Every room has a private bath, air conditioning and cable TV. Deluxe suites add a jetted tub.

Is parking available?

Yes, there is free parking on site.

Is the inn open all year?

Yes. The Sudbury Inn is open year-round, through ski season, summer and fall foliage.

Can I host a private event at the inn?

Yes. The dining room and bistro are available for private parties and special events. Call 207-824-2174 or email info@thesudburyinn.com.

Find us

The inn sits on Main Street in Bethel Village, within walking distance of shops, restaurants, the movies and Gould Academy. Sunday River is about 10 minutes away, and in ski season the free Mountain Explorer shuttle stops right out front.
