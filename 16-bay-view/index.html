An industrial landmark, reimagined as authentic luxury

In the heart of downtown Camden, on Bay View Street, a 100-year-old industrial brick building has been transformed into a one-of-a-kind boutique hotel. Amid a thriving shopping district full of galleries, fine restaurants, and boutiques — and just footsteps from Camden Harbor — 16 Bay View delivers an authentic Midcoast Maine experience.

The rooftop

The View — rooftop bar & terrace

When the sun's out or the stars are shining, locals and visitors alike head to The View — the rooftop bar and restaurant atop 16 Bay View, with over 2,000 square feet of panoramic views of Camden Harbor and the surrounding hills.

Craft cocktails and locally inspired bites in a stylish open-air setting, open to hotel guests and the public alike — dogs included.

Open seasonally, early June to mid-October · Daily 4–9pm, weather permitting
