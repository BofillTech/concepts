Peg’s dream, realized

A Key Largo schoolteacher, two little motels, and one torn-down fence.

Peg Mullin Laron spent years teaching in Key Largo and dreaming of the small family-run pensions she had stayed in across Europe. In 1994 she bought Coconut Bay, a five-unit bayfront motel. In 1997 she added Bay Harbor next door, with a “tear-down-the-separating-fence” celebration.

Today it’s one two-and-a-half-acre waterfront garden, with mango, papaya and key lime trees, a morning bulletin board guests love, and more than 25 flavours of scones: guava, key lime, chocolate crunch, apricot almond.

“Arrive as a guest and you’ll return as a friend.”
