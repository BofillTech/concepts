Premier Texas Gulf Coast Resort

Since 1959, Isla Grand Beach Resort has been South Padre Island's premier beachfront destination. Located directly on the beach in the southern portion of the island, our 10-acre tropical oasis offers 193 accommodations including beachfront cabanas and high-rise Gulf-view condominiums.

From our two sparkling pools and hot tubs to beach volleyball, tennis and pickleball courts, every amenity is designed for your perfect beach vacation. Dine at Windjammer's Beachfront Café overlooking the Gulf, sip cocktails at the Whiskey Shack poolside bar, or dance the night away in the Quarterdeck Lounge featuring live entertainment.

Whether it's a family vacation, romantic getaway, or business conference, experience where white sand meets blue waves at Isla Grand Beach Resort.

Dining & Entertainment

Windjammer's Beachfront Café

Enjoy breakfast, lunch, and dinner with stunning views of the pools, waterfall, and Gulf of Mexico. Our signature restaurant serves fresh coastal cuisine in a relaxed beachfront setting.

Whiskey Shack Poolside Bar

Sip frozen cocktails, ice-cold beers, and refreshing drinks at our palapa bar next to the pool. Open daily from noon until dusk, weather permitting.

Quarterdeck Lounge

Dance to the best bands from across the country, enjoy live comedy shows, crab races, and karaoke nights. The island's premier entertainment destination.
