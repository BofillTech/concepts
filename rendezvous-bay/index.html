Our Story
1958

The island's first taste of the sea

Rendezvous Bay Hotel has welcomed guests to the shores of Anguilla since 1958 — the island's very first beachfront resort. Family-owned to this day, we pair authentic island charm with genuine Anguillian spirit.

Wake to panoramic ocean views, gentle sea breezes, and the sound of waves just steps from your room. Whether it's a romantic escape, a family holiday, or a quiet stretch to recharge, this is a true home away from home.

65+ years of Anguillian hospitality on Rendezvous Bay
The Bay

Two and a half miles of powder-white shore

Rendezvous Bay is famous for its ultra-calm, warm water and soft white sand. Settle into a private chaise lounge and umbrella — included with every room — and let the day unfold at its own pace.

Dining

Barefoot on the sand at Tastys

Rendezvous Tastys Beach Club is a hidden gem in the heart of the bay — open-air, toes-in-the-sand, and one of the best beachfront restaurants in Anguilla. Open for breakfast and lunch, with a menu of fresh seafood, local flavors, and, of course, lobster.

When the sun drops, drift over to The Place, our beachside bar, for a frozen cocktail and a Rendezvous Bay sunset you'll be chasing long after you leave.
