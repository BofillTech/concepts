1962 Our Story

The island's first taste of the sea

Set on forty quiet acres between a bird-filled salt pond and the Caribbean Sea, Rendezvous Bay Hotel has welcomed travelers since 1962 — Anguilla's very first beachfront resort. Still family-owned, still gathered along the island's calmest, whitest bay.

Thirteen beachfront villas sit right on the sand. Open your double doors to a postcard view of Rendezvous Bay and the peaks of St. Martin beyond — a home away from home that hasn't lost an ounce of its Anguillian spirit.

Dining

Barefoot on the sand at the Coral Club

The Coral Club is a hidden gem in the heart of the bay — open-air, toes-in-the-sand, and one of the best beachfront tables in Anguilla. Open for breakfast and lunch, with fresh seafood and lobster.

When the sun drops, drift over to The Place, our beachside bar, for a frozen cocktail and a Rendezvous Bay sunset you'll be chasing long after you leave.
