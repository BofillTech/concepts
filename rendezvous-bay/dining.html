Open-Air Dining

Barefoot on the sand at the Coral Club

The Coral Club is a hidden gem in the heart of the bay — open-air, toes-in-the-sand, in a magical island setting. Open for breakfast and lunch, the kitchen leans on what the sea gives up that morning: fresh fish, crayfish and lobster.

Come evening, The Place — the hotel's own beachside bar — pours frozen cocktails against a Rendezvous Bay sunset you'll be chasing long after you leave.
