A Short Walk Away

The Coral Club, just along the sand

A short walk down the beach from the hotel, the Coral Club is an open-air, toes-in-the-sand restaurant on Rendezvous Bay. Open for breakfast and lunch, the kitchen leans on what the sea gives up that morning — fresh fish, crayfish and lobster — and come evening the beachside bar pours frozen cocktails against the sunset.

The Coral Club is independently owned and operated. Menu, hours and reservations are handled directly with the restaurant.
