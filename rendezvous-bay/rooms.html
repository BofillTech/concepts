Our Rooms

Every room steps from the sea

Seventeen beachfront villas line the sand — some at ground level, some a flight up. Choose a room with a full kitchen or without; either way, the bay is right outside your double doors. Rooms are furnished in warm, dark wood with ceiling fans, air conditioning, a shower and a separate vanity, and a covered porch made for lingering.

All Beachfront

Beachfront Room

Walk off your covered porch and directly onto the sand of Rendezvous Bay. Double doors open to a postcard view of the water so you never lose the scene, even from inside. A comfortable retreat with a shower and separate vanity.
