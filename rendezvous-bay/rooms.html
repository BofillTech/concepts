Our Rooms

Every room is just steps from the sea

Seventeen beachfront villa rooms line the sand — some at ground level, some a flight up. Choose a room with a full kitchen or without; either way, the bay is right outside your double doors. Rooms are furnished in warm, dark wood with ceiling fans, air conditioning, a shower and a separate vanity, and a covered porch made for lingering.

Ground Level

Downstairs Villa — No Kitchen

A ground-floor villa that opens straight onto the sand of Rendezvous Bay. One bedroom with a comfortable sitting area and a private porch, with the sea just a few steps from your door. No kitchen here — simply settle in and let the beach set the pace.
