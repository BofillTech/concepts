Getting Here

Finding your way to the bay

By air

Fly into Clayton J. Lloyd International Airport (AXA) — just about 4.3 miles from the hotel.

By sea

Ferries cross from St. Martin to Blowing Point; from there it's a short island drive to Rendezvous Bay.

The gate

A gated, private lane leads only to the hotel and the Coral Club. Guests receive a key for a quiet, secure arrival.
