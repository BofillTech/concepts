On the Bay

More than a place to stay

You could happily spend your entire vacation without leaving Rendezvous Bay — sunset walks on the sand, snorkeling the calm shallows, or a chaise, a book and the view of clear turquoise water. And when you want to explore, the rest of Anguilla is a short drive away.

Sunsets at the Coral Club

Frozen cocktails and a glowing horizon at the Coral Club, a short walk down the beach.

The Beach

Two and a half miles of powder-white shore, calm water, and private chaise lounges and umbrellas waiting on the sand.

The Reef Shoreline

Where the sand gives way to rock and reef at the edge of the bay — calm, clear water and easy snorkeling just offshore.

Weddings on the Sand

Say your vows where the Caribbean meets the aisle. See our weddings page to plan.

Swim & Snorkel

The bay's gentle, clear water is made for easy swimming and snorkeling, steps from your porch.

Explore Anguilla

The gated lane leads out to South Hill and the wider island — beaches, restaurants and the neighboring Anguilla Great House.

Beyond the Bay

More to do in Anguilla

Thirty-three beaches, boat trips to the cays, reefs, restaurants and live music — the Anguilla Tourist Board keeps the full list of what's on across the island.
