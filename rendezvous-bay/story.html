1962 Since 1962

The island's first taste of the sea

When Rendezvous Bay Hotel opened its doors in 1962, it became the very first beachfront resort on Anguilla — a quiet pioneer on a stretch of sand that many still call the island's finest.

More than six decades later, it is still family-owned and still gathered along the same calm, whitest bay. Forty acres of natural beauty sit between a bird-filled salt pond and the Caribbean Sea, home to seventeen beachfront villas that open right onto the sand.

Much has changed over the years; one thing hasn't — the beach it sits on. Guests return year after year for the charm, the history, and the beauty of one of the Caribbean's most stunning bays.
