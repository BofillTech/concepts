1962 How it began

Built by hand, one stone at a time

Long before a paved road ever reached this end of Anguilla, Jeremiah and Lydia Gumbs fell for the same unspoiled two-and-a-half-mile shoreline that had drawn the island's Arawak forebears centuries earlier — a historic stretch of land where the Arawaks once prepared for their voyages across the sea.

With little more than vision and determination, the Gumbses raised the first five rooms by hand on the rocky edge of the property, mixing every stone with the island's very first stone crusher and cement mixer. In 1962 they opened the doors of Rendezvous Bay Hotel — Anguilla's first beachfront resort.

How we grew

From five rooms to a beloved bay

In those early days the roads were wild and the nights still dark — yet the land gave freely, with chickens, fruit and fresh vegetables to set beside the day's catch from the sea. Word spread, and over the decades the little hotel grew, welcoming families, travelers and the occasional celebrity to its quiet corner of sand.

The beachfront villas that open onto the bay today are its newest chapter — renovated to pair modern comfort with the pioneering spirit the Gumbses built in. Much has changed since 1962; the one thing that hasn't is the beach it sits on.

Our hotel today

Gated, quiet, and all your own

The entrance runs along a gated, private road that leads only to the hotel, its villas, and the Coral Club — an even quieter corner on the eastern end of Rendezvous Bay, where guests hold a key to the gate and watch the waves put on a show from the comfort of the room.
