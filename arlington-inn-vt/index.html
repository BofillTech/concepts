Where the past meets the present in picturesque Vermont

A Historic Southern Vermont Bed & Breakfast

You cannot drive by the Inn without stopping to admire this landmark property in the center of the historic village of Arlington. The breathtaking architecture reminds one of a bygone era and brings to mind a sense of history and grandeur.

Walk through the door and become drawn in by the crackling fireplace, and the elegantly curving staircase to the second floor. Fine wood details made of maple, cherry, and walnut grace the ceilings, mantles, floors, and moldings.

Once a fine private home built in 1848 by Martin Chester Deming, the property became an inn in 1888 and has remained one ever since. Today travelers will find four buildings on the National Historic Register offering 15 elegant guest rooms which include a full country breakfast.

Elegant Lodging

Three buildings on our Southern Vermont inn property feature unique rooms and suites. Many rooms include two-person jetted tubs, fireplaces, private porches, and patios.

The Main House

Three rooms and our breakfast solarium are located in the Main House, steps away from common and parlor areas. Rooms feature period decor with modern comforts, air conditioning, private bathrooms, and complimentary Wi-Fi.

The Carriage House

Just off the gardens, the Carriage House offers six rooms. Five rooms have gas fireplaces, and four feature jetted tubs perfect for a romantic getaway.

The Old Parsonage

Built in 1830, the Historic Parsonage houses six guest rooms. Some rooms feature private patios or porches, most offer newly updated baths with walk-in tiled showers.
