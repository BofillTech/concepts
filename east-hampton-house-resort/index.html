Nestled in the Hamptons' Most Beautiful Village

East Hampton House is a complete year-round resort set on five private acres of manicured lawns in the heart of East Hampton. Specimen trees and bursts of color from flowering hydrangea and impatiens create a setting that rivals the magnificent estates nearby.

Whether a weekend getaway or a week's vacation, a stay at East Hampton House will leave you refreshed. Savor the fresh ocean breezes, explore world-class beaches, and enjoy the clear starry nights.
