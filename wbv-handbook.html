The Property & The Villas

White Bay Villas is a private 18-acre hillside estate above White Bay on Jost Van Dyke — family owned and operated for 40 years, with 20 standalone buildings hosting anywhere from one to 80 guests. Every villa is private, freestanding, and within walking distance of White Bay Beach, with bedroom air conditioning throughout. The property includes the Main Office, the White Bay Villas Beach Club, and walking trails directly to the beach.

How to talk about air conditioning

Every bedroom on property has powerful A/C. Some living rooms do not — Paradise Villas, Sunset Villa East, and certain Hillside Villas. Fans are available on request. Set expectations honestly: guests here run the A/C before heading to the beach and while sleeping, which is the island rhythm. This is also why some villas are deliberately advertised below their physical sleeping capacity.

Getting Here

Transportation is the most common guest conversation you'll have. Start every one the same way: which airport are they flying into, and what are their flight details? Then walk the matching route below.

Route 1 — Beef Island Airport, Tortola (EIS)

The most direct option. Recommend when flights work.

  1. Land at Beef Island Airport (EIS) and collect luggage.
  2. Taxi to the West End Ferry Terminal — 45–60 minutes depending on traffic. Pre-arranged drivers below.
  3. Arrive at least 30 minutes before the ferry — all ferry terminals are ports of entry.
  4. Board the New Horizon Ferry to Great Harbour, Jost Van Dyke (~20 minutes). Buy tickets in advance at newhorizonferry.com.

Route 2 — St. Thomas Airport (STT), via public ferry

More flight options, but adds a ferry leg. Only works if they land by 3:00 PM.

  1. Land at Cyril E. King Airport (STT) by 3:00 PM at the latest to make the last public ferry connection.
  2. Taxi to the ferry terminal. Shared airport taxis wait to fill and make multiple stops — guests on a ferry deadline should reserve a private taxi in advance (drivers below).
  3. Ferry to Road Town, Tortola on Native Son (nativesonferry.com) or Smith's Ferry (smithsferry.com).
  4. Clear BVI Customs in Road Town.
  5. Taxi from Road Town to West End Ferry Terminal — 25–30 minutes. Leave buffer between connections.
  6. New Horizon Ferry to Great Harbour, Jost Van Dyke.
Departure rule for St. Thomas flyers

Return flights out of STT should be no earlier than 1:30 PM — guests need time to leave Jost Van Dyke, clear U.S. Customs, and reach the airport. Earlier flight? Recommend a private water taxi.

Route 3 — From St. John

Only three days a week.

  1. Inter Island Ferry runs the only direct public route St. John → Jost Van Dyke: Tuesdays, Fridays, and Sundays only (interislandboatservices.com).
  2. Traveling any other day: book a private water taxi instead.

Route 4 — Private water taxi (any airport, any schedule)

Recommend for arrivals outside ferry hours, early STT departures, larger groups, lots of luggage, or anyone who values convenience.

  1. Preferred provider: Allure Charters & Water Taxi — Captain Michael Clyne. Two boats; pickups from both St. Thomas and Tortola; private charters too. allurechartersbvi.com · allurechartersbvi@gmail.com · +1 (284) 441-0000.
  2. Backup: Paradise Water Taxi — paradisewatertaxi.com.
  3. Always book in advance — transfers are scheduled around the guest's flight times, so get flight details first.

On the Island

Provisioning, getting around, dining, and staying connected — the answers guests ask for once the booking is made.

Grocery provisioning — how it works

We've worked with Tortola grocery stores for over 20 years so villas are stocked before guests walk in. Pitch this on every booking — it's a signature service.

  1. Guest places their grocery order with the store several days before arrival.
  2. Guest asks the store to send the order on the 1:00 PM New Horizon Ferry to Jost Van Dyke. Cold and frozen items travel in insulated cooler boxes (the crossing is 15–20 minutes).
  3. The White Bay Villas team collects the order at the ferry dock, delivers it to the villa, stocks the refrigerator and kitchen, and clears the packaging — all before the guest arrives.
  4. Guest inspects the order on arrival. Anything missing, substituted, or incorrect: contact the store immediately for a replacement or refund.
Set the expectation

Island grocery availability differs from the mainland. If a guest can't find an item on the store's website, have them contact the store directly — more products are often available than are listed online.

Jeep rentals

Every villa is walking distance to White Bay Beach, so beach-only guests can skip the jeep. Guests who want to explore the island should rent — taxis charge per person, so a jeep usually wins on cost.

  1. Recommend Paradise Jeep Rentals — the only SUV rentals on Jost Van Dyke. paradisejeeprental.com · +1 (284) 547-1040 · paradise_rentals@yahoo.com.
  2. Reserve as early as possible — vehicles sell out in the busy season, December through April.

Guest Communication Playbook

The non-negotiables. If you follow these on every guest interaction, you're doing the job right.

  • Always ask which airport the guest is arriving at, and request flight details if you don't have them. Every transportation answer depends on it.
  • Recommend the route based on arrival day and time — not a generic answer. Check the St. John day rule (Tue/Fri/Sun) and the STT timing rules (land by 3:00 PM; depart 1:30 PM or later).
  • Always include the official ferry websites in guest communications, remind guests that schedules change, and encourage buying ferry tickets in advance.
  • Always include Allure Charters' contact info whenever a private water taxi comes up — and make clear water taxis must be booked ahead, scheduled to their flights.
  • Never mention Aquatic Rentals — permanently closed as of September 1.
  • Verify occupancy on Sunset Villa East when multiple adults book — confirm who's sleeping where before arrival (living-room sleeper has no A/C).
  • Verify guest counts on Palm Villa OTA bookings — Airbnb imports can show the wrong occupancy. Confirm adults, children, and totals.
  • Pitch grocery provisioning on every booking — order several days ahead, on the 1:00 PM ferry.
  • Recommend WhatsApp before travel — it's how the island communicates.
  • Hold the Paradise Villas occupancy line — 3 adults max (or 2 + 2 kids), and explain the living-room A/C reason honestly.

New Hire Self-Check

Can you answer these without scrolling up? Click each to reveal the answer.

A couple wants a king bed and doesn't mind a slightly longer walk. What do you recommend?

Paradise Villas — king bed, powerful bedroom A/C, quieter hillside location, 5–6 minutes to the beach. Hillside Villas also have kings if they want more space.

A guest lands at St. Thomas at 4:30 PM. What's your answer?

They've missed the safe window for the public ferry chain (land by 3:00 PM). Recommend a private water taxi — Allure Charters, +1 (284) 441-0000 — booked in advance around their flight.

Why are Paradise Villas capped at 3 adults when they physically sleep more?

The queen sofa bed is in the living room, which has no air conditioning. The cap keeps expectations honest and guests comfortable.

Which Seaside Cottage do you offer first, and why?

Mango — bottom of the driveway, closest to the beach trail, and the most requested of the three.

A family of five wants to come over from St. John on a Wednesday. Options?

The Inter Island Ferry only runs Tuesday, Friday, and Sunday — so it's a private water taxi (Allure Charters) on a Wednesday.

What's the earliest return flight you'd let a guest book out of St. Thomas?

1:30 PM. Anything earlier needs a private water taxi to make U.S. Customs and the airport in time.

An Airbnb reservation for Palm Villa shows 4 guests. What do you do?

Verify it — OTA imports often carry wrong guest counts on Palm Villa. Confirm adults, children, and total occupants directly with the guest before arrival.

A guest asks about renting a car. What do you tell them?

Paradise Jeep Rentals — the only SUV rentals on the island (+1 284-547-1040, paradisejeeprental.com). Book early for December–April. If they're planning a beach-only stay, they can skip it — every villa is walking distance to White Bay Beach.

Which ferry do grocery orders travel on, and what happens when they land?

The 1:00 PM New Horizon Ferry from Tortola. Our team collects the order at the dock, delivers and stocks the villa, and removes the packaging before the guest arrives.

What service do you never recommend anymore?

Aquatic Rentals — permanently ceased operations September 1.
