Welcome

Victorian chic, with every modern comfort.

Built by wealthy banker and lead smelter Augustus Estey, this Italianate estate once opened its doors for entertaining Galena's most prominent citizens. Today it welcomes travelers the same way — into eight guest rooms, each with its own private bath and unique character.

Every room is dressed in Egyptian cotton linens and a deluxe pillow-top mattress. Mornings begin with a full breakfast before you head out to explore Jo Daviess County; evenings end with a soak in the hot tub spa or a quiet turn through the English gardens.

Rooms & Suites

Three Porches & English Gardens

An evening that unfolds slowly.

Relax on one of three porches, or slip out to the English gardens with a glass of iced tea while you hear the history of the mansion over seasonal drinks and cheese. Challenge a fellow guest to a spirited game of pool in the billiard parlor.

When the evening cools, ease into the hot tub spa — secluded under the stars in a private gazebo on the edge of the gardens.

  • Three porches for morning coffee or evening wine
  • English gardens across two manicured acres
  • Billiard parlor
  • Private hot tub spa in a garden gazebo
