Where Adventure Meets Comfort

Nestled on the shores of Munising Bay in Michigan's Upper Peninsula, Roam Inn is your gateway to four-season adventure. Our historic boutique hotel combines lumber-baron elegance with modern comfort, offering 18 thoughtfully designed rooms—including suites with private balconies overlooking the bay.

From sunrise kayaks to winter snowmobile trails, from Pictured Rocks National Lakeshore to Hiawatha National Forest, you're minutes from world-class outdoor recreation. Return to Tracey's restaurant and bar, where locally inspired cuisine and craft cocktails await on our waterfront terrace.

Check in at 3 PM, check out at 11 AM, and make every moment count in the heart of the UP.
