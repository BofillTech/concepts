Your Home Away From Home

Ponus has offered members a relaxed, welcoming place since 1911 — a boating and social club tucked into a scenic, nautical corner of Stamford's Harbor Point waterfront. What began as a modest boating society has grown into both a protected home port and a clubhouse where a strong sense of camaraderie runs as deep as the channel outside.

Members enjoy harborfront dining with spectacular sunset views, whether on the spacious outdoor deck or inside the clubhouse, alongside a seasonal calendar of live music, holiday parties, and summer entertainment. As proud members of the Yacht Clubs of America, Ponus members also carry reciprocal privileges at hundreds of clubs nationwide.

The Marina

Dock with ease in a protected harbor.

Individual slips sit inside a breakwater-protected channel, sized for vessels from 18 to 47 feet. Every slip includes dockside water and power, with both 110V and 220V service on 30- and 50-amp connections.

A designated area is set aside for members and insured contractors to handle repairs and maintenance, and secure winter storage — on land or in the water, protected by a bubbler system against freeze damage — carries boats through the off-season. A private boat ramp rounds out the setup for smaller craft.

The Clubhouse & Deck

The clubhouse pairs a full-service dining room with a lively bar and a banquet room that opens straight onto a newly renovated waterfront deck. Lunch and dinner run year-round in a smoke-free setting — the game on flat-screens in the bar, or a sunset over Stamford Harbor from the dining room or deck.

The culinary team moves between casual pub fare and more considered dinner plates, built fresh for every service.

Private Events

The waterfront banquet space holds up to 145 guests, with a deck that puts Stamford Harbor on full display. Ponus hosts weddings, birthdays, corporate meetings, fundraisers, and holiday parties — and while membership has its privileges, non-members are welcome to book private events here too. Menus run to upscale buffets, chef stations, and hors d'oeuvres rather than formal plated dinners.

Membership & Reciprocity

Ponus is a members-only organization, closed to the public. Guests are welcome when accompanied by a member, and reciprocal Yacht Club members are welcome with identification. As a member of the Yacht Clubs of America, Ponus extends reciprocal privileges at hundreds of clubs nationwide — and the calendar fills in year-round with live music, holiday parties, and summer entertainment from bands to comedy nights.
