A quiet natural setting

Stay among the pines, minutes from everywhere in Bend

Pine Ridge Inn has welcomed guests since 1996 from a quiet rise above the Deschutes River. It's the kind of place where the pace slows the moment you park the car — gas fireplace lit, curtains open to the trees — but where downtown Bend, the Old Mill District, and the Cascade Lakes Highway are all just minutes away. Come for the ski season at Mt. Bachelor or the summer float down the river; either way, you're never far from the good stuff.
