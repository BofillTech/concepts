Pricing & Fees

How much does QuillStay cost?

One flat price: $10 per room, per month. That covers the PMS, the booking engine, the channel manager and all the core modules. A 36-room motel pays $360 a month. The only extras are two optional add-ons, dynamic pricing and door lock integration, at $100 a month each. There are no surprises on the invoice.

Are there setup, migration, training or support fees?

No. Setup, data migration and staff training are free, and support is included. We configure your rooms, rates, taxes and policies for you, and you don't pay anything until you go live.

Do you charge per booking or take a commission?

No. QuillStay doesn't charge per-booking fees or commissions, so direct bookings through your own website skip the OTA commission entirely. Card processing fees are billed by your payment processor, not by us. We work with Host Merchant Services, and they guarantee a better rate than your current processor.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. QuillStay is month to month. If you're not happy, you're never locked in. If you require a longer-term contract, we'd be happy to put one together.

Switching & Migration

How hard is it to switch from my current system?

We do the setup for you. We load your room types, photos and branding, set up your rates, minimum stays, fees, taxes, deposits and cancellation terms, connect payments and your OTAs, and test everything before you switch. Your part is small: answer a few questions, check what we built, and pick your go-live date. Your current system stays live until QuillStay is fully configured and tested, so there's no downtime.

Will my rooms, rates, policies and photos carry over, or do I rebuild everything?

You don't rebuild anything. We do it. We set up your room types, rate plans, policies, photos and booking content in QuillStay, matched to how you run today, and you review it before go-live.

Can you move my existing reservations and guest data?

Yes. Future reservations are loaded so they're ready on day one, and we check every count before you switch. We also import your guest/customer list (and owners, for condo-hotels). Past stays and guest history come over too. We'll find a way to move it. For one property we even loaded future reservations from photos of their reservation book.

How long does onboarding take?

We can move as fast as 1–2 weeks, but we'll go at your pace. We only turn on the parts you use, and you pick the go-live date. Front desk staff are usually fully trained in two to four hours.

Do you have an onboarding guide?

Yes. Our onboarding guide walks through the whole journey, step by step. In short:

  • Getting to know you: we learn how you run today, confirm QuillStay covers it, and flag any special integrations early.
  • Setting up your property: we load your rooms, photos and branding, then set up your rates and stay rules, fees, taxes and policies, payments, and automatic guest emails and booking site.
  • Optional tracks: only what your property already uses, such as group blocks, owners and co-op reporting, or door locks and key cards (an add-on).
  • Your reservations: we bring your future and past reservations over from your previous system, and we check every count before you switch.
  • Going live: we link your OTA channels, test sample bookings, reports and emails, and go live. We train your team on daily workflows and support you after launch.

Your part is small: answer a few questions, review what we build, and tell us when you're ready to switch. The timeline fits your property, and we only turn on the tracks you need.

Booking Engine & Website

What does the guest booking experience look like?

The booking engine matches your brand. Guests can browse rooms with photos, descriptions and real prices (taxes shown up front), open a room's detail page, or pick dates off a month-view availability calendar. Then they book in a couple of clicks. Promo and group codes are built in. We'll set up a test version with your own rooms so you can click through it before you decide.

Do I need to change my website?

Usually not. The only thing that changes is where your "Book Now" buttons and links point, so they go to your QuillStay booking engine, and we can make that change for you. Your website itself stays as is. Embeddable booking widgets are coming, and Google Analytics / ad-pixel tracking on the booking engine is coming by January 2027.

Can I require a card without taking a deposit?

Yes. For each property you choose how the booking engine handles cards: take a deposit (for example, the first night), or take a card on file with a $0 deposit.

Do you have an AI chatbot for my website?

It's coming in January 2027. You'll get an AI chatbot for your website, plus a chatbot inside the booking engine, to answer guest questions and help them book.

Channel Manager & OTAs

Which OTAs do you connect to?

QuillStay has a built-in channel manager with two-way sync to Booking.com, Expedia, Airbnb, Vrbo and Google Hotels, all live today. Reservations flow in, and your rates and availability update across every channel automatically, so there's no re-typing and no double bookings.

Do I have to give the OTAs all my rooms?

No. You control how many rooms go to each OTA.

How do OTA payments and virtual cards work?

QuillStay labels OTA virtual cards on the reservation. If the property collects payment, you charge the virtual card from the reservation. If the channel collects, you just record the OTA commission. Guest emails are suppressed for OTA bookings so guests don't get duplicates.

Payments & PCI

Who processes card payments?

Typically Host Merchant Services, using the Fluid Pay gateway. We set it up and test it end to end before you go live, and Host guarantees a better rate than your current processor. You can see how it fits together on our payments page.

Is card data secure? What about PCI?

Yes. Cards are stored as secure tokens in a PCI DSS Level 1 certified third-party card vault, so your PMS holds tokens instead of raw card numbers. The vault is certified as a PCI DSS Level 1 Service Provider (PCI DSS v4.0.1), the highest level of payment card security certification, and an independent Qualified Security Assessor audits it every year.

How do deposits, refunds and incidental holds work?

Deposit and cancellation rules are set per property. Front desk staff take payments, process refunds, add credits and discounts, and place or remove authorization holds right from the reservation. Incidental holds can be required at check-in, or you can set the system to warn staff if one wasn't taken. A hold drops off automatically after 7 days, or you can release it at checkout.

Can I take a card in person?

Yes. Once your payment gateway is linked up, you can enter cards right in the system. A physical card reader integration is coming in early 2027 (January/February).

Day-to-Day Operations

How do changes, cancellations and no-shows work?

Everything happens from the reservation itself. You can extend, shorten or shift a stay, and QuillStay charges only the difference. You can also change the room type, rate plan or occupancy, move a guest to another room, or split a reservation. When you cancel or mark a no-show, you choose to apply your policy fee, enter a custom amount, or charge nothing. If someone makes a mistake, you can undo a check-in, reopen a checkout, reinstate a cancellation or undo a no-show.

Will I know when a new booking comes in?

Yes. QuillStay can email your team about every new reservation the desk didn't make, whether it came from your booking engine or an OTA.

What emails do guests get?

Confirmation, pre-arrival reminder, post-stay and cancellation emails go out automatically, and staff can resend any of them from the reservation. Post-stay emails can link guests to your Google Business page (or wherever you want reviews), and we can add a QR code too.

Can I track guest vehicles?

Yes. Each reservation has a license plate field, and the Vehicle List report shows every plate in the lot based on who's in-house.

Do owner stays need a credit card?

That's up to you. Owner reservations lock automatically, and since there's usually nothing to charge, a card on file is optional.
