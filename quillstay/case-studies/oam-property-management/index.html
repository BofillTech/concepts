The challenge

Before switching to QuillStay, OAM Property Management managed online bookings, guest payments, and refunds across a system that put most of the manual work on the front desk team — every payment logged separately, every refund a multi-step process, every new hire needing extended training before they could run a shift confidently.

"QuillStay has made it much easier to manage online bookings, payments, and refunds."

The switch

OAM migrated to QuillStay with a full data transfer of existing reservations and guest history, and their front desk team was brought up to speed on the new system directly — no lengthy manual, no separate payments platform to learn on top of the PMS.

Before

Payments, refunds, and bookings handled as separate manual steps, requiring more front desk time per transaction.

After

Bookings, payments, and refunds handled in one system — described by the team as "extremely easy... taking a lot of work off their shoulders."
