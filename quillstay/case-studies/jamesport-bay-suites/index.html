The property

South Jamesport, NY — bayfront, 11 rooms

Jamesport Bay Suites is an 11-room waterfront property on the bay in South Jamesport, NY — in the heart of Long Island's North Fork wine country. Small properties like this one run lean: the same handful of people handle the front desk, housekeeping, rates, and guest communication, often all in the same shift.

The impact

Since moving to QuillStay, Jamesport Bay Suites has found day-to-day operations dramatically easier to run — and is generating more bookings directly online, without leaning as heavily on OTAs to fill rooms.

Before

Day-to-day operations took more manual effort for a small team, and online bookings weren't where the property wanted them to be.

After

Operations the team describes as wildly easier day to day, alongside a real increase in bookings coming directly through the property's own site.
