Text Messaging Opt-In

QuillStay is a hotel property management system and booking engine operated by Bofill Technologies Inc. Hotels that use QuillStay can text their guests about a reservation, but only guests who agree to it.

How guests opt in

When a guest makes a reservation on a hotel's booking page, the form asks for a mobile phone number. Under the phone field is a checkbox that is not checked by default. A guest agrees to receive texts only by checking the box themselves. The example below shows what guests see.

Opting in is optional

Agreeing to receive text messages is optional and is not required to book a reservation. Guests who leave the box unchecked can still complete their booking and will receive no texts.

What the messages are

Texts are transactional messages from the hotel about the guest's own reservation: confirmations, pre-arrival and check-in details, and replies to the guest's questions. They are not marketing messages.
