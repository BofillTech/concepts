Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026

QuillStay is a hotel property management system and booking engine operated by Bofill Technologies Inc. of Stamford, Connecticut ("QuillStay," "we," "us"). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and how we protect it. It covers our website at quillstay.com, the QuillStay software used by hotel staff, and guest reservations that hotels process through QuillStay.

1. Who this policy covers

We handle information about three groups of people:

  • Hotel staff who have an account in the QuillStay software.
  • Guests whose reservations are made and managed through QuillStay by a hotel that uses it.
  • Website visitors who contact us or request a demo.

For guest reservation information, the hotel is the business the guest chose to book with. We process that information on the hotel's behalf and under its instructions, to provide the QuillStay service.

2. Information we collect

Hotel staff account information

Name, work email address, phone number, hotel name, job role, sign-in credentials, and records of activity in the software (such as logs of who changed a reservation and when).

Guest reservation details

Processed on behalf of our hotel clients: guest name, email address, phone number, stay dates, room type and rate, number of guests, special requests, and the messages exchanged between the hotel and the guest about the reservation.

Payment information

Payment cards are handled by our PCI-compliant payment processors. QuillStay does not store full card numbers. We may receive limited payment details from the processor, such as the card type, the last four digits, and whether a payment succeeded.

Website visitors

Information you choose to send us, such as your name, email address, phone number and property details when you request a demo or contact support, plus basic technical information (such as IP address and browser type) that is collected in the ordinary course of serving web pages.

3. How we use information

  • To run reservations: creating, changing, confirming and cancelling bookings, and managing rooms, rates and availability.
  • To process payments through our payment processors.
  • To support guest communications for hotels, including emails and, for guests who have agreed to receive them, text messages about a reservation.
  • To provide customer support, keep the service secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and improve the software.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell personal information.

4. Payment cards

Card payments are collected and processed by our PCI-compliant payment processors, which follow the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard. Card details are sent to those processors and are not stored on QuillStay systems.

5. Text messaging

Hotels that use QuillStay may send text messages about a guest's reservation to guests who have agreed to receive them. These are transactional messages, not marketing. See our SMS / Text Messaging Terms and how guests opt in on our Text Messaging Opt-In page.

Mobile numbers and text messaging opt-in consent are not shared, sold, or rented to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information may be shared only with service providers that help us deliver messages (such as our SMS provider), and only for that purpose.

6. How information is shared

  • With the hotel. Guest reservation information is available to the hotel the guest booked with, and to that hotel's authorized staff.
  • With service providers. We use providers to host the software, process payments, send email, and deliver text messages. They may use information only to provide their service to us.
  • For legal reasons. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect the rights, safety and security of our clients, guests and QuillStay.
  • In a business transfer. If QuillStay or Bofill Technologies Inc. is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, information may transfer as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

7. Data retention

We keep information for as long as a hotel's account is active and as needed to provide the service, meet legal, tax and accounting obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. After a hotel ends its service, it can request an export of its data for up to 90 days.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and sign-in controls for staff accounts. No method of transmitting or storing information is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices

  • Guests who want to see, correct or delete reservation information should contact the hotel they booked with. We will help the hotel respond.
  • Text messages: reply STOP to any text message to opt out at any time.
  • Hotel staff and website visitors can contact us at the address below to ask about their information.

10. Children

QuillStay is intended for hotel staff and for adults making reservations. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
