About

A straightforward stay, done right.

Belvedere Inn is Schenectady's only independent boutique inn — three miles from downtown Schenectady, Proctors Theatre, Union College, and General Electric, with Albany International Airport just fifteen minutes away.

Every room comes with high-speed wireless internet, so you can get your work done on the road, and a free continental breakfast to start the day. Our goal is simple: make your stay as comfortable and convenient as possible.
