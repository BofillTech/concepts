Questions

Frequently asked questions

What does WaveAerospace make?

WaveAerospace designs and builds autonomous aircraft for U.S. and allied defense: the Falcon II Nyx Group 2 tactical ISR aircraft, the MULE heavy-lift resupply aircraft and the Huntress hover-to-dash turbojet.

Can WaveAerospace aircraft fly in bad weather?

Yes. All three aircraft are built to operate in rain, fog, heavy cloud and degraded visibility, conditions that ground most other unmanned aircraft.

Can WaveAerospace aircraft fly without GPS?

Yes. Every WaveAerospace aircraft uses inertial navigation (INS) to stay on course when GPS is jammed, spoofed or unavailable.

Are WaveAerospace aircraft hardened against electronic warfare?

Yes. Every WaveAerospace aircraft has electronic warfare shielding and coatings.

Has WaveAerospace been tested by the U.S. Army?

Yes. The Falcon flew more than 80 sorties at the Army Expeditionary Warrior Experiment (AEWE) 2025 and was the only aircraft flying through a severe rainstorm while other air operations were grounded.

Where are WaveAerospace aircraft built?

WaveAerospace aircraft are designed and built in Connecticut. Operations are based at 700 Great Meadow Road, Stratford, CT 06615.

How do I get full specifications or pricing?

Full specifications, test data, demonstrations and pricing are shared with qualified U.S. and allied defense customers and investors through a briefing. Request one at sales@waveaerospace.com or 1-203-392-1400.
