Common to the family

Shared across all three.

All-weather

  • Force 10 launch and recovery on all three aircraft, including autonomous recovery on moving, pitching decks.
  • Flies blind. Zero-visibility takeoff and landing.
  • Rain, wind and ice. Heavy and freezing rain in 60-knot winds.

Contested

  • Electronic-warfare hardened. Shielded electronics, anti-spoofing GNSS and inertial navigation.
  • Hard to track. Huntress has flight characteristics and profile unlike any aircraft: abrupt stops and near-right-angle turns make its path hard to predict.
