A peaceful retreat

Mornings over the lake. Evenings around the fire.

Start with breakfast overlooking Lake Michigan. Spend the day on our kayaks and bikes, hike two miles of wooded trails on the property, take a dip in the lake, or wander the shops and galleries in town.

At night, come back for a spectacular sunset around the fire pits, or stargaze from the deck at the water's edge. We sit on four acres of shade trees, lawns and gardens along Lake Shore Drive.
