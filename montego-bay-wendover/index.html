Modern Luxury Meets Desert Adventure

The Montego Bay Resort is Wendover's newest hotel and casino, featuring 552 spacious rooms and suites designed with your comfort in mind. Our contemporary accommodations offer floor-to-ceiling windows, luxurious triple sheeting, and all the modern amenities for a comfortable and convenient stay.

Enjoy three great restaurants including our fine dining choice, Romanza, along with exciting casino action on the floor. Relax by the outdoor pool and jetted tubs, stay active in our workout facility, and stay connected with free Wi-Fi throughout all of the resort and hotel rooms.

Whether you're here for gaming, dining, or desert exploration, Montego Bay Resort offers the perfect blend of luxury and entertainment on the Utah-Nevada border.
