Your Maine Home Base

The Bar Harbor Villager is a charismatic, affordable motel in the heart of downtown Bar Harbor, within a 5-minute walk to shopping, restaurants and the beautiful Bar Harbor waterfront. Breathtaking views and miles of pristine hiking and biking trails await you just 2 miles away in Acadia National Park.

Our family-owned motel offers cozy, clean rooms with modern amenities to serve as your "home away from home" during your visit to Mount Desert Island. We take pride in facilitating memorable guest experiences and providing exceptional customer service in comfortable accommodations.

Wake up to downtown Bar Harbor—filled with artists, exquisite food and drinks, and surrounded by spectacular ocean and mountain vistas. All the bustle of this true New England destination without breaking the bank.
