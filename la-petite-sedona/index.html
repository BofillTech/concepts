The first building into town

Coming down Oak Creek Canyon, we’re the first thing you see.

That puts us right where the scenic drive ends and Uptown begins: a short walk to cafes and shops, yet far enough to hear the quiet. Most guests end up on the patio or the deck with a book and the Red Rocks.

Explore Sedona

Ask the front desk. We know the good stuff.

  1. 01

    Pink Jeep Tours

    Scenic, off-road and high-adventure fun in red rock country, including the Ancient Ruin Tour for history buffs.

  2. 02

    Verde Canyon Railroad

    Refurbished train cars, a drink in hand, and a slow glide through the canyon the way travel used to be.

  3. 03

    Cowboy Way Adventures

    Real working cow horses and an experienced cowboy wrangler, from sandy washes to river crossings.

  4. 04

    Sedona Vortex Tours

    Visit the vortex sites Sedona is famous for, with guides who know the landmarks and the stories.
