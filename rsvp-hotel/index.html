Small-Town Sisters, Big Dreams

Sisters Hillary and Haylee Folkvord — small-town girls raised on a 3-generation Montana farm — meticulously resurrected what was once the Rainbow Hotel into RSVP Motel BZN.

In 2018, they opened The Farmer's Daughters Cafe & Eatery inside the hotel, serving real, whole food to guests and locals alike. Today, RSVP is Bozeman's #1 Rated Hotel with 4.5/5 from 2,813 reviews.

The furnishings? Sourced from Italy, France, and Bozeman. The murals? Hand-painted by local artists. The vibe? Playful, bubbly, and unapologetically colorful.

Midtown Bozeman, Gateway to Yellowstone

RSVP Motel is in Midtown Bozeman at 510 N 7th Ave, just 15 minutes from Bozeman Yellowstone International Airport and steps from downtown Bozeman's walkable restaurants, breweries, and shops.

The ELM — a 1,100-capacity concert venue — is next door. Nearby attractions include Museum of the Rockies, Pete's Hill, Big Sky Resort skiing, Yellowstone National Park, and Bozeman Hot Springs.

Recommended by RSVP: Revelry, Blackbird, Plonk, Dave's Sushi, Bridger Brewing, and the Bozeman farmers market.
