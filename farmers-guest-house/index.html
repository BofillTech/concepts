The oldest restored inn in downtown Galena.

Built in 1867 as the Farmers Home Hotel and General Store, Farmers Guest House has kept its doors open to travelers for more than 150 years — nine rooms and suites, a famous country breakfast, and WineTime every afternoon at five.

A day at the Farmers 334 Spring Street

The house keeps good hours.

Some inns hand you a key and a brochure. This one keeps a schedule it has honed for a century and a half — and every entry below is included with your stay.

MorningFrom the kitchen

Breakfast, made fresh just for you.

You'll wake to the aromas of a full country breakfast served in our spacious dining room — Praline Pecan French Toast, Eggs Benedict, our Signature Egg Casserole, fresh fruit scones and local seasonal specialties, with special diets happily accommodated with advance notice.

DaytimeOut the front door

Main Street is a stroll away.

From our front steps in Galena's downtown historic district, you're one block from storied Main Street — shopping, art galleries, antique shops, downtown wine tasting rooms, trolley tours, world-class spas, and the U.S. Grant State Historic Site.

5:00P.M. · Daily

WineTime, delivered to your room.

Every afternoon, WineTime arrives — your choice of our red, white or sweet wine with an accompanying cheese assortment. Enjoy it in the comfort of your room, out in the private gardens, or in the first-floor common rooms among the games, puzzles and books.

EveningIn the gardens

Patios, gardens, and a night in.

Our private outdoor patios and gardens are yours in every season. Inside, the living room and library hold a vast collection of games, puzzles and a DVD library — and The Littlest Gift Shop, our on-site Galena gift shop, stays open round the clock.

The register Nine rooms & suites

Nine rooms. Nine names.

Every room in the house carries a woman's name and its own character — with a private bath, TV, mini fridge, microwave and Keurig coffee maker in each, and king beds in over half of them. Sign the register under whichever name suits you.
