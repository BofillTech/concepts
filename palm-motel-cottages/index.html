The Sign

The town voted to keep it.

The P.A.L.M. opened in 1944 as the Palm Motor Court. In 1966 a distinctive palm-shaped sign went up, spelling out "Palm Motel." By the late '90s the property had grown into Palm Cottages — three new luxury cottages, Ashland's only heated saltwater pool — and outgrew its own name. In 2002, Ashland put it to a community vote on whether the old sign should come down. Thousands of signatures later, the sign stayed. It's protected today, and it's not going anywhere.

P.A.L.M.

Peace And Love Matter — the acronym that stuck after three name changes, and the closest thing the motel has to a mission statement.

Rooms & Suites

Sixteen rooms. No two alike.

Every room carries the same retro-cool style with a full set of modern comforts — flat-screen TV, free Wi-Fi, air conditioning, and a kitchenette or breakfast nook in most. Here are three to start with; browse all sixteen when you book.

#0 · One Double Bed

The most economical room, greeting you with a bistro table right outside the door — ideal for a solo traveler or a couple on a budget. Not pet-friendly, but most of our other rooms are.

#1 · Deluxe Queen

A retro-styled kitchenette with microwave, coffee pot, fridge and cooktop — brewed with coffee from an Ashland organic roaster. Great for an extended stay, and pet-friendly.

#12 · Deluxe Suite

A two-room suite with two queen beds and a full kitchenette behind a curtained privacy wall — a guest favorite for groups and families, and pet-friendly too.

Pool & Gardens

Ashland's only heated saltwater pool.

Open in summer and fall, the pool comes with private cabanas, a fire pit, and lounge chairs — first-come, first-served, or reserve a cabana for a block of time at the front desk. It sits inside grounds that have quietly won awards: mature trees, lawn seating, and gardens expanded in 2000 that keep getting better.

  • Private cabanas & a fire pit, first-come or reserved
  • Rain barrels collect water for the gardens
  • Zero-gasoline landscaping and irrigation equipment
  • Two EV charging stations, plentiful drive-up parking

The Courtyard

Meet artist Krysta Bannis.

Drawn to the beauty of artistic expression, the owners fashioned their new courtyard into a mosaic masterpiece — engaging local artist Krysta Bannis to design a piece that mirrors the essence and vitality of the property's vision. It spells out the motel's own acronym in tile: P.A.L.M. — Peace and Love Matter.
