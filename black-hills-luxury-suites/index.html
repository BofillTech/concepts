In the heart of the forest

The Black Hills Luxury Suites sit in the “heart” of the Black Hills National Forest — your central vacation location.

Enjoy local tours, fishing, trail rides, Custer State Park, and Needles Highway — with Mount Rushmore and Crazy Horse Memorial only minutes away. We are a family owned and operated property, dedicated to bringing you the personal service you deserve.

From our front door

  • 0.3 MI Breakout Hill City The only live escape games in the central Black Hills — three rooms to choose from, right on Main Street.
  • 6.9 MI Mount Rushmore National Memorial The massive mountainside memorial dedicated to four former United States Presidents.
