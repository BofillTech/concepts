The Garden House

Your Island Escape in Old Town Key West

Party in Front, Escape Out Back

The Garden House is an intimate, island-style bed & breakfast nestled in the historic Old Town district of Key West. Located on a quiet, tree-lined street with comfortable beds, crisp white linens, and a temperature-controlled pool, our homey atmosphere will have you forgetting your worries as you are overcome by a sense of calm and tranquility.

Footsteps away from Duval St., Mallory Square, and the Historic Seaport, the Garden House is the perfect starting point as you set out to explore everything our island paradise has to offer.
