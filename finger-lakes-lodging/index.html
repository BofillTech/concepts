The Great Room

The whole lodge shares one hearth.

In the centre of the building is a room every guest has a key to: a large stone and gas fireplace, a single-serve coffee and tea station, a microwave, an eight-person dining table, a couch, a television and a shelf of board games.

The back door opens onto the deck and the lawn — which is where most of a summer evening here tends to end up.

Pet policy

Three rooms take dogs, and we mean it kindly.

Up to two small dogs are welcome in the standard queen rooms at the far right end of the lodge — rooms 712, 713 and 714. The fee is $75 per pet, per night, plus tax. King rooms, double queens and the suites are not pet friendly.

Register your dog when you book: a pet arriving unannounced can’t be accommodated in a pet-friendly room. Dogs stay leashed outside the room, use the designated area behind the building, and stay out of the Great Room.

Booking with a dog is done over the phone rather than online, so call the lodge directly and we’ll put you in the right room.

Checking in

Check-in is at the front desk of the Mirbeau Inn & Spa, across Route 20 and slightly east of the lodge. Rooms are guaranteed by 4:00 p.m. and check-out is noon. Arriving early? Tell us and we’ll try to have the room ready sooner.

Good to know

  • Free parking on site.
  • Smoking is not permitted in the rooms or on the grounds.
  • Cancel seven or more days ahead for a refund of the deposit — fourteen days on holiday and event weekends.
  • The Adirondack Suite is on the second floor; there is no elevator.
