The Property & The Villas

White Bay Villas is a private 18-acre hillside estate above White Bay on Jost Van Dyke — family owned and operated for 40 years, with 20 standalone buildings hosting anywhere from one to 80 guests. Every villa is private, freestanding, and within walking distance of White Bay Beach, with bedroom air conditioning throughout. The property includes the Main Office, the White Bay Villas Beach Club, and walking trails directly to the beach.

How to talk about air conditioning

Every bedroom on property has powerful A/C. Some living rooms do not — Paradise Villas, Sunset Villa East, and certain Hillside Villas. Fans are available on request. Set expectations honestly: the property runs on energy-efficient solar panels and practices water conservation, so A/C is used at night for sleeping — which is the island rhythm anyway. This is also why some villas are deliberately advertised below their physical sleeping capacity.

Getting Here

Transportation is the most common guest conversation you'll have. Start every one the same way: which airport are they flying into, and what are their flight details? Then walk the matching route below.

Option 1 — Beef Island Airport, Tortola (EIS): the most direct

Two ways from EIS — the Trellis Bay water taxi is the fastest, most direct way to reach Jost Van Dyke, period.

  1. Fastest: clear BVI Customs at the airport, walk 4–5 minutes to Trellis Bay Marina, and board a pre-booked water taxi with Allure Charters (allurechartersbvi.com) — about 25 minutes to Jost Van Dyke.
  2. By ferry: land taxi to the West End Ferry Dock (45–55 minutes), then the New Horizon Ferry to Great Harbour, Jost Van Dyke — 15–20 minutes across (newhorizonferry.com).

Option 2 — St. Thomas (STT) via Red Hook: our recommended ferry route

More flight options than Tortola, with two ferries. Only works if they land by 3:30–4:00 PM.

  1. Land at Cyril E. King Airport (STT) by 3:30–4:00 PM at the latest to make the last 5:30 PM fast ferry out of Red Hook.
  2. Taxi to Red Hook on the island's East End. Shared airport taxis wait to fill and make stops — guests on a ferry deadline should reserve a private driver (below).
  3. Road Town Fast Ferry from Red Hook to West End, Tortola — last departure 5:30 PM (roadtownfastferry.com). Clear BVI Customs.
  4. New Horizon Ferry from West End to Great Harbour, Jost Van Dyke — last departure 6:00 PM.
  5. Arriving at West End after 6:00 PM? They won't make the last New Horizon — a pre-arranged Allure water taxi covers the final leg to Jost Van Dyke.
Departure rule for St. Thomas flyers

Return flights out of STT should be no earlier than 1:30–2:00 PM if taking the earliest ferry back — guests must clear U.S. Customs and allow a 45-minute taxi to the airport, and the airport/Port Authority asks travelers with checked bags to arrive 3 hours before departure. Earlier flight? A water taxi costs more but is well worth it and far less stressful.

Option 3 — St. Thomas (STT) via Charlotte Amalie

The alternative STT ferry routing, through Road Town.

  1. Taxi about 5 minutes from the airport to the Charlotte Amalie Ferry Dock.
  2. Road Town Fast Ferry to Road Town, Tortola (roadtownfastferry.com); clear BVI Customs.
  3. Land taxi 25–30 minutes to the West End Ferry Dock.
  4. New Horizon Ferry to Great Harbour, Jost Van Dyke. Reaching Road Town at 5:00 PM means likely missing the last 6:00 PM New Horizon — pre-arrange a water taxi for the final leg.

Option 4 — Inter Island Ferry from St. Thomas & St. John: 3 days a week

The direct public ferry for guests already on island — Tuesdays, Fridays, and Sundays only, early morning.

  1. Departs Red Hook, St. Thomas at 7:30 AM and Cruz Bay, St. John at 8:00 AM (interislandboatservices.com). Check-in 45 minutes before departure.
  2. Returns from Great Harbour, Jost Van Dyke at 3:30 PM.
  3. Fares do not include customs/immigration fees — an additional $30 per person.
  4. Traveling any other day: book a private water taxi instead.

Option 5 — Private water taxi / charter (any airport, any schedule)

Recommend for arrivals outside ferry hours, late arrivals, early STT departures, larger groups, lots of luggage, or anyone who values convenience. Always book ahead — the sooner the better; transfers are scheduled around flight times, so get flight details first.

  1. Pickups: from EIS it's the 4–5 minute walk to Trellis Bay Marina. From St. Thomas, water taxis pick up on the East End — Red Hook / American Yacht Harbor or Sapphire Beach Marina — clear BVI Customs at Jost Van Dyke or West End, then continue to the villas' side of the island.
  2. First choice: Allure Charters & Water Taxi — Captain Michael Clyne. Two boats; pickups from St. Thomas and Tortola. allurechartersbvi.com · allurechartersbvi@gmail.com · +1 (284) 441-0000.
  3. Also recommended: Paradise Water Taxi (paradisewatertaxi.com) and Chillout Charters (chilloutchartersbvi.com).
  4. All three also run private charters — snorkeling, diving, island hopping — a great way to explore more of the islands during a stay.
Ferry rules that apply to every route

All ferry terminals are ports of entry — arrive 30 minutes before departure (Inter Island asks 45). Schedules change: confirm times on each ferry's own website and buy tickets online ahead to guarantee seats. Native Son Ferry (nativesonferry.co) and Smith's Ferry (smithsferry.com) also make the crossing but do not run every day — check their sites against the guest's exact dates.

On the Island

Provisioning, getting around, and staying connected — the answers guests ask for once the booking is made.

Grocery provisioning — how it works

Arrive on island with the villa fully stocked with favorite snacks, beverages, and breakfast items — most lunches and dinners are enjoyed out at the beach restaurants and bars. We've worked with the Tortola stores for over 20 years. Pitch this on every booking — it's a signature service.

  1. Guest orders online 5–7 days before arrival ($50 minimum order) from either Riteway Foods (riteway.vg) or Bobby's Marketplace (bobbysmarketplacevi.com) — Bobby's recently added online ordering.
  2. Guest tells the grocer they're staying at White Bay Villas and selects delivery on the 1:00 PM ferry from West End to Jost Van Dyke on their arrival day. Cold and frozen items travel in insulated cooler boxes (the crossing is 15–20 minutes).
  3. Guest forwards the store's confirmation email to maria@jostvandyke.com and info@jostvandyke.com, and confirms the service in writing before arrival so it's added to the reservation.
  4. Once the ferry lands, our staff picks up the order, delivers it to the villa, stocks the kitchen, and takes away all packaging — everything is ready when the guest arrives.
  5. Guest checks the order after settling in. Stores sometimes substitute a similar item or send an out-of-stock item the next day — for anything missing, the guest contacts the grocer directly for the replacement or a refund.
Fees to quote

Provisioning service fee: $50 for 1–5 boxes · $75 for 6–10 boxes · $100 for 11–20 boxes. Ferry charge: $4 per box, paid by us in advance and billed to the guest's folio.

Guest Communication Playbook

The non-negotiables. If you follow these on every guest interaction, you're doing the job right.

  • Always ask which airport the guest is arriving at, and request flight details if you don't have them. Every transportation answer depends on it.
  • Recommend the route based on arrival day and time — not a generic answer. Check the Inter Island days (Tue/Fri/Sun from STT & St. John) and the STT timing rules (land by 3:30–4:00 PM; depart 1:30–2:00 PM or later).
  • Lead with the fastest EIS option — a pre-booked Allure water taxi from Trellis Bay Marina, a 4–5 minute walk from the Beef Island airport, about 25 minutes to Jost Van Dyke.
  • Always include the official ferry websites in guest communications, remind guests that schedules change, and encourage buying tickets online ahead to guarantee seats.
  • Always include Allure Charters' contact info whenever a private water taxi or charter comes up — allurechartersbvi.com, +1 (284) 441-0000 — and make clear water taxis must be booked ahead, scheduled to their flights.
  • Tell every arriving guest to pre-check-in — call Maria at (410) 570-1974 before boarding the ferry. Taxi drivers wait at the Great Harbour dock to bring guests up for check-in.
  • Never mention Aquatic Rentals — permanently closed as of September 1.
  • Know the holiday rule — all villas island-wide require a 7-night minimum stay over Christmas/New Year's (Foxy's "Old Years Night" is the bucket-list draw).
  • Pitch the sunset dinner — Alice's is the only restaurant on White Bay Beach with a sunset view. Check the sunset table for timing and share the OpenTable link to book.
  • Verify occupancy on Sunset Villa East when multiple adults book — confirm who's sleeping where before arrival (living-room sleeper has no A/C).
  • Verify guest counts on Palm Villa OTA bookings — Airbnb imports can show the wrong occupancy. Confirm adults, children, and totals.
  • Pitch grocery provisioning on every booking — order 5–7 days ahead from Riteway or Bobby's ($50 minimum), 1:00 PM ferry on arrival day, and the guest forwards the store confirmation to maria@ and info@jostvandyke.com in writing so it's added to the reservation.
  • Recommend WhatsApp before travel — it's how the island communicates.
  • Hold the Paradise Villas occupancy line — 3 adults max (or 2 + 2 kids), and explain the living-room A/C reason honestly.

Guest Packing List

Copy-paste ready for pre-arrival emails — with the why, so the team can answer follow-up questions.

  • Reusable insulated water bottles — tap water is filtered, potable, and delicious, and every villa fridge has a Brita pitcher. There's no recycling in the Caribbean, so this keeps plastic off the island.
  • Reef-safe sunscreen — required in the Virgin Islands. No chemical sunscreens; they're extremely damaging to the reefs, corals, and sea life.
  • Personal hygiene products — shampoo, conditioner, soap, lotion. Pack them in, take the plastic back out.
  • Swimsuits, beach cover-up or rash guard, sunglasses, and a hat
  • Comfortable, sturdy shoes — for walking and hiking, plus flip-flops or sandals ("flip-flops & flats are where it's at in the islands!") and water shoes.
  • Lightweight clothing
  • Bug spray — some is provided in the villas; guests may prefer their own brand or a natural alternative like coconut or lemongrass oil.
  • Cell phones & U.S. chargers — plus a backup travel charger for long beach days. Villas have U.S. 110V plugs, so no adapters needed.
  • Prescription medications
  • Passport
  • Travel insurance documents (if applicable)
