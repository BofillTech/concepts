A Family Story, Two Generations Deep

In 1998, Jay and Jennifer Yilmaz opened the doors of what was then called Jacaranda House at 8 Holbrook Street. By 2001, their vision had grown — they expanded with a second building and renamed the inn Applewood, a nod to Maine’s heritage and the warmth of home.

Today, Applewood Inn stands as a rare gem in Freeport: a Victorian bed and breakfast where history lives in the architecture, where the Great Room’s cherry harvest table — handcrafted in Maine and seating 26 — becomes the center of morning conversation, and where guests discover what it means to stay somewhere that feels less like a hotel and more like a gracious friend’s home.

Jay greets you at check-in. Abby and Lilly, the family dogs, might wag a welcome. And when you step out the front door, you’re already in the heart of one of New England’s most beloved villages.

In the Heart of Freeport

Applewood Inn sits on Holbrook Street, just 250 to 300 yards from L.L. Bean’s flagship store and the entrance to Freeport’s historic village — home to 170+ shops and outlets.

Beyond shopping, you’re minutes from Maine’s natural treasures: Bradbury Mountain State Park, Wolfe’s Neck Woods, Winslow Memorial Park, and the South Freeport waterfront. Portland is just 20 minutes south.
