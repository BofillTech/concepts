Welcome to Dutchland Motel

Discover the scenic beauty of Lancaster County at Dutchland Motel, where clean, friendly, and welcoming accommodations meet affordable comfort. Whether you're a business traveler, a family on vacation, or exploring the picturesque Amish countryside, our motel offers the perfect base for your Lancaster adventures.

Located on Lititz Pike in the heart of Lancaster, we provide convenient access to the area's most beloved attractions, from historic downtown Lancaster to the rolling farmlands of Amish Country.
