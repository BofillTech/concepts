Your High Sierra Basecamp

Well known among High Sierra vacation resorts for exceptional four-season outdoor activities, Sierra Woods Lodge stands out as the clear favorite for adventure enthusiasts or for those just interested in a leisurely yet luxurious stay in the mountains.

California's newest "Wild and Scenic River" is minutes away. The historic towns of Truckee and North Lake Tahoe ski resorts are just over Donner Pass. Whether you're here for world-class fly fishing, skiing, hiking, or simply to enjoy the mountain air, our lodge provides the perfect home base.

Getting Here

We're easily accessible from Interstate 80, about 70 miles from Sacramento and 20 minutes from Lake Tahoe. The historic town of Truckee and North Lake Tahoe ski resorts are just over Donner Pass.
