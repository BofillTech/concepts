Penthouse Suite

Two bedrooms with sweeping ocean views

Watch the waves from the kitchen

Our newest two-bedroom suite offers sweeping views of the ocean and pool area.

Located on the third floor of our main house, this suite offers a king-size bed in the master bedroom and a queen-size bed in the second bedroom. There is also a queen sleeper in the living area and a full bath. The Penthouse Suite can accommodate up to 6 people comfortably.

Imagine cooking in the kitchen while watching the waves lap along the coast as you breathe in the fresh ocean air. On cooler evenings, enjoy the gas fireplace while watching the waves or snuggling up with a good book.

There are two private balconies where you can enjoy a morning cup of coffee while watching the sun rise, or wind down in the afternoon with your favorite drink. Includes daily housekeeping.
