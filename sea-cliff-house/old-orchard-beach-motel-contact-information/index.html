Getting here

Only 2 hours from Boston, 5 hours from Montreal and Quebec City, and 5½ hours from New York City. We are easy to reach from all areas north and south, in the heart of Old Orchard Beach, Maine.

From the south

Take I-95 north into Maine (it becomes the Maine Turnpike). Take Exit 36 for Saco and Old Orchard Beach. Go through the tollbooth and continue on I-195; the highway becomes a residential road. At the fork (mini rotary), continue straight or bear right onto Temple Avenue, keeping the 7-Eleven gas station on your left. Stay on Temple Avenue for 1.2 miles to the four-way stop in the center of Ocean Park. Turn left onto Route 9 (West Grand Avenue), go 0.5 miles, and turn right onto Sea Cliff Avenue.

From the north

Take Exit 36 off the Maine Turnpike (I-95) and follow the directions above.

From Portland International Jetport

Turn left onto Congress Street and continue straight through the next light to Johnson Street, heading toward Maine Mall Road. Stay on this road as it becomes Payne Road and meets Route 1. Turn right onto Route 1. After about 3 miles, just past the Cascade Restaurant on your left, turn left at the light onto Route 98. At the three-way stop in the center of town, turn left down the hill toward the Pier. Just before the Pier, turn right at Palace Playland, keeping the ocean on your left. After the second stoplight, we are the second street on the left: turn left onto Sea Cliff Avenue and we are on your left, on the beach.
