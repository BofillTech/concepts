Enjoy a quiet section of Old Orchard Beach

Walk out of your room directly onto the sand. We sit on a peaceful stretch of beach near Ocean Park, a ten-minute walk along the sand or West Grand Avenue to the Pier, amusements, dining and shopping downtown.

Our property features an outdoor heated pool, a hot tub and a barbecue picnic area with gas grills and umbrella tables. The Sea Cliff House is family owned and managed by the Pelletier family, and we are open all year.

  • Outdoor heated pool overlooking the dunes
  • Hot tub seasonal, adults only
  • Grill & picnic area gas grills, umbrella tables
  • Ten minutes on foot to the Pier

Kitchenettes, ocean view rooms, whirlpool rooms and specialty suites
