The Grand Suite

The ultimate in beachfront luxury

Once the owners’ own home

Our two-bedroom, two-and-a-half-bath suite on the main level of the oceanfront building was once occupied by the owners of the Sea Cliff House.

This luxury, condominium-like apartment has daily maid service and oceanfront views. It offers all of the comforts of home, yet provides the perfect vacation getaway.

The open-concept gourmet kitchen, dining room and living room all offer serene luxury with breathtaking oceanfront views. A sliding glass door leads from the kitchen to an intimate, private deck with an arbor, surrounded by perennial gardens, with outdoor furniture and a gas grill (seasonal).

Kitchen and dining

The eat-in gourmet kitchen features maple floors, granite countertops, custom-designed cabinets and all of the amenities the cook in the family needs. You will also find a washer and dryer hidden inside specially designed kitchen cabinets.

The dining room also features maple floors and a remote-controlled gas fireplace, and seats 8 to 10 people. It leads through an archway to the newly carpeted living room.

Bedrooms and decks

The master bedroom has a king-size bed, a sitting area and a full bath. The second bedroom also has a full bath, plus two queen-size beds. The Grand Suite sleeps up to 10 people.

The entertainment and day room porch has another sliding glass door to a second private deck for relaxing or sunbathing. This deck overlooks the pool and hot tub and is next to the outdoor showers and bathhouse.

Let the sound of the ocean waves soothe and relax you. We want this to be your “beach house” for the week and your home away from home.

Grand Suite rates include

  • Use of the beachfront pool (seasonal)
  • Use of the hot tub (seasonal)
  • Poolside gas grills, chairs and umbrella tables
  • Free parking in our private lots
  • HDTV with Showtime
