Whirlpool Tub Rooms

Nearest the beach, beside the pool and hot tub

Closest to the sand

Our four whirlpool tub rooms are located nearest the beach and adjacent to the pool and hot tub. These rooms all offer private decks and ocean views.

Three of these rooms (2, 3 and 4) have a queen bed and can accommodate 4 guests. Room 1 sits next to the sand dunes with a stunning three-sided ocean view. With a queen bed and a table with two chairs, it accommodates 2 guests.

All of these rooms feature individually controlled heat, air conditioning, wireless high-speed internet, cable TV, refrigerator, microwave, coffee maker, a bathroom with a shower, and deck chairs.
