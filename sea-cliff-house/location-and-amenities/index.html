Amenities & location

Heated pool, hot tub and miles of beach

Walk straight onto the sand

At Sea Cliff House you walk directly to a beach entrance onto miles of sand. Our serene section of beach is a picturesque, wide stretch that runs for miles in either direction, an ideal Old Orchard Beach vacation on the Maine coast.

You can walk along the beach or the road to the excitement of the Pier, amusements and restaurants, or to the quiet Ocean Park section of Old Orchard Beach.

Relax outside your room on a private or shared deck, depending on the room you choose. Many decks have ocean views. The shared decks on our efficiency rooms are breezy and pleasant, with seating outside each room.

On the property

  • Outdoor heated swimming pool
  • Hot tub (adults only)*
  • Barbecue picnic pavilion with gas grills
  • Umbrella tables around the pool
  • Private and shared decks
  • Outdoor showers and bathhouse
  • Free private parking
  • Wireless high-speed internet

*Hot tub notice: due to safety concerns about children and babies in water above 98°F, only adults may use the hot tub. Guests under 18 are welcome to enjoy our heated swimming pool.
