Honeymoon Suite

A direct ocean view from two private balconies

Third-floor views, fireplace glow

Our Honeymoon Suite is located on the third floor of our main house and offers one bedroom with a king-size bed.

There is a queen sleeper in the living area, along with a gas fireplace, full kitchen and full bath. The Honeymoon Suite can accommodate 4 guests comfortably.

This fully appointed suite offers a direct ocean view and two private balconies. Just bring your clothing and sundries and leave the rest to us. Includes daily housekeeping.
