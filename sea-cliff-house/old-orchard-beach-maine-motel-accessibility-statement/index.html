Accessibility statement

On-site accessibility: Sea Cliff House does not have handicapped-accessible accommodations available. If you have any questions about accessibility, please contact us at 207-934-4874 or info@seacliffhouse.com.

Website accessibility

We are committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards and best practices.

Conformance status

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) define requirements for designers and developers to improve accessibility for people with disabilities, with three levels of conformance: Level A, Level AA and Level AAA. Our goal is WCAG 2.1 Level AA conformance.

We have installed an automatic accessibility tool that runs in the background, using AI and machine learning to fix accessibility issues in the page code. It also provides an interface that lets visitors adjust their browsing experience.

Additional considerations

Although our goal is WCAG 2.1 Level AA conformance, we have also applied some Level AAA success criteria: images of text are only used for decorative purposes. Some Level AA requirements fall outside the scope of the website code and are not included, including but not limited to video captions.

Measures taken

  • Complete WCAG 2.1 Level AA website audit and remediation on page load
  • Automatic image and link recognition for content missing alt or title text
  • Quick access to dictionary definitions and pronunciations
  • Colorblindness adjustments for red-green, blue-yellow and other color blindness
  • Option to reset fonts to Arial or a dyslexia-friendly font
  • Highlighting of titles and links
  • Text magnification
  • Content, font, line height, line spacing and zoom scaling
  • Content alignment: left, center and right
  • Dark, light and high-contrast modes, and full color inversion
  • Saturation adjustments, including monochrome
  • Ability to stop motion, hide images and mute sounds
  • Reading guide and page mask to help focus
  • Highlighting of elements on hover or focus
  • A screen reader that reads back highlighted content
  • Complete keyboard navigation
  • Saved browsing profiles for quick setup

Compatibility

We strive to be compatible with Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Apple Safari, Opera and Microsoft Edge, and with the JAWS and NVDA screen readers, on both Windows and Mac. This site relies on HTML, WAI-ARIA, CSS and JavaScript.

Notes

While our goal is to make the entire site accessible to all users, some pages may not yet have a satisfactory accessibility solution. We are committed to constantly improving the accessibility of this site. If you run into a barrier, please contact us at 207-934-4874 or info@seacliffhouse.com.
