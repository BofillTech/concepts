Ocean View Rooms

Old Orchard Beach ocean view motel rooms

Wake up to the Atlantic

Our ocean front rooms are located in the main house. These rooms offer sweeping ocean views with private or common decks, or side ocean views and decks.

For your additional convenience, they also have their own refrigerator, microwave and coffee maker. Of course they have individually controlled heat, air conditioning (not that we need it here on the ocean in Maine), wireless high-speed internet, HBO and deck chairs.

Our whirlpool tub rooms also have ocean views.
