Two-Room Kitchenettes

Our most popular accommodation

The best value at Sea Cliff House

The kitchenette is the best value at the Sea Cliff House. Although you will not have an ocean view from a kitchenette, you are only steps away from our beautiful beach.

We offer first- and second-floor units, and guests share the deck areas. There are comfortable chairs on the decks to catch a few rays of sunshine or enjoy your morning coffee.

The living and kitchen areas and the bathrooms have ceramic tile, and the bedrooms are carpeted. There is a table in the kitchenette area with four chairs.

Each two-room kitchenette has a bedroom with either a king or queen bed (depending on availability), and the living area offers a futon that opens into a full-size mattress. Our kitchenettes comfortably accommodate 4 to 5 guests. If required, we can furnish an air mattress. Wi-Fi and nationwide calling are both complimentary.
