Ocean Park

Old Orchard Beach’s quieter side

A quaint seaside village, right down the beach

When most people think of Old Orchard Beach, they think of the festive atmosphere around the Pier. Few realize that just 0.7 miles south of the Pier is a quiet seaside community directly on the beach, and a place to stay called the Sea Cliff House.

Less than half a mile south of the Sea Cliff House is Ocean Park, a classic seaside vacation village with old-world charm. The original soda fountain in the heart of town still serves homemade ice cream, as it has for over 100 years.

Ocean Park welcomes the public to enjoy movies and concerts, the seasonal library, Recreation Hall, clay tennis courts and shuffleboard courts. All of Ocean Park is a State of Maine game preserve, with walking trails through towering cathedral pines.

Ferry Beach and Camp Ellis

Just down the road from Ocean Park is Ferry Beach State Park, with its scenic boardwalk and remote beach. Beyond that, at the mouth of the Saco River, sits Camp Ellis, a classic Maine fishing village with shops and restaurants. Its half-mile breakwater is an excellent place to fish for stripers, and deep-sea fishing, whale watches and river cruises depart daily from the Camp Ellis docks.

Pine Point

Just 3 miles north of the Pier is the quiet vacation community of Pine Point, with gift shops, restaurants and lobster pounds at the mouth of the Scarborough River. It is also home to the Scarborough Marsh nature preserve, the largest saltwater marsh in Maine, where you can take a guided canoe trip and sneak up on cranes and other birds.

Enjoy an Old Orchard Beach vacation at the Sea Cliff House, a vacation on the quieter side.
