Reservation policies

What to know before you book

All room rates include

  • Use of the beachfront pool (seasonal)
  • Use of the hot tub (seasonal)
  • Poolside gas grills, chairs and umbrella tables
  • Free parking in our private lots
  • Wireless high-speed internet

Limited housekeeping is available. Towel exchange is available in the office daily. Sheet changes during stays are available on request. Housekeeping is available on request for guests staying 7 days or more.

General policies

  • Check-in time: 4:00 pm. Check-out time: 11:00 am.
  • Rates are based on 2 people per unit; $10 per day per extra person. Children 12 and under sleep free.
  • The Grand Suite rate is based on 4 people; $20 per day per extra person.
  • The credit card and reservation holder must be present at check-in.
  • You must be 21 to reserve a room, and present a valid government-issued photo ID (for example, a driver’s license or passport) and the card used to make the reservation. The name on the reservation must match the name on the card, and only the person named on the reservation can check in.
  • Early check-in requests need at least 24 hours’ notice and depend on availability.
  • Please call the front office before check-in time if you may arrive late. If we do not hear from you, your reservation could be cancelled or incur additional charges, regardless of weather, illness or other circumstances.
  • Late check-out requests need at least 24 hours’ notice, depend on availability, and may carry additional charges.
  • We do not accept bookings for group events or parties, including bachelor and bachelorette parties.

Holidays and minimum stays

  • Memorial Day and Columbus Day weekends: 3-night minimum.
  • Fourth of July, Labor Day and Columbus Day weekends: 3-day minimum.
  • Late June to early September: 3-night minimum stay.

Deposit, cancellation and payment

The first night’s stay is required in full at the time of reservation. A confirmation will be emailed or mailed once we receive your deposit.

  • Arrivals May 1 to October 11: cancel at least 14 days before arrival for a full refund, minus a $25 cancellation fee.
  • Arrivals October 12 to April 30: 48-hour cancellation. Cancellations within 48 hours of arrival forfeit the first night’s deposit; cancellations made earlier are free.

Grand Suite cancellation (June to September)

10% is non-refundable for any reason. If you cancel at least 30 days before your scheduled arrival, your payment will be refunded less a cancellation fee equal to 10% of the rental rate. If you cancel within 30 days of arrival, payments will not be refunded unless we are able to re-rent the suite; if re-rented at a lower rate, refunds will be adjusted accordingly. See the Grand Suite page for details.

Payment

Rates apply to reservations made 48 hours in advance. Payment in full is required on arrival, by credit card (Visa, Mastercard or Discover) or cash. Sorry, no personal checks. We do not offer refunds for any reason, including shorter stays, bad weather or illness.

Security deposit

All reservations are subject to a minimum $100 damage and theft deposit. As long as no policies are broken, including no smoking, and nothing is missing or damaged, it will be refunded within 3 to 6 business days of check-out.

Visitors

Visitors of guests who use the pool are charged $20 per person per day.

Pets

Sorry, no pets. We do permit service dogs, which are working dogs trained to provide assistance directly related to a person’s disability. Dogs whose sole function is comfort or emotional support do not qualify as service animals under the Americans with Disabilities Act or the Maine Human Rights Act.
