Your Mountain Basecamp

Sweetwater Lift Lodge offers affordable convenience in an unbeatable location at the base of Park City Mountain Resort. Step out of your suite and onto the slopes in minutes, or take a short stroll to historic Main Street for world-class dining and shopping.

Our clean, family-friendly accommodations feature full kitchenettes perfect for extended stays. After a day on the mountain, relax in our heated indoor pool or covered hot tub. With comfortable suites, convenient amenities, and ski-in/ski-out access, Sweetwater Lift Lodge is your ideal Park City retreat.

Whether you're hitting the slopes in winter or exploring mountain trails in summer, experience Park City in comfort and convenience year-round.
