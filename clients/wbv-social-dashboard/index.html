      How these numbers are counted
      • Reach — unique accounts that saw each post published in the month, added together (the same method as the monthly agency report).
      • Instagram engagements — all likes, comments, shares and saves the account received during the month. Facebook interactions — reactions, comments and shares on posts published that month.
      • Engagement rate — each post's engagements divided by its reach, averaged across the month's posts.
      • New followers — Instagram's daily new-follower count (Instagram only keeps 30 days, so this dashboard stores each day as it is pulled) and Facebook's daily Page follows.
      • Instagram link taps and profile visits are not available through Windsor.ai; they are entered from the agency's monthly report (Meta Business Suite) and marked as such.
      • Facebook profile visits — Page views. Facebook link clicks — link clicks on posts published that month.
      • Month to date: when a month isn't finished, changes are compared with the same days of the previous month.
      • Numbers can differ slightly from the agency report because post totals keep growing after a report is written.
