Heritage

Built by a family who's been at this for 50 years.

The Hedeen family has been building quality vacation homes in Door County for more than five decades — Ephraim Yacht Harbor, Winding Brook, The Rushes, and Little Sweden all carry their name. Every villa here stays true to its Swedish design roots: clean lines, a soaring cathedral ceiling, and an authentic Door County fieldstone fireplace waiting just inside the front door.

A four-seasons porch was built into every home to bring the outdoors in, because nature mattered to the Swedes as much as it matters here. Deer and wild turkeys still wander past the door — the 89-acre bluff at the center of the property was left as close to untouched as a golf course and four miles of trails allow.

The Good Life

More to do than you'll fit into one stay.

Golf & Trails

A PGA-recognized nine-hole course climbs the bluff with a different view from every tee, connected to four miles of groomed private trails for hiking in summer and cross-country skiing in winter.

Pools & Hot Tubs

An indoor pool, an outdoor pool, and spacious whirlpool hot tubs — open to every guest, every season of the year.

Courts & Rec Center

Tennis and pickleball courts, plus a recreation center that lends out sports equipment at no charge — nothing to pack, nothing to rent.

Concierge, Year-Round

A 7-days-a-week concierge team and a reception center open 9 a.m. to 9 p.m., 365 days a year — the good life doesn't take a season off.

Visit

Secluded on the bluff, minutes from everything.

Little Sweden sits between the villages of Fish Creek and Egg Harbor — a short mile north or two miles south for Door County's shopping, dining, and cherry and apple orchards. The Peninsula Players, the oldest summer stock theater in the country, and Peninsula State Park, the largest state park in Wisconsin, are both close by, along with Birch Creek Music Center and the Door Community Auditorium.
