The House

A private home of 1865, opened to guests for over 38 years

T.C. Smith Historic Inn was built in 1865 as a private home, and stands today as one of Lake Geneva's most exquisite bed and breakfast inns. For over 38 years, our family has graciously opened its doors to guests who seek the luxurious warmth and ambiance of the Grand Victorian era.

Noted for outstanding architectural, historical, and artistic significance, the house is a fine example of pre–Civil War Lake Geneva — its exterior blending Greek Revival and Italianate elements in High Victorian style. Inside, authentic antiques fill every room: oil paintings, fine porcelain, and a collection of antique furniture that makes each stay an extraordinary experience.

Each guest room features a fireplace and is decorated with fine 19th-century furnishings in keeping with the historical home.

Every Morning

“The best thing about T.C. Smith is the personal experience — especially the lavish breakfast.”

Enjoy the Inn's hot entrée buffet-style breakfast at your chosen time between 7:30 and 10:30 am — fresh coffees and teas, eight fruit selections each day, homemade pastries, coffeecake and breads, and at least one hot egg and meat entrée, individually prepared and served to the guests of each room.

Vegetarian, gluten-free, low-fat, and restrictive menus are happily accommodated, along with afternoon tea, picnic lunches, and late-night snacks. Breakfast is served in the dining room, the summer & fall garden, or brought to your room.
