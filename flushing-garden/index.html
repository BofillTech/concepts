The Idea

A garden name, in the middle of the busiest blocks in Queens.

Flushing's Chinatown doesn't slow down — the food stalls, the markets on Main Street, the crowds heading for the 7 train. Flushing Garden Hotel sits right inside that energy, five stories of glass and brick that went up in 2023, a short walk from Amore Pizzeria and the block's late-night diners.

Inside, it's a different pace. Every room has soundproofed windows, air conditioning, and a kitchenette for guests who'd rather cook than order in. Families get a playground on site; business travelers get a 24-hour desk and a business center; everyone gets free parking, which is not a small thing in this part of Queens.
