The Oregon Coast Awaits

Miles of beach are just steps from your room. Hunt for agates, explore tide pools, fly a kite, or take a sunset stroll. Watch for sea lions, bald eagles, and seasonal whale sightings.

Lincoln City offers charming shops, local restaurants, and year-round coastal attractions. Our friendly staff is dedicated to ensuring your stay is unforgettable.
