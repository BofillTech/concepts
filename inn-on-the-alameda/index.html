Southwestern comfort

A quiet inn wrapped in trees, a short walk from everything.

Our guest rooms sit in smaller adobe buildings shaded by mature trees, and snug pathways lead to casitas with their own brick patios. Many suites have a kiva fireplace for cool high-desert nights.

Most of Santa Fe is within walking distance: museums, galleries, cafes and the 400-year-old streets of the Plaza. The ski basin and the Sangre de Cristo Mountains are 20 minutes away.

On site

Joe's Tequila Bar

Named for our founder and long-time owner, Joe Schepps. By day it's a bright, intimate cafe for breakfast and lunch. By night, choose from more than 250 tequilas, mezcals and sotols, poured neat, in flights or in margaritas made with fresh-squeezed lime.

Pan-American cooking made in house every day: carne asada, chipotle shrimp, red chile roasted chicken and pollo asado.

Open daily, 11:30 AM to 9:00 PM
