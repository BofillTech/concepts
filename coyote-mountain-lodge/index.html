Where the Rockies Meet Mid-Century Modern

49 inviting rooms in Estes Park, Colorado — minutes from Rocky Mountain National Park

A Mid-Century Vibe That Brings the Outside In

With views of the Rockies and 49 very inviting rooms, Coyote Mountain Lodge inspires healthy vacationing with a comfortably heated pool. Take in golf, cross-country skiing, a stroll through downtown Estes Park — or simply do nothing but relax.

Re-energize and relax at the same lodge where elk play in Estes Park. Our signature A-frame glass lobby anchors a collection of thoughtfully updated rooms and suites, each designed to frame the peaks beyond.

Lodge Amenities

Everything you need to unwind in the Colorado Rockies

Seasonal Heated Pool

Our kidney-shaped outdoor pool is comfortably heated and open May 29 through September 4. Surrounded by tall spruces with mountain peaks as your backdrop — this is Colorado pool life at its finest.

Mountain-View Decks

Settle into a patio chair on one of our shared decks and watch the light shift over the Continental Divide. Perfect for morning coffee or evening conversation under the pines.

A-Frame Glass Lobby

Our signature mid-century A-frame lobby brings the outside in. Floor-to-ceiling glass walls, wood-plank ceilings, and comfortable seating make it the heart of the lodge. Borrow books and games for your room.

Hot Tubs & Fire Pit

Soak under the stars in our hot tubs (open until 10 PM in summer, 7 PM off-season). Gather around the outdoor fire pit or grill your catch of the day on our charcoal and propane BBQ areas.

Dog-Friendly

Up to 3 dogs welcome ($20 + tax per dog, per night). Fast and friendly service for all guests and their dogs.

Morning Coffee

Complimentary hot coffee, hot chocolate, and teas served 7–10 AM in the lobby.

Free WiFi & Parking

High-speed fiber internet throughout the property. Ample free outdoor parking for all guests.

Groups & Events

Meeting spaces available for reunions, corporate retreats, and small weddings. Outdoor gazebo at sister property Discovery Lodge.

Estes Park & Rocky Mountain National Park

Our lodge sits just minutes from Rocky Mountain National Park — one of America's most spectacular alpine landscapes.

Downtown Estes Park

Stroll Main Street's shops, cafes, and restaurants. Discover galleries, outfitters, and local flavors in this charming mountain town.

Rocky Mountain Adventures

Hiking and climbing trails for all abilities. Fishing, horseback riding, kayaking. Cross-country skiing and snowshoeing in winter. Wildlife watching year-round — elk frequent our meadows.

Easy Access

36.4 miles northwest of Boulder. Front desk open 7 AM–7 PM off-season, 7 AM–10 PM peak summer. Check-in after 4 PM, check-out 11 AM.
