Welcome to Adventure Suites

Created with a cup of fun, a pound of adventure, a bunch of love, and a sprinkle of magic, Adventure Suites offers themed experiences to suit everyone. We are one of the top ten theme hotels in the world and the #1 hotel in the Mount Washington Valley.

Adventure Suites is a one-of-a-kind property—we are not a chain and have no other locations. Our fun, themed suites combined with our love for our customers and many add-on adventures make each stay an original adventure.

From the Haunted Castle to the Treehouse, from Roman Rendezvous to the Cave, each suite is painstakingly designed with products sourced from around the world. Perfect for families, couples, and anyone seeking an unforgettable North Conway experience.
