A Hearth That Has Welcomed Travelers Since ~1820

Step through our doors and you'll walk on the same pumpkin pine floors that have creaked under two centuries of footsteps. At the heart of our historic Cape Cod inn stands the open hearth—complete with its original brick oven and iron crane—where generations of innkeepers have kindled warmth for weary travelers.

Today, as part of the Ohana Inns family, we carry forward that legacy of hospitality with a Hawaiian spirit: Ohana means family, and family means no one gets left behind or forgotten. Whether you're here to hike Cadillac Mountain, photograph Bass Harbor Light, or simply escape into coastal Maine, you'll find that same welcoming fire still burning.

Just a mile from Acadia National Park's entrance, we've grown from that original Cape Cod structure to include cottages, apartments, and lodge rooms—each offering its own blend of historic charm and modern comfort. But some things never change: the warmth of the hearth, the welcome for your pets, and the invitation to become part of our Ohana.
