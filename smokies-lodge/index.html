Welcome

Relax in our earth-inspired lobby, then go do everything else.

Grand Smokies Resort Lodge is a 160-unit condo-hotel built for families and groups who want to be in the thick of Pigeon Forge without the downtown price tag. Come stay and relax in the natural ambience of our earth-inspired lobby — recently remodeled, along with our meeting spaces.

Choose from deluxe Double Queen and King Jacuzzi units with private balconies, Queen fireplace rooms, and our new Family Suites. Adjoining units connect into a mix-and-match layout, so groups can build their own multi-room suite.
