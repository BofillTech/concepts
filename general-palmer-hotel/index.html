A Legacy of Honor & Rails

Named for General William Jackson Palmer—Civil War Medal of Honor recipient, founder of the Denver & Rio Grande Railroad, and the visionary whose railroad founded Durango, Colorado Springs, Pueblo, Salida, and Alamosa.

Built in 1898, our hotel has welcomed travelers for over a century. Today, 39 individually hand-decorated rooms preserve the elegance of the Victorian era with antiques and period replicas. No two rooms are the same.

Located in downtown Durango's National Historic District, we share the same block as the legendary Durango & Silverton Narrow Gauge Railroad. Guests enjoy watching the historic steam train from our 2nd- and 3rd-floor patio decks.
