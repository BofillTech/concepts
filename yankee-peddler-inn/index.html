A Victorian Treasure in Newport's Heart

Built in the 1800s for a Boston mariner and naval officer, our early Victorian home offers an oasis of calm, comfortable, casual charm in Newport's Historic Hill neighborhood.

Just uphill from the waterfront and downhill from Bellevue Avenue, we're perfectly positioned for exploring Newport's legendary mansions, harbor, and dining scene — while providing a peaceful retreat from the bustle.
