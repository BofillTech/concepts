Why Montauk

The quiet side of Montauk, the way it used to be.

Decades ago, this site was the Holiday Motel — where our family spent seasons fishing with local charter captains who became an extension of the family. That welcoming feeling is what drew us to Montauk in the first place.

Montauk has since become the in-spot for everyone who loves beaches, biking, swimming, dining, nature, and an active life. Our hope is that North Harbor Montauk lets you come home too — to the quiet side of Montauk, the way it used to be, with the comforts we’ve worked hard to build in.
