Stay with us in comfort

The Ocean Resort Inn is located 65 footsteps from the beach and the captivating coastline of the Atlantic Ocean in Montauk. Our bright, comfortably furnished rooms feature local ocean-inspired art, and our studios and suites have in-room spa tubs, a peaceful retreat after a day in the sun.

Golf, tennis and Montauk Downs State Park are only a five-minute drive away, and town is an easy walk.
