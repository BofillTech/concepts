Our Rooms

Named for the elements, built for unwinding

Every room at Earthbox takes its cue from the island — Water, Earth, Sun, Sky, Sea, and Wind. Each one is a little different, all comfortably Earthbox: retro-modern style, custom beds, local photography, and eco-friendly body care made nearby.

Splash, Soak & Sweat

The only hotel on San Juan Island with an indoor pool

Back from a long day of adventuring — or easing into your morning — the Earthbox pool house is the place to unwind. Take a dip, soak in the spacious jacuzzi, or warm up in the dry sauna. Open year-round, whatever the island weather is doing.

  • Heated to 85°F
  • 35 feet long
  • Jacuzzi & dry sauna
  • Open year-round

Yes, Bring the Dog

Your pup deserves a vacation too

Dog-friendly rooms are available in nearly every room type, and Friday Harbor is a pup's paradise — water bowls outside shops, dog-friendly patios, and beaches made for stick-chasing. Just let us know you're bringing your dog when you book.
