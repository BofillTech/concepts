Welcome to the Sundown

Clean, comfortable and cozy, right in the middle of town.

Leave the car and walk: the waterfront, the golf course, Morro Bay State Park and the downtown shops and restaurants are all close by. Morro Rock is about a mile away.

Every room has HBO, in-room coffee and tea, a refrigerator, a microwave, A/C and the classic Magic Fingers bed massage. ADA accessible and family rooms are available on request.

Morro Bay

Sunset at the Rock

Walk two blocks down to the Embarcadero for chowder, harbor views and sea otters, then follow the water out to Morro Rock as the sun goes down.

Hearst Castle is about 30 minutes north, and Montaña de Oro, Cayucos and San Luis Obispo are all an easy drive.
