A wilderness sanctuary, not a subdivision

Snowcreek sits against the Eastern Sierra with panoramic views of Mammoth Mountain, Mammoth Rock, and the Sherwin range. It was built into the land rather than onto it — a meandering creek, aspen groves, and open meadow run through the village the same way they ran through this valley before any of it was built.

Guests staying in any of the resort’s vacation rentals get complimentary run of what the village was built around: a Ted Robinson golf course, a 30,000-square-foot athletic club, and miles of hiking and biking trails that start at the back door.

Seven phases, one creek running through all of them

Snowcreek grew in phases over the decades, each with its own character — from the fairway homes along the golf course to Creekhouse, the resort’s newest neighborhood set directly on Mammoth Creek.

Mammoth’s original golf course

The Alpine Course, designed by Ted Robinson, is a public 9-hole, par-35 layout playing 3,306 yards from the back tees at 8,000 feet of elevation — with streams crossing seven of the nine holes and some of the fastest greens in the Eastern Sierra.

Snowcreek guests receive discounted greens fees (seasonal). Book a tee time →

A 30,000-square-foot athletic club, included

Five tennis courts, a racquetball court, indoor and outdoor pools, steam rooms, hot tubs, a full gymnasium, and the Double Eagle Spa — complimentary for every Snowcreek vacation rental guest during their stay.
