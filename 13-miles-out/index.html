The View

Graceful porches, commanding views.

Comfortable rooms and porches that look out in every direction — over the rooftops, across the Great Salt Pond, and out to the open Atlantic. The intersection of peace and tranquility, as we’ve always thought of it.

Crescent Beach

A beach that keeps
making the lists.

Both Coastal Living and Elle have named Crescent Beach among the best beach destinations in the country. From The Beach House it is a short walk; from The MacGregor, the town beach is just across the street.

Our Story

Our home, your home away.

The MacGregor and The Beach House have been family owned and operated as summer rental properties since 1907. Michael O’Brien took them on in 2016, and his commitment has been to service excellence — restoring and renovating both houses so they keep their long history while gaining modern conveniences.

We really appreciate our repeat guests, and we thank them for continuing to return year after year. To our new guests: we hope you enjoy many happy returns too.
