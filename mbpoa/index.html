MWhy the association exists

Formed so the deed to the Ocean View Beach could be held forever.

The MBPOA was formed so that the deed to the Ocean View Beach could be transferred to our association. The association and its members own — and are responsible for — the designated area, its cliffs and vegetation, as well as the MBPOA parking lots for members' use. We also maintain the pathways leading to the beach from Old Montauk Highway.

We are a social organization where members gather on the beach and at sponsored activities. Our dues allow us to maintain the property and advocate for our homeowners. We love our beach and hope you do too.

MOne mile of oceanfront

Know your beach — every entrance, lot, and project.

The MBPOA coastline runs roughly one mile along Old Montauk Highway. Nine numbered beach entrances (the Orange M signs), four member parking lots, and the SUNY marine erosion study sites are mapped below — tap any marker for details. In an emergency, give rescue services the entrance number on the nearest Orange M sign.

The coastline at a glance

  • 1M – 9M — nine numbered beach entrances with Orange M location signs
  • P1 – P4 — four MBPOA member parking lots (locks & keys via the association)
  • S1 – S2 — SUNY marine erosion study sites along the bluffs
  • Pathways — maintained steps and paths from Old Montauk Highway to the sand

MSince 1942

From the "Miami Beach of the North" to a beach held in trust.

Carl Fisher's original plan for our corner of Montauk was an 18-hole golf course — part of his vision to make Montauk the "Miami Beach of the North." The Great Depression ended that dream, and the land passed through the Montauk Beach Development Corporation and the Mirror Holding Corporation, which sold 100' × 25' lots for $100 each.

On July 15, 1942, the newly incorporated MBPOA took title to what was then known as the Ocean View Beach. The association once owned the Playhouse below Montauk Manor, and twice nearly lost the beach itself — at one point almost transferring the property to the Town of East Hampton. The deal was cancelled just before it closed. Our dues have kept the beach ours ever since.

We are the only homeowners in Montauk with access to our own privately deeded ocean beach — right down the street.
