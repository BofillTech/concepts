MMission & history

A safe, enjoyable beach — held in trust since 1942.

Our mission is to afford a safe and enjoyable beach experience for all, to preserve and protect the environment we love, and to bring members together. The association took title to the Ocean View Beach on July 15, 1942, and our dues have kept it ours ever since.

We are the only homeowners in Montauk with access to our own privately deeded ocean beach — right down the street.

MMembers & guests

Beach rules & party guidelines.

Leave no trace. No animals on the beach 10 AM–6 PM, May 15–September 15. Fires in a metal container, out by midnight. Parking lots need a valid, visible MBPOA permit. Planning a party? Read the permit and insurance rules first.

Look for the Orange M.

Orange M Beach Location Signs stand at the top and bottom of all beach paths and parking lots. Use the sign nearest you to relay your location to Public Safety.
