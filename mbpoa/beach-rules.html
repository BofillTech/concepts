Beach Rules & Party Guidelines

For members and their guests. Please share these rules with anyone using your MBPOA property.

Beach rules

  1. Trash. Take all your items and trash back home with you (“Leave No Trace”).
  2. Dogs and animals. Animals are not allowed on the beach from 10 AM to 6 PM, May 15 to September 15. Bag dog waste and put it only in the designated parking lot receptacles.
  3. Fires. Keep fires in a metal container at least 50 feet from beach grass, vegetation, and staircases. Put them out by midnight and fully extinguish them with water. The Town of East Hampton’s Beach Fire Regulations apply.
  4. Cliffs and beach grass. Do not climb or play on the cliffs or the rocks at their base, and do not harm or damage them. Keep beach blankets, chairs, umbrellas, equipment, and structures off the beach grass and away from the cliff base. Do not drive or park vehicles within 10 feet of the grass or cliffs. Members are planting grass near the bluffs, and donations support this work.
  5. Parking lots. Only cars with a valid, visible MBPOA permit may park there. Secure the chain lock when entering or leaving. Overnight parking is prohibited year-round. Commercial vehicles are never allowed.
  6. Enforcement. Breaking these rules may prevent a property owner’s membership from being renewed. Members must share these rules with anyone using their MBPOA property.

Parties and gatherings

  • Jurisdiction. MBPOA owns the beach above the Mean High-Water (debris) line.
  • Permits. A Town of East Hampton Special Events permit is required if any of these apply: 20 or more guests, catered service (including drop-offs to the beach or parking lots), or structures on the beach or parking lots (including chairs and wedding arches or chuppahs). MBPOA also requires its own authorization if any of these apply. Events held in the parking lots always need MBPOA authorization.
  • Guest limits. A catered beach event or wedding ceremony on the beach is capped at 50 attendees.
  • Insurance. Member hosts and their renters need Special Event Insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence, naming MBPOA as an additional insured and including Host Liquor Liability. This applies whether or not alcohol is served. Certificates are due two weeks before the event.
  • Music. Amplified music is not allowed.
  • Vehicles. Commercial vehicles are never allowed in the parking lots.
  • Responsibility. The responsible member pays for any damage to MBPOA property and for cleanup.
  • Enforcement. Violations may prevent membership renewal.

Reserving your party

Members in good standing need MBPOA authorization before the event. Email MBPOAINC@gmail.com with the event date, the location, whether the event is for renters, and your insurance documents attached. Please confirm that you have read the Beach Rules. We will reply by email.
