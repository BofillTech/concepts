Resources

Town information, emergency alerts, local organizations and helpful hints.

Community resources

  • Register to vote in Montauk
    Encourages homeowners to register to vote in Montauk, where each vote carries more weight than in larger towns. Registration needs to be completed 30 days before an election.
  • Local government contact
    The East Hampton Town website, with information on departments, finances, permits, town code, and archived Town Board meetings and work sessions.

Need to know

  • Beach fires
    Town code for beach fires. Unattended fires and fires left burning overnight are prohibited. The code also covers fire size, distance from cliffs, use of a metal fire pit, permitted materials, and keeping a water bucket nearby.
  • Excessive noise
    Explains the 7:00 PM limit on excessive noise under Chapter 185 of the town code. Contact the homeowner or guests first, then call the Montauk Police Precinct if the problem continues. Please allow reasonable everyday noise.
  • Dog restrictions
    From May 15 to September 15, dogs are not allowed on beaches from 10 AM to 6 PM (Town Code chapter 91, Section 4L). The restrictions lift in the off-season. Owners must keep dogs under control and take waste bags with them, not leaving them on the beach or at path tops.
  • House numbers must be visible from the road
    Town law requires 4-inch address numbers that are visible from the road, to support emergency response.

East Hampton alerts

  • Emergency alert sign-up
    Receive emergency and severe weather alerts by text, email, pager, or voice mail. Not meant for routine information.
  • Special needs emergency contact list
    For people who need extra help during an evacuation to submit contact details to the Human Services Department.

Community organizations

  • Concerned Citizens of Montauk
    Active since 1970, working to protect Montauk’s environment and character through education, advocacy, and citizen action.
  • Montauk Library
    Books, technology, and cultural and educational programs for the community.
  • Grouper laws (rental occupancy)
    No more than four unrelated people may rent a house, and no more than four cars may be parked there. Also covers single-family zoning and rental frequency and duration limits under Chapter 195. Report violations to East Hampton Code Enforcement at (631) 354-3858.
  • Ordinance issues complaint form
    An online form on the town website for reporting ordinance violations.
  • Montauk Chamber of Commerce
    A visitor’s guide to Montauk’s hotels, restaurants, and activities.

Helpful hints

  • Ticks
    Guidance on protecting yourself from ticks, removing a bite, and saving the tick for identification. Not a medical source — consult a doctor when needed.

Assistance or emergency help

  • Montauk Police Precinct: (631) 668-3707
  • East Hampton Police Precinct: (631) 324-3018
  • East Hampton Marine Patrol: (631) 537-7575

These numbers are also used to report fights, prohibited animals on the beach, and illegal fires.

Being a good neighbor

Our community is residential, not commercial, and residents and renters should be able to enjoy peaceful surroundings. Renters should tell their guests the rules, ideally in a posted page of rules, and share their contact information with neighbors. Please be considerate of neighbors and follow the code of neighborliness.

Beach emergencies

Call 631-537-7575. Use the Orange M sign nearest you to relay your location.
