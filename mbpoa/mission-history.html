Mission & History

What we do, and how a beach came to be held in trust since 1942.

Mission

  • Afford a safe and enjoyable beach experience for all.
  • Preserve and protect the environment that we all love. Promote the preservation and availability of natural resources for the benefit of members.
  • Encourage our members’ social and civic interaction through, when possible, a summer beach party and a members’ cocktail party, along with communications to members of activities and civic concerns.

What we do

Maintenance

  • Regular preventive maintenance of pathways and steps leading to the beach and ongoing repairs when needed.
  • Maintain parking lot locks, provide keys to members, and replace chains as needed.
  • Provide, update, and maintain MBPOA signage.

Social events

  • Organize beach and property cleanups. Board Directors participate, and volunteers from the member base are requested to volunteer.
  • Organize social events for MBPOA Members and their guests.

Legal

  • Retain when needed representation to protect the property rights of the MBPOA-owned property.

Local government & civic organizations

  • Engage with our local government to understand laws and possible developments impacting the property rights of our Association.

History

  • July 15, 1942Title taken to the “Ocean View Beach”
  • June 17, 1942MBPOA incorporated
  • 500+Paid member home and property owners

Montauk Beach Property Owner’s Association took title to what was then known as the “Ocean View Beach” on July 15, 1942 and were incorporated on June 17, 1942. The original plan for our area of Montauk — as envisioned by developer Carl Fisher — was for an 18-hole golf course. Due to the great depression, his plans to make Montauk the “Miami Beach of the North” crumbled. Montauk Beach Development Corporation were the next owners of the property in 1926. The Mirror Holding Corporation who sold 100′ x 25′ lots for $100 each, subsequently owned the area. The Mirror Holding Corporation eventually became The Montauk Improvement Company, which today is the Tuma Agency.

When MBPOA took title to the Ocean View Beach it also took title to the building below the Montauk Manor known as the Playhouse. Yes, at one time our association owned the Playhouse. When the MBPOA was unable to maintain the building due to the cost of upkeep and taxes, it reverted back to the original deed holder.

The history of our beachfront property also shows that the MBPOA almost gave the property to the Town of East Hampton. Again, the problem was the lack of money for upkeep. However, just prior to the transfer of the property to the town, the deal was cancelled. Our dues allow us to maintain the property and advocate for our homeowners.

Because of our unique beachfront rights, MBPOA members should be aware that the value of their homes / property could be higher than that of a similar house in almost any other area in Montauk. We are the only homeowners in Montauk who have access to our own privately deeded ocean beach right down the street. MBPOA pays taxes, insurance, and other costs to maintain the property. Our organization is also known for our active civic participation in the Montauk community. MBPOA is always alert to the environmental and other concerns that affect our area and the value of your property.

At this time, our paid membership encompasses over 500 home / property owners. MBPOA is the largest active civic association in Montauk.

We would like to thank all of you for the outstanding support you have given this association throughout the years. For new members, we would like to welcome you to the MBPOA and encourage your active participation in our association. With your involvement, the MBPOA and our ocean beach will be a source of pleasure and pride for many generations to come.
