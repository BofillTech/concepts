A story in every hallway

Bette Davis, Howard Hughes and Will Rogers slept here.

In 1932 Jim Webb imagined a grand hotel for Boulder City, with private baths, air conditioning and a wood-paneled lobby to welcome dignitaries visiting the new dam. Through the 1930s it drew movie stars, royalty and industrialists.

Today the hotel is owned and operated by the Boulder City Museum and Historical Association, so every stay helps preserve Boulder City's history.

A Brief History

The hotel that welcomed the world to Boulder Dam

A.P. Giannini, the Vanderbilts, Henry Fonda, Boris Karloff, Shirley Temple and the Maharajah of Indore all signed the register. Howard Hughes recuperated here after crashing his plane on Lake Mead.

Hoover Dam and Lake Mead are minutes away, and Las Vegas is an easy drive.
