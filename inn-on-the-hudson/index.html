Formerly the Peekskill Inn

The Inn on the Hudson has watched this bend of the river for decades. Over the past ten years the property has been renovated top to bottom — modern rooms, property-wide internet, and satellite HDTV, all with a touch of colonial flair.

We're a short ride from the Peekskill Metro-North station and one hour from Manhattan, but close enough to downtown Peekskill's art district, shops, and restaurants to walk there for dinner. Every stay includes a complimentary continental breakfast and access to our lobby snack shop.

The View Guests Come Back For

Ask any guest what they remember, and it's the river. Several of our rooms open onto private terraces facing west across the Hudson toward Bear Mountain — the kind of view that's easy to sit with over morning coffee.

A guest who's stayed here more than once calls it quaint, charming, and reasonably priced — with a view of the Hudson from her favorite room that keeps bringing her back every year. — Repeat guest, Ossining, NY

Around the Inn

Discover the Hudson Valley

Overlooking the Hudson at the edge of historic Peekskill, the Inn is the ideal stepping-off point for the entire region — and much of it is within an easy walk or short drive of our front gate.

Downtown Peekskill

An eclectic mix of art studios, shops, parks, and restaurants, all within walking distance of the Inn.

Bear Mountain State Park

Hiking trails and river views a short drive away — several of our rooms even face the mountain directly.

West Point

The United States Military Academy is about fifteen minutes from the Inn, a popular day trip for guests.
