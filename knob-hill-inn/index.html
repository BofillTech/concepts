The Inn

A chalet built for two seasons at once

Twenty-nine rooms and suites sit behind a timbered, old-world facade, each with a marble bath, feather bedding, and a balcony aimed at either the Sawtooths or Bald Mountain. It's the kind of hotel that reads like a European lodge from the street and feels like a well-kept second home once the door closes behind you.

Seven ways to stay

In the Inn

Everything that isn't your room

The inn does the small logistics of a mountain trip so you don't have to — a heated pool for after the slopes, a shuttle that runs across town, and a concierge desk that acts like it's known you for years.

Dining

The Grill at Knob Hill

Locals Bob and Jolie Dunn — formerly of Warm Springs Ranch Restaurant and The Bigwood Grill — run the inn's restaurant like a neighborhood institution that happens to sit inside a hotel. The Rocky Mountain-influenced menu leans on Idaho trout, prime steaks, local lamb, and wild game, with nightly specials from Chef Sarah Janego.

Dinner moves easily between the private Fireplace Room, a glass-walled indoor/outdoor terrace, and garden lawn seating with views north — one of the better outdoor tables in the valley.
