Welcome to Bear Creek Lodge

Bear Creek Lodge sits on 65 acres surrounded by the beautiful Payette National Forest. Adventure awaits with Brundage Mountain and Payette Lake just 5 miles away!

The Lodge offers a variety of rooms and sizes to meet every need of your event. Inside we offer free WiFi, DirecTV, fireplaces in most rooms, a 15' screen in our Theater Room, a pool table and more. Outside we have hot tubs, large fire pit, gazebo, pond, and beautiful grounds.

Whether it's a wedding, family reunion, church retreat, ski trip, snowmobile adventure, corporate retreat, or romantic getaway, we are committed to making your time at Bear Creek everything you imagined it could be and MORE!

Perfect for Every Event

Weddings & Celebrations

Experience two extraordinary days at Bear Creek Lodge. Our 65-acre property includes a 5,000 sq. ft. lodge, heart-shaped pond, gazebo, spacious deck, and multiple ceremony sites. Wedding packages include 200 garden chairs, 40 tables, beautiful arch, and exclusive use of the entire property with 2 nights lodging for up to 48+ guests.

Reunions & Group Events

The ideal location for family reunions, church retreats, corporate events, and group gatherings. Reserve all guest buildings and grounds for your special event. Accommodate overnight groups as small as 10 guests or day groups as large as 200+.

Vacation Rentals

Family cabins are available for private short-term trips or romantic getaways. Experience the magic of McCall and Payette National Forest from your own private wilderness retreat.
