ABOUT US

We are a family-owned bed and breakfast located only a few minutes from downtown Pagosa Springs, Colorado. Our lodge includes a daily scrumptious breakfast and access to amenities such as a sauna, hot tub, pool table and wrap around decks with breathtaking views of the nearby mountains.

Our hope is that you'll cherish your time in the mountains with fond recollections of great memories and new friends. We at Elktrace Bed and Breakfast are here to ensure that you and your loved ones enjoy your time here and make memories to last a lifetime!
