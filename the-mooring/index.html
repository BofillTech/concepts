Cape May's original guest house.

Situated one block from the beach in downtown Historic Cape May, The Mooring has hosted seaside retreats since 1882 — breakfast on the breezy veranda, rocking chairs on the porch, and the Washington Street Mall a short stroll around the corner.

the veranda, from 7:30 a.m.

Savory one morning. Sweet the next.

Breakfasts at The Mooring are chef-prepared, alternating in style from day to day — perhaps a three-egg omelet or a savory baked egg dish one morning, and the next a French-toasted brioche with homemade fruit compote or a crisp blueberry waffle, hot off the iron. Served with savory sausages, Applewood smoked bacon, or the well-loved peppered bacon.

Intimate breakfasts are enjoyed at individual tables for two, seatings at 8:30 and 9:30, throughout the dining room and the spacious front porch. Early coffee and tea from 7:30 — and the guest kitchen keeps coffee and hot water going 24/7.

inside the house

Kept the way a summer house should be.

Mornings begin with early coffee on the porch. The guest kitchen stays open around the clock — refrigerator, microwave, mugs, wine glasses, ice buckets and wine keys all at hand. Beach chairs and towels wait by the door, there's an outdoor shower for sandy feet, and a bicycle if you'd rather ride the island than drive it.
