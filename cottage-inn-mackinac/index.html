Welcome

Victorian charm, without the creaky floorboards.

Mackinac Island has been charming visitors for decades with its landscape, history, shopping, and sightseeing. The Cottage Inn encompasses all the charm the island offers — and it’s located steps away from it all.

Whether you’re celebrating a birthday, anniversary, or honeymoon, or you just need a weekend getaway, the Cottage Inn is your home away from home. Our guests become our friends, and our friends are always welcome.

Rooms & Rates

Eleven rooms, each with its own story.

Every room is uniquely appointed with a pillow-top queen bed, private bathroom with tiled shower, air conditioning, and flat-screen TV — and every stay includes our complimentary homemade breakfast.

Mackinac Suite

Our third-floor, three-room suite: vaulted ceilings, a sitting turret with three stuffed chairs, two queen beds with pillow-top mattresses and down pillows, two TVs, and a refrigerator. Comfortably sleeps four.

Wake to the aroma of a homemade breakfast.
