Ask dispatch something else

Do you provide car service throughout Putnam County?

Yes. We serve towns across Putnam County, including Carmel, Mahopac, Brewster and Cold Spring.

Which airports do you serve from Putnam County?

JFK, LaGuardia, Newark, Westchester County Airport (HPN) and Teterboro, with flight tracking on every airport pickup.

How long will my chauffeur wait at the airport?

We track inbound domestic and international commercial flights from 90 minutes before arrival. You get 30 minutes of courtesy waiting time from the last reported arrival time; after that, waiting is billed in 15-minute increments. FBO arrivals have 15 minutes and other pickups a 10-minute grace period.

Is dispatch available 24/7?

Yes. Dispatch answers 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year, at 914-725-9444 or toll free at 888-444-3108.

How do I book?

Book online in a few minutes through our reservation system, call dispatch at 914-725-9444, or request a quote and we will confirm the details with you.
