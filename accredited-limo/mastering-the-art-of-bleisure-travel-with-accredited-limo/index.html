Why Bleisure Travel is the New Business Trip MVP

  • Double Down on Your Hours: You can’t squeeze in sightseeing with a packed schedule? Bleisure travel begs to differ. With strategic planning and the right transportation (ahem, Accredited Limo, anyone?), you can maximize your itinerary and fit in those must-see sights during free pockets between meetings or after work hours.
