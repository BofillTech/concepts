Frequently asked questions

Straight answers before you book.

Ask dispatch something else

How many passengers fit in each vehicle?

Sprinters and the limo Sprinter carry up to 12, minibuses 24 or 36, and motor coaches 40 or 56. Sedans and SUVs suit one to three passengers with luggage; ask dispatch for exact seating.

How old are your vehicles?

No sedan or SUV in our fleet is older than two years, and every vehicle passes a safety inspection before each trip.

Is Wi-Fi available?

Yes, on the Sprinter, minibuses and coaches when requested at booking.

Do you offer child seats?

If you are traveling with a child who needs a car or booster seat, you are responsible for bringing and securing it. Children under 16 must travel with a parent or legal guardian.

Which vehicle should I book?

Tell us the passengers and luggage and we will recommend one. Get an instant quote or call 914-725-9444.
