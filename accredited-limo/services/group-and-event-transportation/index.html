Frequently asked questions

Straight answers before you book.

Ask dispatch something else

How many passengers can you move?

From 10 to 56 per vehicle, using Sprinters (up to 12), 24- and 36-passenger minibuses and 40- and 56-passenger coaches, and more vehicles for larger groups.

Can I book transportation for an event?

Yes. Request a group quote with your dates, venues and headcount, and we will recommend the right vehicles.

How far in advance should I book a group?

As early as possible. Group vehicles book up quickly on busy weekends and during conference season.

What is your cancellation policy for buses?

Call or text 914-725-9444, or email reservations@accreditedlimo.com, as soon as your plans change. A cancellation inside the scheduled pickup time is charged in full, deposits are non-refundable, and policies can differ for buses, minibuses and event bookings. See our terms and conditions.

Are you available 24 hours a day?

Yes. Dispatch answers 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year, at 914-725-9444 or toll free at 888-444-3108.
