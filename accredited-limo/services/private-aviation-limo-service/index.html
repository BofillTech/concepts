Frequently asked questions

Straight answers before you book.

Ask dispatch something else

Which private airports do you serve?

Teterboro (TEB), Westchester County (HPN) and the FBOs at JFK, LaGuardia and Newark, plus other regional airports on request.

How long will the chauffeur wait at an FBO?

FBO arrivals have 15 minutes of courtesy waiting time. After that, waiting time is billed in 15-minute increments.

Can the car meet us planeside?

Where the FBO and airport allow it, yes. Otherwise your chauffeur meets you inside the FBO.

Can I book for a passenger or crew?

Yes. Assistants, schedulers and flight departments book for passengers and crew all the time. Call dispatch at 914-725-9444 any hour.

Are you available 24 hours a day?

Yes. Dispatch answers 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year, at 914-725-9444 or toll free at 888-444-3108.
