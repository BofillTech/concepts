PRIVATE AVIATION LIMO SERVICE

As your aircraft graces the tarmac at any of New York City's airports, our professional chauffeur will be on hand to welcome you with a warm smile and a gracious demeanor. After a long flight, we understand the significance of a friendly face, and our chauffeurs are adept at ensuring your comfort from the moment you disembark.

Are you jetting into the bustling heart of New York City? Accredited Limousine extends a warm invitation to enhance your journey and make it truly exceptional. Our Private Aviation Limo Service caters to the diverse needs of the global private aviation market, serving corporate fleet departments, FBOs, fractionals, and aircraft/management charter companies. When you select Accredited Limousine, you're choosing a partner who recognizes the value of a seamless airport transfer.

Traveling can be a cumbersome affair, especially with cumbersome luggage. Our chauffeurs are not just skilled drivers; they are here to provide you with expert assistance in managing your bags. Whether you're toting a single carry-on or an assortment of suitcases, we have it all under control. Ease your mind and allow us to manage the hard work.

Our fleet of limousines radiates luxury and sophistication. From classic stretch limousines to sleek modern vehicles, we have a ride that caters to your style and comfort preferences. Our limousines are meticulously maintained to ensure a seamless and comfortable journey to your hotel or final destination.

Time is a precious commodity, and we understand its importance. We monitor your flight schedule in real-time, so even if your plane is delayed, your chauffeur will be on-site, ready and waiting—eliminating the hassle of long waits and uncertainties, offering only punctual and dependable service.
