Your schedule, not a dispatch queue

With hourly service you choose your stops and set your own pace. Your chauffeur stays with the vehicle, ready when you walk out.

It suits roadshow-style days, property tours, client entertaining and any day where the plan might change.

  • Vetted chauffeurs. Licensed, background-checked, with at least two years of commercial driving.
  • Late-model vehicles. No sedan or SUV older than two years, inspected before every trip.
  • Live updates. Chauffeur name, photo, vehicle and a live map, sent by text.

When hourly service makes sense

Back-to-back meetings

No waiting for a car between appointments.

Site and property visits

Several locations, one chauffeur who knows the route.

Client entertaining

Dinner, a show and the ride home, with the car waiting.

Visiting executives

A chauffeur on call for the length of the visit.

How it works

Three steps, the same every time.

  1. Book online or call

    Reserve online in minutes or call dispatch 24/7. You get a confirmation, then a notification when your chauffeur is on the way.

  2. Live updates

    Fifteen minutes before pickup we send who is picking you up, and where. Through the app you can see where your chauffeur is.

  3. Pickup

    Your chauffeur arrives, helps with your bags, and you are on your way.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers before you book.

Ask dispatch something else

How does hourly service work?

You book a vehicle and chauffeur for a block of time. The chauffeur follows your schedule and waits with the car between stops.

What is included in the bill?

Your quote covers the reserved service. Extras such as additional stops, waiting time beyond the booking, tolls, parking, airport fees and holiday or after-hours service are itemized on your statement.

Can I add a stop on the day?

Yes. Tell your chauffeur or call dispatch, and we will adjust the plan.

How far in advance should I book?

As early as you can, especially for groups, weddings and busy travel days. For a same-day ride, call dispatch at 914-725-9444 and we will tell you what is available.

Are you available 24 hours a day?

Yes. Dispatch answers 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year, at 914-725-9444 or toll free at 888-444-3108.
