How it works

Three steps, the same every time.

  1. Book online or call

    Reserve online in minutes or call dispatch 24/7. You get a confirmation, then a notification when your chauffeur is on the way.

  2. Live updates

    Fifteen minutes before pickup we send who is picking you up, and where. Through the app you can see where your chauffeur is.

  3. Pickup

    Your chauffeur arrives, helps with your bags, and you are on your way.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers before you book.

Ask dispatch something else

Can you move hosts and guests at the same event?

Yes. We combine sedans and SUVs for hosts and VIPs with Sprinters or minibuses for guests, on one schedule.

Will the cars wait during the event?

Yes, on an hourly booking the chauffeur stays with the vehicle and is ready when you are.

How far in advance should I book?

As early as you can, especially for groups, weddings and busy travel days. For a same-day ride, call dispatch at 914-725-9444 and we will tell you what is available.

What is your cancellation policy for events?

Call or text 914-725-9444, or email reservations@accreditedlimo.com, as soon as your plans change. A cancellation inside the scheduled pickup time is charged in full, deposits are non-refundable, and policies can differ for buses, minibuses and event bookings. See our terms and conditions.

Are you available 24 hours a day?

Yes. Dispatch answers 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year, at 914-725-9444 or toll free at 888-444-3108.
