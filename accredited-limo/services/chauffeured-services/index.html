Frequently asked questions

Straight answers before you book.

Ask dispatch something else

Do you set up corporate accounts?

Yes. Open a corporate account and your assistants and travel managers can book for any traveler, with fast confirmations and accurate receipts.

How are your chauffeurs vetted?

Licensing, registration, background checks and employment history verification, plus a minimum of two years of commercial driving experience and a clean driving record. See our duty of care.

Will my traveler know who is picking them up?

Yes. You receive a text with your chauffeur's name, photo and vehicle details, and a live GPS map of the car as it heads to you.

Are you available 24 hours a day?

Yes. Dispatch answers 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year, at 914-725-9444 or toll free at 888-444-3108.

Can I cancel or change a booking?

Call or text 914-725-9444, or email reservations@accreditedlimo.com, as soon as your plans change. A cancellation inside the scheduled pickup time is charged in full, deposits are non-refundable, and policies can differ for buses, minibuses and event bookings. See our terms and conditions.
