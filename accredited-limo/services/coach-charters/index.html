Three steps, the same every time.

    Reserve online in minutes or call dispatch 24/7. You get a confirmation, then a notification when your chauffeur is on the way.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers before you book.

Ask dispatch something else

What sizes of minibus and coach do you have?

Minibuses for 24 and 36 passengers and motor coaches for 40 and 56 passengers. A 26-passenger minibus may also be available; ask dispatch.

What amenities do the coaches have?

The 56-passenger coach has reclining seats, seat belts, a PA system, storage compartments, climate control and a restroom. The minibuses have reclining leather seats, overhead racks, reading lights and USB outlets. Wi-Fi is available on request.

How far in advance should I book a charter?

As early as possible, especially for spring and fall weekends.

What is the cancellation policy?

Call or text 914-725-9444, or email reservations@accreditedlimo.com, as soon as your plans change. A cancellation inside the scheduled pickup time is charged in full, deposits are non-refundable, and policies can differ for buses, minibuses and event bookings. See our terms and conditions.

Can I get a quote?

Yes. Request a group quote with your date, route and headcount.
