How do I plan roadshow transportation?

Send us the meeting schedule, the number of travelers and any airport legs. We confirm the vehicles and timing, and one dispatcher stays with your day.

Can you handle roadshows in other cities?

Yes. Through our global network we can arrange chauffeured service in more than 700 cities.

What if a meeting runs long?

Your chauffeur waits with the car. Waiting time and extra stops are itemized on your statement.

Are your chauffeurs discreet?

Yes. They are trained to handle confidential information with discretion and professionalism.
