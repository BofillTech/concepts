Frequently asked questions

Straight answers before you book.

Ask dispatch something else

Who is a corporate account for?

Companies whose executives, clients and visiting teams ride regularly, and the executive assistants and travel managers who book for them.

What do we get with an account?

Fast confirmations, clear pickup instructions and accurate receipts, with dispatch answering 24/7.

How are your chauffeurs vetted?

Licensing, registration, background checks and employment history verification, with at least two years of commercial driving experience and a clean record. See duty of care.

Can travelers see who is picking them up?

Yes. You receive a text with your chauffeur's name, photo and vehicle details, and a live GPS map of the car as it heads to you.

What is your cancellation policy?

Call or text 914-725-9444, or email reservations@accreditedlimo.com, as soon as your plans change. A cancellation inside the scheduled pickup time is charged in full, deposits are non-refundable, and policies can differ for buses, minibuses and event bookings. See our terms and conditions.
