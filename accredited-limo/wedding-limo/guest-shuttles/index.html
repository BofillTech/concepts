Frequently asked questions

Straight answers before you book.

Ask dispatch something else

How do wedding guest shuttles work?

We run scheduled loops between your hotel block, the ceremony and the reception, sized to your guest count, with a final departure after the last dance.

What size vehicles do you use?

Sprinters for up to 12, minibuses for 24 or 36, and motor coaches for 40 or 56 guests.

How many shuttles do we need?

It depends on the guest count, distances and timing. Send us the plan and we will recommend the number of vehicles and loops.

What is the cancellation policy?

Call or text 914-725-9444, or email reservations@accreditedlimo.com, as soon as your plans change. A cancellation inside the scheduled pickup time is charged in full, deposits are non-refundable, and policies can differ for buses, minibuses and event bookings. See our terms and conditions.
