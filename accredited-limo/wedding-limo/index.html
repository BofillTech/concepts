Frequently asked questions

Straight answers before you book.

Ask dispatch something else

How far in advance should we book wedding transportation?

As early as you can. Spring and fall Saturdays book up first, especially for minibuses and coaches.

Which vehicle is right for bridesmaids and groomsmen?

A Sprinter or limo Sprinter carries up to 12 in formal wear. For the couple, a sedan or SUV. For guests, a 24- or 36-passenger minibus or a 40- or 56-passenger coach.

Can you shuttle guests between the hotel and venue?

Yes. See wedding guest shuttles for loops between hotels, ceremony and reception.

Are deposits refundable?

No. Deposits are non-refundable, and dispatch will confirm deposit and payment terms with your quote.

What is the cancellation policy for weddings?

Cancellations for Sprinter vans, minibuses and coaches need two weeks' notice before the scheduled pickup, and a no-show is charged in full. See our terms and conditions.
