Wedding Transportation

Wedding day transportation for the couple, bridal party and guests: sedans, Sprinter vans and guest shuttle minibuses across Westchester and NYC.

For the Couple

Arrive in style in a 2026 Lincoln Aviator or 2025 Volvo S90. Your chauffeur ensures you arrive on time for every photo.

Bridal Party Transportation

Mercedes-Benz Sprinter vans seat up to 12 passengers with luxury leather seating and plenty of room for dresses and suits.

Wedding Guest Shuttles

Minibuses for 24 to 36 passengers and motor coaches up to 56 passengers coordinate hotel pickups, ceremony and reception transportation.

How We Plan Your Wedding Day

  1. Check your date - Contact us to confirm availability for your wedding date
  2. Plan the timeline - We coordinate with you and your planner for all pickup times
  3. Wedding day - Your chauffeurs arrive on schedule and everyone gets where they need to be
