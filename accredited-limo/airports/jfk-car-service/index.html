How JFK pickups work

From Westchester and Fairfield County, JFK is a long run, and the one where timing matters most.

We watch your inbound flight from 90 minutes before arrival and plan departures around traffic on the Hutchinson, the Whitestone and the Van Wyck, so you are not the one checking the clock.

  • Flight tracking. From 90 minutes before arrival.
  • Courtesy wait. 30 minutes from the last reported arrival time.
  • Live updates. Chauffeur name, photo and GPS map by text.

JFK car service questions

Straight answers before you book.

Ask dispatch something else

How long will my chauffeur wait at JFK?

We track inbound domestic and international commercial flights from 90 minutes before arrival. You get 30 minutes of courtesy waiting time from the last reported arrival time; after that, waiting is billed in 15-minute increments. FBO arrivals have 15 minutes and other pickups a 10-minute grace period.

What happens if my flight into JFK is delayed?

We track inbound domestic and international commercial flights from 90 minutes before arrival and adjust your pickup to the actual landing time.

Will I know who is picking me up?

Yes. You receive a text with your chauffeur's name, photo and vehicle details, and a live GPS map of the car as it heads to you.

How do I book a ride to JFK?

Book online in a few minutes through our reservation system, call dispatch at 914-725-9444, or request a quote and we will confirm the details with you.

Which vehicle should I book for the airport?

A sedan for one or two travelers with standard luggage, an SUV for more bags, and a Sprinter for up to 12. Tell us the bag count and we will confirm.

What is your cancellation policy?

Call or text 914-725-9444, or email reservations@accreditedlimo.com, as soon as your plans change. A cancellation inside the scheduled pickup time is charged in full, deposits are non-refundable, and policies can differ for buses, minibuses and event bookings. See our terms and conditions.
