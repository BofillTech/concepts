NYC Airports: Quick guide

Take the hassle out of planning your next trip to New York City by familiarizing yourself with all three major airports: JFK, EWR, and LGA. Your airline choice and hotel accommodations will determine which airport is most suitable for you; however, our quick guide can help narrow down potential options. And be sure that Accredited Limousine is a dependable option when it comes time to arrange reliable transportation—allowing you more freedom over how much fun you want on your vacation!

Enjoy the ease and convenience of global travel when you fly out of John F. Kennedy International Airport! With a selection of nonstop flights offered by major international carriers, it's no wonder why JFK is one of New York City's most popular airports – especially for travelers in eastern Queens or lower Brooklyn looking to hop across continents. Plus, you won't have any trouble finding something to keep you occupied while waiting; this airport offers terrific amenities like restaurants, stores, and lounges that are sure to make your journey more enjoyable. But wait… there's even more reason to choose JFK as your departure point: JetBlue has made it it's home here at Terminal 5 with exciting renovations underway and two new terminals for completion by 2025!

Travelers will find Newark Liberty International Airport a hub of international activity! With over 30 airlines, including more than a dozen foreign carriers and United utilizing Terminal C as the airport's key hub, there are plenty of flights to choose from. While it may be an expensive yet time-consuming journey for those traveling from Queens or Brooklyn – it's much easier if you live on Manhattan's west side. So pack your bags to experience all that awaits in this unique corner of New York before taking off!

Are you looking to travel domestically? Put LaGuardia (LGA) at the top of your list! This hub is conveniently located just a short distance from northern Brooklyn and Upper or Midtown Manhattan. It provides flights through American Airlines, Delta, JetBlue, United, and Southwest, plus many more airlines – perfect for every vacationer looking to explore the U.S! Plus with Terminal B & C's recent renovations, you can enjoy smoother sailing throughout their services as well … So what are you waiting for?!

Accredited Limo is your one-stop luxury travel shop! From sedans to stretch limousines, we offer a sleek selection of chauffeured vehicles for any occasion. You can be sure you're at the airport on time with our convenient transfer services from JFK, EWR, and LGA in NYC. Traveling has never looked so good!
