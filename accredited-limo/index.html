Frequently asked questions

Straight answers before you book.

Ask dispatch something else

What transportation services does Accredited Limo provide?

Chauffeured sedans, SUVs and Sprinters for corporate travel, airport transfers, hourly service, private aviation, roadshows, group and event transportation, minibus and coach charters, weddings and global bookings in more than 700 cities.

Do you provide airport transportation?

Yes. We cover JFK, LaGuardia, Newark, Westchester County (HPN) and Teterboro, with flight tracking and meet and greet.

How far in advance should I book?

As early as you can, especially for groups, weddings and busy travel days. For a same-day ride, call dispatch at 914-725-9444 and we will tell you what is available.

Do you set up corporate accounts?

Yes. A corporate account keeps your team's bookings in one place, with fast confirmations, clear pickup instructions and accurate receipts.

Will I know who is picking me up?

Yes. You receive a text with your chauffeur's name, photo and vehicle details, and a live GPS map of the car as it heads to you.

Are you available 24 hours a day?

Yes. Dispatch answers 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year, at 914-725-9444 or toll free at 888-444-3108.
