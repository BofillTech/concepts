Frequently asked questions

Straight answers before you book.

Ask dispatch something else

How do I request a quote?

Fill in the form on this page or call dispatch at 914-725-9444. Give us your date, pickup, drop-off and group size, and we will reply with a price.

How far in advance should I book?

As early as you can, especially for groups, weddings and busy travel days. For a same-day ride, call dispatch at 914-725-9444 and we will tell you what is available.

What can affect the final price?

Quotes are based on the details you give us. Extra stops, waiting time, tolls, tax, airport fees, parking, fuel surcharges, after-hours service and holiday travel are itemized on your statement.

How long will my chauffeur wait at the airport?

We track inbound domestic and international commercial flights from 90 minutes before arrival. You get 30 minutes of courtesy waiting time from the last reported arrival time; after that, waiting is billed in 15-minute increments. FBO arrivals have 15 minutes and other pickups a 10-minute grace period.

What is your cancellation policy?

Call or text 914-725-9444, or email reservations@accreditedlimo.com, as soon as your plans change. A cancellation inside the scheduled pickup time is charged in full, deposits are non-refundable, and policies can differ for buses, minibuses and event bookings. See our terms and conditions.
