Your Host

“Traveling through the Ozarks many years ago, I stopped in Eureka Springs for a glass of tea, and I never left. I invite you to discover the magic and tranquility of the Benton Place Inn — it too will steal your heart.”

— Rebecca

The enchantment begins as you climb the stone stairs to a private garden entry. Once inside, you'll be charmed by Victorian furnishings, stained-glass lamps, and antique prints — each of the three suites offering complete privacy and a garden gazebo perched in the trees above, ideal for morning coffee or quiet conversation under the stars.

The Benton Place Inn was named one of the Most Intimate Places in America by Inn Traveler, The Book of Lists — with over twenty years of guests who return, year after year, to make more memories.

Accommodations

Three suites. Each one entirely your own.

Every suite at the Benton Place Inn has its own private entrance, so you never cross paths with another guest — just antiques, a two-person jacuzzi, and a fireplace to warm the room on cool Ozark nights.

01

Memories Suite

Antiques, art, and a private garden deck

Fine antiques and vintage-style furnishings fill this suite, with a private deck set among garden rock and a slow-moving koi pond just outside. A frequent favorite for anniversaries and honeymoons.

  • Two-person jacuzzi
  • Fireplace
  • Full kitchenette
  • Private entrance & deck
02

Cliffside Suite

Set against the rock, warmed by the fire

Tucked closest to Eureka Springs' ancient rock formations, the Cliffside Suite pairs an inviting bedroom fireplace with a whirlpool tub built for two — a quiet, secluded retreat just a short walk from Main Street.

  • Two-person jacuzzi
  • Fireplace
  • HBO / cable & DVD
  • Private entrance
03

Benton Suite

The wrap-around balcony, overlooking the street

Upstairs and set back for extra privacy, the Benton Suite is the roomiest of the three, with a wrap-around balcony guests return to trip after trip — comfortable, quiet, and an easy stroll to Spring Street.

  • Two-person jacuzzi
  • Wrap-around balcony
  • Full kitchenette
  • Private entrance

The Grounds

Koi ponds, a waterfall, and rock older than the town itself.

Surrounded by native foliage, local wildlife, and ancient Ozark rock formations, the Benton Place Inn is beautiful, private, and secluded — yet steps from everything downtown Eureka Springs has to offer.
