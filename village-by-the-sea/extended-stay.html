Extended stay FAQ

Common questions.

Does Village by the Sea offer extended stays?

Yes. Village by the Sea offers furnished condominium-style lodging that can be a comfortable option for guests staying for several weeks. Availability and applicable stay requirements vary by date and accommodation.

How long can I stay?

Under the Town of Wells' lodging requirements, occupancy of lodging units is limited to 28 consecutive days. If you need accommodations beyond 28 consecutive days, please contact us before booking so we can discuss your circumstances and the applicable requirements.

Is Village by the Sea corporate housing?

Village by the Sea is a lodging property rather than a residential corporate-housing complex. However, our furnished condominium suites can be a comfortable option for employees and business travelers who need temporary accommodations for a work assignment.

Do you offer monthly rentals?

Village by the Sea does not operate as a traditional monthly apartment or residential rental property. Our accommodations are lodging units subject to the Town of Wells' applicable requirements, including the 28-consecutive-day occupancy limit. If you need accommodations beyond 28 consecutive days, please contact us before booking.

Are the suites furnished?

Yes. Our condominium suites are furnished and individually decorated by their owners. Furnishings and amenities vary by unit, so we recommend reviewing the photos and details of the specific suite before booking.

Do the suites have kitchens?

Many of our condominium suites include full kitchens, making them particularly convenient for guests staying for several weeks.

Is Village by the Sea near Portsmouth?

Village by the Sea is located in Wells, Maine, and offers a Southern Maine lodging option for guests working or traveling in the Portsmouth and New Hampshire Seacoast area.

Is Village by the Sea near Ogunquit and Kennebunkport?

Yes. We're located in Wells, Maine, between Ogunquit and Kennebunkport, making Village by the Sea a convenient home base for exploring Southern Maine.

Stay-length requirements reflect the Town of Wells lodging ordinance. Please contact us before booking if you need accommodations beyond 28 consecutive days.
