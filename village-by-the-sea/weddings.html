Why couples book here

Nobody has to drive home.

Most venues end when the music stops. Here the ceremony lawn, the terrace, the ballroom and your guests’ beds are all inside the same eleven acres — and those beds are full one- and two-bedroom condominium suites with kitchens, living rooms and private decks, not hotel rooms.

The day

Four rooms, none of them
more than a walk apart.

01

The pergola & gardens

Vows happen outside, under the white columned pergola at the top of the lawn, with the gardens and beach grass behind you. There’s a raised deck for the ceremony, a paved aisle for the walk, and enough lawn on either side to seat everyone comfortably.

It takes flowers beautifully — couples have hung everything from a full grass-and-rose installation across the crossbeam to draped white fabric down the columns.

  • Raised ceremony deck
  • Paved aisle
  • Gardens for portraits
  • Lawn seating

02

The stone terrace

Cocktail hour moves onto the covered outdoor terrace off the ballroom — high-tops, passed hors d’oeuvres, and the gardens still in view while the room is flipped behind you.

It’s the part of the property that does the most work in a Maine summer: shaded, breezy, and a few steps from both the lawn and the bar.

  • Covered & open-air
  • Adjacent to the ballroom
  • Garden views

03

The Maine Ballroom

Up to 200 guests, with a dance floor and room left for a band or a DJ. French doors open onto the gardens, a bank of windows keeps the daylight coming, and bistro lighting takes over after dark.

The decor is deliberately neutral — it will take whatever color and theme you bring without arguing with it.

  • Up to 200 guests
  • Dance floor
  • Bistro lighting
  • French doors to the gardens
  • Room for a band or DJ

04

And then, home

This is the part that’s hard to arrange anywhere else. When the music stops, your guests walk across the lawn to a suite with a kitchen, a living room and a private deck — and the party keeps going in somebody’s living room instead of ending in a parking lot.

Grandparents get a real bed nearby. Parents with young kids get a second bedroom and a door to close. And in the morning everyone is still here.

  • 954–1,260 sq ft suites
  • Full kitchens
  • Private decks
  • Indoor pool & hot tub year-round
