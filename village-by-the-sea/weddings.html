The wedding weekend

Four days, not four hours.

Because everyone is already staying here, the events on either side of the ceremony stop being a logistics problem and start being the best part of the weekend.

  1. Thursday & Friday

    Arrive and settle in

    Guests check into their suites, unpack groceries, and find the indoor pool. Getting ready happens in a suite with room to spread out rather than a cramped hotel room.

  2. Friday evening

    Rehearsal and welcome

    Rehearsal at the pergola with your coordinator, then a rehearsal dinner or welcome reception in the York Room, without anyone getting back in a car.

  3. Saturday

    Ceremony, cocktails, reception

    Vows at the pergola, cocktail hour on the terrace, then dinner and dancing in the Maine Ballroom until the sparklers come out.

  4. Sunday

    Breakfast, then the beach

    A farewell breakfast on site while everyone is still here, and Wells Beach a few minutes down the road for anyone staying on.

The spaces

Over 4,700 square feet,
and eleven acres of lawn.

The conference center handles groups from two to two hundred. For a wedding you will typically use three of these spaces on the day itself, and a fourth the evening before.

The main reception space, with a dance floor and room left over for a band or a DJ. French doors open onto the gardens and a bank of windows keeps the daylight coming. The decor is deliberately neutral, so it takes whatever colour and theme you bring.

Ceremony & photographs

The pergola & gardens

Vows happen outside under the white columned pergola at the top of the lawn, with the landscaped gardens behind you for portraits afterwards. The ceremony fee covers a rehearsal with an experienced planner, 100 white chairs and use of the bridal suite.

  • Raised ceremony deck
  • 100 white chairs
  • Rehearsal included
  • Bridal suite

Cocktail hour

The outdoor terrace

A covered stone terrace running off the ballroom, set with a barrel bar and high tops while the room is flipped behind you. Shaded, breezy, and a few steps from both the lawn and the bar. It does the most work of any space here in a Maine summer.

  • Covered & open air
  • Off the ballroom
  • Garden views

Seats up to 60

The York Room & the rain plan

Upstairs in the conference center, and the reason a wedding here can be a whole weekend rather than an afternoon. Rehearsal dinners, showers and farewell breakfasts happen in this room. And if the weather turns, the ceremony moves indoors to the ballroom without anyone scrambling.

Wedding FAQ

The questions
we get asked most.

How many guests can Village by the Sea accommodate?

The Maine Ballroom seats up to 200 guests for a wedding reception. The conference center handles groups from two to 200 across more than 4,700 square feet.

Does Village by the Sea have on site accommodations for wedding guests?

Yes. There are more than 70 one and two bedroom condominium suites on the same eleven acres, each with a full kitchen, living room, private baths and a deck or patio. Guests walk from the reception to their own front door.

Can we host the ceremony and reception in the same place?

Yes. Ceremonies take place at the outdoor pergola at the top of the lawn, cocktail hour on the covered terrace, and the reception in the Maine Ballroom. All three are steps apart.

What happens if it rains?

The ceremony moves indoors to the ballroom. The room is set for an indoor ceremony and flipped for the reception, so a wet forecast does not change your plans.

Does Village by the Sea offer wedding room blocks?

Yes, and the block is created at no up front cost to you. Guests book their own suites against it. Blocks are also available for family reunions, corporate retreats and other group stays.

Can we host a rehearsal dinner, welcome party or farewell brunch?

Yes. The York Room seats up to 60 and hosts rehearsal dinners, welcome receptions, showers and farewell breakfasts. The Everlasting Love package builds all three into one price.

Is Village by the Sea an all inclusive wedding venue?

Close to it. Every package includes the ballroom for up to five hours, a professional on site coordinator, Chiavari chairs, linen, china and flatware, dinner, wedding cake and cake cutting, coffee and tea service, and a deluxe suite for the couple on the wedding night.

How much does a wedding at Village by the Sea cost?

Wedding packages start at $99 per person, with three further packages at $129, $139 and $149. Final pricing depends on your date, guest count and which spaces you use. Request a custom proposal and we will put real numbers against your plan.

How far is Village by the Sea from Boston?

About 75 miles, or roughly an hour and a half by car. It is a destination wedding for Boston couples without the destination wedding logistics.

How far is it from Ogunquit and Kennebunkport?

Village by the Sea sits on Route 1 in Wells, directly between the two. Both are a short drive, which gives out of town guests somewhere to go on the days either side of the wedding.

Do the accommodations have kitchens?

Every suite has a full kitchen, a living room, a dining area, two baths and a private deck or patio. One bedroom suites run about 954 square feet and two bedroom suites about 1,260, which is roughly four times the size of an average hotel room.

Can we take our wedding photographs on the property?

Yes. The gardens, the pergola, the lawn and the terrace are all used for portraits, and the light on the lawn in the evening is the reason so many ceremonies here run late in the day.
