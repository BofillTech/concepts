Things to do

Wells, and everything
within a short drive.

Wells is the third-oldest town in Maine and sits on the southern coast between two of the state’s best-known destinations. Beaches, seafood, summer theatre, antiques and outlet shopping are all close, and the wildlife refuge starts at the back edge of the property.

Right here

The Rachel Carson National Wildlife Refuge

The refuge borders the property and stretches along the southern Maine coast, protecting salt marsh and estuary habitat for migratory birds. Great blue herons are regulars in the shallows behind the buildings, you can watch them from your own deck without going anywhere.

  • Out the back door
  • Best at low tide
  • Bring binoculars

A few minutes away

Wells Beach

Miles of sand, and the reason most people come. Wells has a long, gentle beach that suits families, plus the harbor and jetty at the southern end. Parking fills early in July and August, go in the morning or late afternoon.

  • Short drive
  • Go early for parking
  • Family friendly

South

Ogunquit & the Marginal Way

The Marginal Way is a paved cliff walk running above the water into Perkins Cove, galleries, lobster shacks and boat trips at the far end. Ogunquit Playhouse runs professional summer theatre a few minutes further on.

  • Cliff walk
  • Perkins Cove
  • Summer theatre

North

Kennebunkport

Dock Square, the shops, the boatyards and some of the best-known restaurants on this stretch of coast. An easy afternoon from the resort, and the drive up Route 1 or Route 9 is half the pleasure.

  • Dock Square
  • Harbor views
  • Dinner out

Rain plan

Antiques & outlet shopping

More than two dozen antique shops line Route 1 through Wells, it’s one of the best browsing stretches in New England. Kittery’s outlet malls are a straightforward drive south when the weather turns.

  • Route 1
  • Two dozen shops
  • Outlets to the south

Worth the trip

The Wells Reserve at Laudholm

A coastal research reserve with trails through fields, woods and salt marsh down to the beach, set around a historic saltwater farm. Quiet, well signposted, and one of the better walks in the area.

  • Marked trails
  • Historic farm
  • Walk to the beach

On the water

Lobster, boats and the harbor

Wells Harbor has the working end of things, boats, bait, and the places that serve what came off them. Deep-sea fishing and scenic cruises run out of the harbors up and down this coast in season.

  • Lobster in the rough
  • Harbor walks
  • Seasonal cruises

Off season

October to April

Many seasonal businesses close between October and April, and the coast gets very quiet. That’s the appeal for some guests, and the indoor pool, hot tub, fitness center and game room stay open all year, as do the fireplaces and full kitchens.

  • Quiet coast
  • Indoor pool open
  • Lower rates
