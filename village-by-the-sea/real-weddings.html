Real weddings in Wells, Maine

A destination wedding
without the travel headaches.

Our location in Wells makes Village by the Sea an ideal choice for couples planning a destination wedding in Maine or a wedding weekend for guests traveling from Massachusetts, New Hampshire and throughout New England.

Guests can stay together in spacious condominium-style suites while enjoying convenient access to Wells Beach, Ogunquit, Kennebunkport and other popular Southern Maine destinations. With accommodations, wedding celebrations and resort amenities all in one location, couples can spend less time coordinating transportation between venues and hotels and more time celebrating with the people they love.

Together

A wedding venue where everyone can stay together

One of the things that makes Village by the Sea different from a traditional wedding venue is the ability to combine your wedding venue and guest accommodations in one place. Instead of sending your guests to several different hotels, your wedding guests can stay at Village by the Sea and enjoy a full wedding weekend together.

Suites

Space for families and wedding parties

Our spacious suites feature separate living and sleeping areas, kitchens and multiple bathrooms, making them especially convenient for families, wedding parties and guests traveling together. Couples can also take advantage of resort amenities, including indoor and seasonal outdoor pools, a hot tub, fitness center, game room, fire pits and outdoor recreation areas.

The whole day

From the ceremony to the last dance

Village by the Sea offers spaces for wedding ceremonies, cocktail hours and receptions, allowing couples to create a complete celebration without having to move their guests from location to location. Whether you are planning a wedding for 25 guests or a larger celebration for up to 200, our team can help you create a wedding experience that fits your vision.
