Summer season

The indoor pool house keeps the same hours as the main office. Through the peak summer season both are open daily, 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM. The seasonal outdoor pool opens the Friday of Memorial Day weekend and runs to around Labor Day.

Off season

From late autumn through spring the office keeps shorter evening hours and the outdoor pool is closed. The indoor heated pool, hot tub, fitness center and game room stay open all year. Arriving late? Call ahead on 207-646-1100 and we will make arrangements.

Hours are adjusted seasonally. Call to confirm before traveling out of season.
