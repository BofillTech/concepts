Comfort, Deluxe, Premium — what the tiers actually mean

The property was built in 1986 with timber construction, and every unit is owned and decorated by its owner. Rather than pretend they’re identical, each one is independently rated by how much updating it has had.

Comfort

Budget-friendly and spacious, with a focus on comfort and value. A restful night’s sleep and plenty of room, at the friendliest rate.

Deluxe

Thoughtfully refreshed and updated — a welcoming, home-away-from-home feel. Good for family breakfasts and hosting friends for dinner.

Premium

The units with the greatest number of updates — our most current and nicest suites, for guests planning to spend real time indoors.

None of the tiers are new construction. To see photos of the exact unit you’d be booking, open the booking system and check what’s available for your dates.

Eleven acres, one edge

The heron on our sign
is not a mascot.

The property borders the Rachel Carson Wildlife Preserve and its tidal salt marsh — which is why the view from most decks is grass, water and sky rather than a parking lot, and why great blue herons work the shallows a few hundred feet from your coffee.

  • 11acres
  • 3floors, no elevator
  • 1986built, timber-framed

Ground-floor units with a patio suit families with young children; third-floor units are the quietest, with no neighbours overhead — and stairs.
