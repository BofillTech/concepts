Work with us

Help wanted,
year round and seasonal.

Village by the Sea is a condominium resort on eleven acres in Wells, run by a small team that knows every guest by the time they leave. We hire year round for the front desk and housekeeping, and seasonally across the property.

Where we hire

The departments
we recruit for.

Openings change with the season. If you do not see your role listed, send your résumé anyway and we will keep it on file for the spring.

Year round

Front desk & reservations

Checking guests in, answering the phone, taking reservations and knowing the property well enough to put a family in the right unit.

Year round

Housekeeping

Turning over full condominium suites rather than hotel rooms. Detail matters here and the work is steady through the year.

Seasonal

Banquet & events

Servers, bartenders and set up crew for weddings and functions, mostly Friday and Saturday through the season.

Seasonal

Kitchen

Line cooks and prep for banquet catering, plated dinners and buffets in the Maine Ballroom.

Year round

Maintenance & grounds

Eleven acres, fifteen buildings, two pools and a lot of landscaping. General maintenance, pool care and groundskeeping.

Seasonal

Pool & guest services

Pool attendants and guest services staff through the summer, plus weekend cover for pool parties.

How to apply

Send us a note.

There is no online application form. Email a résumé or simply tell us what you are looking for, or stop by the front desk. We read everything that comes in.
