On the property

Two pools, a hot tub,
and eleven acres of room.

Everything here is a short walk from your own front door — which, since your door opens onto a full condominium suite rather than a corridor, is the whole point. The indoor pool runs year-round; the rest wakes up with the season.

  • Indoor heated pool

    Open year-round, on the same hours as the main office — so a rainy November afternoon still has somewhere to go.

  • Hot tub

    In the pool house alongside the indoor pool.

  • Seasonal outdoor pool

    Opens the Friday of Memorial Day weekend and runs to around Labor Day.

  • Fitness center

    On site, for resort guests and health club members.

  • Game room

    Open to guests, and included with pool-party bookings.

  • Seasonal gas grills

    Outdoor grilling stations on the lawn — useful when your suite has a full kitchen and a dining table.

  • Bocce court

    Seasonal, on the lawn between the buildings.

  • Basketball hoop

    Plus open lawn for whatever games you bring.

  • Eleven acres

    Gardens, lawn and the tidal salt marsh along the back edge.

  • Free wireless internet

    Throughout the resort and inside every suite.

  • On-site coin laundry

    For the ground-floor units that don’t have their own washer and dryer.

  • A kitchen in every suite

    Full-size, fully equipped, with cookware, dishware and a coffee maker.

Inside the suite

The amenities that
matter most are indoors.

A pool is a nice afternoon. A kitchen, a laundry and a door you can close between the kids and the adults are what make a week work.

In every suite

  • Fully equipped kitchen
  • Coffee maker and coffee
  • Living room
  • Dining area
  • Separate bedroom(s)
  • Two private baths
  • Private deck or patio
  • Washer and dryer
  • Air conditioning
  • Cable TV
  • Free wireless internet
  • Towels and bed sheets

One-bedroom, two-bath ground-floor units have no washer or dryer — there’s coin laundry on site. All other units do.
