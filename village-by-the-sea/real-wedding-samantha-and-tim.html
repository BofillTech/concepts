Fall Wedding in Wells, Maine

A Beautiful September Wedding Weekend in Southern Maine

On September 20, 2025, Samantha and Tim celebrated their wedding at Village by the Sea in Wells, Maine, with 110 family members and friends joining them for their special day. Their early-autumn wedding is a wonderful example of a Southern Maine wedding weekend, combining an outdoor ceremony, a reception at Village by the Sea and accommodations for wedding guests all in one location.

A 110-Guest Maine Wedding

With 110 guests, Samantha and Tim’s celebration demonstrates how Village by the Sea can comfortably host a larger wedding while still providing an intimate destination for the couple and their guests. The wedding party and guests stayed on the property, including accommodations in Unit 11H.

A Wedding Weekend, Not Just a Wedding Day

For many couples planning a destination wedding in Maine, the wedding is only one part of the celebration. Samantha and Tim were able to make their wedding a full weekend experience by having their ceremony, reception and guest accommodations connected to the same destination.

Village by the Sea is located in Wells, Maine, between Ogunquit and Kennebunkport and close to Wells Beach and the Southern Maine coast. Guests can enjoy the area before and after the wedding while having a comfortable suite to return to. This makes Village by the Sea especially appealing for couples whose guests are traveling from Massachusetts, New Hampshire, other parts of Maine and throughout New England.
