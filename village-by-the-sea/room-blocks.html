Group stays & room blocks

Wedding Room Blocks in Wells, Maine:
keep your guests together.

One property. One room block.

Weddings, family reunions, corporate retreats, golf trips, milestone birthdays. If your group needs to stay together, this is a resort where they actually can. Blocks are set up at no up front cost to you, and each guest books and pays for their own suite.

How it works

No deposit, no minimum,
no chasing people down.

Step one

Tell us the dates

Call the front desk with your dates and a rough headcount. We will tell you straight away what we can hold and at what rate.

Step two

We hold the suites

The block is created at no up front cost to you. You are not signing for rooms nobody books, and there is no minimum spend attached.

Step three

Guests book their own

Everyone calls in, quotes the block, and pays for their own suite. You share one phone number instead of collecting credit cards.

Who books blocks here

Groups that would
rather not split up.

Weddings

Wedding room blocks

The whole party in one place, a few hundred feet from the ballroom. No shuttles, no midnight taxis, and everyone is still here for breakfast.

Family

Family reunions

  • AV available
  • On site catering

Everything else

Milestone birthdays, anniversary weekends, sports teams, golf groups and club outings. If it needs beds and somewhere to gather afterwards, ask us.

  • Year round
  • Off season rates

What every group gets

  • Suites of 954 to 1,260 sq ft, not hotel rooms
  • Full kitchen, living room and dining area in every suite
  • Private deck or patio

  • Free parking on site
