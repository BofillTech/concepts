Suites & units

Every unit is different.
So here they all are.

Village by the Sea is a condominium resort, every suite is individually owned and individually decorated, which means no two are alike. Most hotels would hide that. We’d rather show you: pick a building, pick a unit, and see the real photographs, including the actual view from that deck.

How booking works here

You reserve a suite type and level online, one-bedroom, two-bedroom or half suite, in Comfort, Deluxe or Premium. Individual units aren’t bookable through the website. If you find a unit you love, call us at 207-646-1100 and ask for it by number and we’ll do our best to hold it for your dates.

Choose a building

Eleven buildings across eleven acres. Pick a building, then click any unit to see it. Not every unit is in the rental program, the ones shown here are.
