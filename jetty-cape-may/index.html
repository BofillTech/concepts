Since 1941

The Demutis family has called this beach home for three generations.

Since 1941, the Demutis family has vacationed in Cape May and considered it a second home. What started as a family motel has stayed a family motel — steps from one of the world’s most beautiful beaches, where you can swim in the Atlantic, fish for blues, or simply have fun.

The Jetty is a recipient of the Keystone & Restaurant Award, and three generations of Demutis builders have kept the property honest, clean, and unpretentious — the way a beach motel should be.

A Stay Built Around the Beach

Nothing fancy. Everything you need.

Rooms are air conditioned and heated, with cable TV and wireless internet throughout. Housekeeping comes daily, and a coin-operated washer and dryer are on site for longer stays. There’s an outdoor pool if you want a break from the salt water, and one off-street parking spot per room — no circling the block in July.
