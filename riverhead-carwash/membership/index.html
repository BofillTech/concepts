Membership programs

Unlimited monthly washes, express-lane entry, and fleet options — without hunting for a price list online.

Why join

Wash often. Keep it simple.

A membership is for people who would rather pull in than think about it. Current rates are posted at the wash and in the Wash Club portal. We do not publish prices on this site.

It pays for itself if you visit often

Most monthly plans are built around a simple break-even: if you wash regularly, later visits are already covered. One flat fee is easier to budget than paying wash by wash.

Express lanes and contactless entry

Members use dedicated express lanes. License-plate recognition or an RFID tag opens the gate as you approach — no rolling down the window to pay each time.

Protection and the extras

Frequent washing takes off road salt, pollen, and sap before they etch the finish. Members also get the self-serve vacuums, towels, mat cleaners, and air tools on every visit. Multi-car discounts are available for household vehicles.

Our plans

Full service and exterior only

Choose the plan that matches how you like your car finished. Ask at the wash or in the portal for what’s current.

Full Service Platinum

Our top full-service membership. Includes everything in Gold, plus Armor All ceramic seal, Rain-X complete surface protectant, Armor All extreme shine wax, tire shine, and triple foam polish.

Full Service Gold

Rain-X, extreme shine wax, tire shine, triple foam polish, clear-coat sealant, underbody blast, dash wipe, windows cleaned, vacuum, wheels cleaned, and a foam bath.

Full Service Silver

Tire shine, triple foam, clear-coat sealant, underbody blast, and the core full-service wash — a solid everyday plan.

Exterior Plus

An exterior-only unlimited plan with Rain-X, underbody blast, wheels cleaned, foam bath, and the other exterior add-ins posted at the wash.

Exterior Basic

Foam bath and towel dry — the straightforward exterior wash, available as a single visit or an unlimited plan.

Join or get help

Open the Wash Club portal to join, add a car, or buy a gift card. For cancellations or billing help, call customer service and include your license plate.

Commercial

Fleet and dealership wash programs

Custom, high-volume car care for company cars, logistics fleets, and dealership inventory — showroom condition without slowing down the day.

  • Centralized monthly billing — one itemized invoice, broken down by vehicle, plate number, or driver ID.
  • Customizable spending caps — daily, weekly, or monthly wash limits per vehicle.
  • Volume-based discounts — the larger the fleet or the more inventory you move, the lower the per-wash cost.
  • Priority scheduling and digital tracking so you stay in control of appearance and budget.
