The wash

Wash packages

The same full-service and exterior packages you can run as a membership, available as a single visit too. Ask the Greeter or Service Personnel for what’s on the board today.

Full service

Platinum, Gold, and Silver

Platinum is the top wash: Gold plus ceramic seal, Rain-X, extreme shine wax, tire shine, and triple foam polish. Gold adds Rain-X, wax, sealant, underbody blast, dash, windows, vacuum, and wheels. Silver covers the core full-service wash with tire shine, triple foam, sealant, and underbody blast.

Exterior

Exterior Plus and Exterior Basic

Exterior Plus is the richer exterior wash, with Rain-X, underbody blast, wheels, and a foam bath. Exterior Basic is a foam bath and towel dry — fast and straightforward.

The detail

Detail services

Times below are typical. We’ll confirm what’s right for your car when you book or walk in.

45–60 minutes

Express Clean & Maintain

A high-efficiency refresh for regular upkeep. Interior: dual-action vacuum of seats and carpets, a light wipe of high-touch dash and console surfaces, and streak-free windows. Exterior: premium foam-cannon wash, microfiber towel dry, clean wheel faces, and tire shine.

2–3 hours

Complete Interior Deep Clean

A cabin-focused restoration for families, pets, or a car that’s being sold. Hot-water extraction and thermal shampooing of fabric seats, carpets, and mats; deep cleaning of vinyl, plastic, and cup holders with a non-greasy UV finish; pH-balanced leather clean and condition; HVAC vent blowout and an odor-neutralizer treatment.

2–3 hours

Complete Exterior Showroom Shine

Advanced paint decontamination and high-gloss protection. Multi-stage scratch-free hand wash including door jambs, trunk seals, and the gas cap; clay-bar decontamination; a single-stage machine gloss polish and a hand-applied hydrophobic polymer sealant; deep brake-dust removal from inner rim barrels, a wheel-well flush, and tire armor.

4–6 hours

The Executive Bumper-to-Bumper Reset

Our signature package: everything in the complete interior and complete exterior services, plus engine-bay detailing and a six-month ceramic spray coating upgrade. Built for a total transformation.

Add-ons

À la carte services

Customize a package or target one problem area.

  • Engine bay detail — degrease, rinse, and dress plastic casings and hoses.
  • Headlight restoration — multi-stage wet sanding and buffing, finished with a UV clear-coat blocker.
  • Pet hair heavy extraction — specialized brushing to lift woven dander and fur from carpets.
  • Ceramic coating upgrades — longer-term paint protection, from shorter spray coatings up to multi-year professional systems.
  • Fabric and carpet stain shield — a hydrophobic layer to help spills sit on the surface instead of soaking in.
