Eight ways to stay on Block Island.

From the 1852 main house to townhomes and seaside rentals — ocean-view hotel rooms and suites, luxury apartments, and whole homes, all under one roofline of hospitality.

Home/Stay

32 guest rooms · est. 1852

Spring House Main Building

The Spring House Hotel is Block Island’s oldest and largest hotel. Built in 1852, the main building includes 32 guest rooms that preserve the island’s authentic New England charm — Standard Kings and Queens, Doubles, Studios, Suites, and Deluxe Suites, with panoramic island or ocean views and air conditioning throughout. Come enjoy a true New England Victorian experience.

17 guest rooms · in the gardens

The Mott House

Directly across from the main building, the Mott House offers 17 guest rooms — Standard Queens, Studios, and Suites with island or ocean views, air conditioning, and private bathrooms for every room. Nestled in the Spring House gardens with a farmer’s porch and rocking chairs, it’s a quieter retreat with a breathtaking ocean view.

Book Now

Adults 21+ · two guests per room

The Inn at the Spring House

Unlike any other inn on Block Island, the Inn at Spring House is impeccably furnished and fully air-conditioned, with private baths and scenic island views. Rooms are unique — many include rain-head showers and private balconies — and every bed is fitted with luxurious cotton sheets atop heavenly mattresses. The Inn truly is something special.

Please keep in mind this inn is for adults 21 years of age or older, with a limit of two people per room.

10 custom suites · 585 Beach Avenue

The Spring House in New Harbor

Be one of the first guests at the newest hotel on Block Island. Located at 585 Beach Avenue, the Spring House in New Harbor is the place to stay — ten custom suites offering an upscale experience with the finest furnishings and services.

Book Now

3 luxury apartments · open year-round

Barn Apartments

The Barn sits right on the Spring House property, with three luxury apartments on its upper levels. Each provides a dining area with a full-size refrigerator, microwave, toaster, coffee pot, dishes, and glassware, plus flat-screen televisions with full DIRECTV. Complete with central air, the Barn is open year-round — downstairs, it hosts a full restaurant in the off-season featuring rustic farm-to-table cuisine.

Book Now

3 townhouses · sleep 4–6

Seawinds Rentals

Located on High Street, the Seawinds townhomes are a five-minute walk from the hotel and fifteen minutes from downtown. Each of the three townhouses provides three bedrooms, two and a half baths, a full kitchen, air conditioning, and washer and dryer units — with panoramic island views and beautiful hardwood floors.

Book Now

Ocean-view homes · Wickford is pet friendly

Seaside Homes

Aptly named Chaffee, Bristol, and Wickford, the Seaside Homes sit right on the property with ocean views — popular with larger families and bridal parties. Each home provides three bedrooms, three full baths, a full kitchen, and washer and dryer units. One of the homes, Wickford, is pet-friendly.
