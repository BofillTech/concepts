Block Island, Rhode Island · Est. 1852

“The place to stay on Block Island.”

A shining white landmark on a green lawn above the Atlantic — guest rooms, rental homes, two restaurants, and a bar and lounge, welcoming guests for more than 170 years.

Explore the Collection

Welcome to the Spring House

Block Island’s oldest and largest hotel.

Built in 1852, the Spring House preserves the island’s authentic New England charm — a true Victorian experience with panoramic island and ocean views, a sprawling lawn overlooking the sea, and a collection of rooms, inns, and rental homes around it.
