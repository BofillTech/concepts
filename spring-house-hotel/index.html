Welcome to the Spring House

Block Island’s oldest and largest hotel.

Built in 1852, the Spring House preserves the island’s authentic New England charm — a true Victorian experience with panoramic island and ocean views, a sprawling lawn overlooking the sea, and seven distinct ways to stay.
