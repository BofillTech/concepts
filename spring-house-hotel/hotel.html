About & History

An elegant, historic landmark.

Built in 1852, the Spring House Hotel is Block Island’s oldest and largest hotel — a red mansard roof, a wraparound veranda, and a distinctive cupola on a 10-acre promontory above the Atlantic.

Home/About & History

Our Story

The best ocean breezes on Block Island.

Yankee Magazine called the Spring House home to “the best ocean breezes on Block Island” and one of the island’s most elegant historic landmarks. The 10-acre promontory the hotel sits on creates its most beloved feature: an unbeatable view of the Atlantic Ocean and Block Island’s rolling foothills. With relaxing Adirondack chairs lining the crest of the undulating lawn, many island visitors find themselves stopping in for a signature cocktail.

History & Tradition

A colorful 170-year history.

The Spring House Hotel is considered one of Block Island’s most elegant historic landmarks. With its wraparound veranda, mansard roof, distinctive cupola, and breathtaking view of the Atlantic Ocean and Block Island’s foothills, it has welcomed guests since 1852.

Throughout its colorful 170-year history, the island’s oldest hotel and resort has hosted notable guests, such as Ulysses S. Grant, Mark Twain, and Billy Joel. The Spring House Hotel has even provided the formal setting for a Kennedy wedding in its recent past.

Accommodations for Everyone

Over 55 hotel rooms — and so much more.

With over 55 hotel accommodations, three on-site apartments, three on-property guest homes, and off-site townhomes, there are accommodation types available for every party. The property’s most recent expansion is the Inn at Spring House, an adults-only luxury accommodation located just down the driveway from the Main Building.

Family Managed for 30+ Years

Old-world charm, constant care.

The current family has managed the property for over 30 years. Though changes and upgrades are constantly in motion, maintaining its famous old-world charm is the top priority. An important mission has become farm-to-table dining: three gardens on the hotel property are in a constant state of bloom, and the produce is reflected daily in our menus.

Getting Here

A short walk uphill from Old Harbor.

The Spring House Hotel is just a short walk uphill from Old Harbor, making it easy to get to and from the ferry and shops. Check in on the island anytime from the Cupola Cam.
