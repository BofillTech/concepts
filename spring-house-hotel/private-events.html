Weddings

Green grass, a shining white building, a deep blue ocean.

A wedding on Block Island is an unforgettable experience. Just a short ferry ride away, the island provides the perfect small getaway for you and your guests — and the Spring House provides the most picturesque backdrop, one your photographs will carry for years to come.

Imagine exchanging vows on the lawn, spending your cocktail hour on the wraparound Seaside Veranda, enjoying a formal meal in the Dining Room, and dancing the night away in Victoria’s Parlor. Whatever your dream wedding may be, our entire staff will go above and beyond to ensure a beautiful event.

Indoor & Outdoor

Venues to capture your vision.

Charming indoor and outdoor spaces for ceremonies, receptions, rehearsal dinners, and private functions.

The Front Lawn

One of the Spring House Hotel’s most distinguishing features is the front lawn. White Adirondack chairs are aligned at the crest of our signature hill. On any given summer evening, children are rolling down the hill as adults relax with a cocktail. With the Atlantic Ocean as the backdrop, our front lawn is a popular venue for outdoor ceremonies. A more beautiful setting cannot be imagined.

Seaside Veranda

The Seaside Veranda is the Spring House Hotel’s landmark feature. A partially covered, wraparound porch is the perfect setting for enjoying cocktails and hors d’oeuvres. It features an outdoor bar and plenty of space for guests to mingle. The view from the veranda cannot be beat.

East Overlook Patio

The East Overlook Patio is a perfect site for your welcome party, rehearsal dinner, or wedding reception. Imagine a cocktail party or seated meal in this tented space overlooking the ocean with your closest friends and family.

Grand Dining Room

The Dining Room is a classic space overlooking the Atlantic Ocean. Picture your private dinner or corporate function here, with a fresh breeze from our French doors or choose to take advantage of the air-conditioning. The Dining Room also features an intimate lounge area with a stone fireplace.

Victoria’s Parlor

The luxurious Victoria’s Parlor features a beautiful oak bar, along with a stage. This room is most commonly reserved for receptions. With plenty of room for dancing, sitting, and even a designated spot for performers, this classic room will accommodate all of your guests.

Meetings & Conferences

An oceanfront setting for your next event.

If you are considering a breathtaking oceanfront location for your upcoming conference or event, you’ve come to the right place. Our professionally experienced catering team coordinates meetings, breaks, buffets, as well as cocktail receptions and formal dining events. We have extensive lawns for outdoor activities — a wonderful getaway from the work event.

We offer our Sunroom, Dining Room, and Ocean View Terrace as private spaces. All meeting spaces are charged a facility fee, which includes chairs, tables, linens, projection screens, and extension cords. Any audio or visual needs are charged additional fees.
