Our award-winning eateries.

Two restaurants and a bar and lounge with ocean views and fresh, seasonal, farm-to-table menus — the produce from our three on-property gardens is reflected daily.

Spring House Main Restaurant

Enjoy the highest quality and freshest cuisine. The extensive menu features Prime meats and cuts, wonderful local fish dishes, and an extensive wine list that will be sure to impress. You’ll find a perfect seat on our wraparound veranda, tented East Overlook Patio, or inside Victoria’s Parlor.

Hours may change due to private events. Reservations are a must.

The Barn Restaurant

The Barn is one of Block Island’s best-kept secrets. This unique dining venue features an open kitchen and a wood-fired grill, creating wonderful flavors that cannot be duplicated at home. Our culinary staff cooking right in front of you and the intimate ambiance make for a perfect evening — a quiet dining experience designed for adults only.

Reservations are highly recommended and credit cards may be taken to hold your time. A $50 charge applies if a reservation is not canceled within an hour of your scheduled arrival. Hours are subject to change due to private events.

The Coffee Bar

The Coffee Bar features Illy Italian coffee. Open daily, the café offers fresh-squeezed juice made from vegetables grown in our own garden, homemade pastries, Illy espresso, and even egg sandwiches in the morning.
