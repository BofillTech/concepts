The Offer

Buy $1,500 worth of gift cards — receive a complimentary $300 gift card. Online purchase ONLY.

Purchase Gift Cards Online

How To Purchase

Visit the Spring House gift card store and add $1,500 worth of gift cards. The complimentary $300 gift card automatically populates and is taken off. You can send multiple cards to multiple people.

Gift Card Details

  • One offer per person.
  • Offer valid Monday, November 30, 2026 — online purchases from 12:00 AM to 11:59 PM.
  • Purchases must be made online; email and phone orders will not be accepted.
  • Gift cards may be purchased in denominations of $100, $200, $250, $300 or $500, totaling exactly $1,500.
  • Online purchases result in e-gift cards; the complimentary $300 e-gift card is emailed to the purchaser.
  • Gift cards are usable at the on-site restaurants and toward hotel rooms in the Main Building, Mott House, Barn Apartments, the Inn at Spring House, and the Spring House in New Harbor.
  • Gift cards are not usable toward the nightly rate and taxes for the Seaside Rental Homes or Seawinds Rental Townhomes, or toward event billing.
  • Lost or stolen gift cards will not be replaced and are not exchangeable for cash.
