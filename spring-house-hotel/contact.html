Good to Know

Frequently asked questions.

Where is the Spring House Hotel?

At 52 Spring Street on Block Island, Rhode Island — a short walk uphill from the Old Harbor ferry landing.

How do I get to Block Island?

Most guests take the Block Island Ferry from Point Judith, Rhode Island (traditional and high-speed service); seasonal ferries also run from Newport, New London, and Montauk, and New England Airlines flies from Westerly. The hotel is a short walk uphill from the Old Harbor landing.

Do I need a car on Block Island?

No. The hotel is an easy walk from Old Harbor's ferry, shops, and restaurants, and in season a complimentary shuttle connects the properties and Town Beach.

Is parking available?

Yes — parking is complimentary at all Spring House properties.

When is the hotel open?

The Inn at Spring House, the Barn Apartments, the Seaside Homes, the Seawinds Townhouses, and the Cooneymus Cottage welcome guests year-round. The Main Building, Mott House, and the Spring House in New Harbor are open seasonally, roughly mid-May through mid-October.

How many ways are there to stay?

Eight: the 1852 Main Building, the Mott House, the adults-only Inn at Spring House, the Spring House in New Harbor, the Barn Apartments, the Seawinds Townhouses, the Seaside Homes, and the Cooneymus Cottage.

Which properties are adults-only?

The Inn at Spring House and the Spring House in New Harbor are adults-only. Guests must be at least 21 to reserve any accommodation; children are welcome at the Main Building and the Barn Apartments.

Is breakfast included?

Guests of the Inn at Spring House and the Spring House in New Harbor receive a complimentary à la carte breakfast and coffee. Guests elsewhere can purchase breakfast in season at The Coffee Bar near the Main Building Front Desk, open daily from 7 to 11 am.

Do the rooms have air conditioning and Wi-Fi?

Yes — every guest room is air-conditioned with a private bathroom, and Wi-Fi is complimentary across the properties.

Are accessible rooms available?

ADA-accessible guest rooms are available at the Inn at Spring House and the Spring House in New Harbor, which also has elevator access. Contact the Front Desk at 401-466-5844 about accessible options in the Main Building.

Is there a free shuttle to the beach?

Yes — in season, a complimentary shuttle runs every hour between the Spring House Hotel, Town Beach, and the Spring House in New Harbor. Complimentary beach towels are available at the Front Desk. Shuttle service is seasonal and subject to change.

Where can I eat at the Spring House?

The adults-only Barn Restaurant serves dinner Wednesday through Saturday from 5 to 9 pm. The Main Restaurant and The Coffee Bar will see you in spring 2027.

Can I make a dinner reservation?

Yes — reservations for The Barn are recommended; call 401-466-5844. The restaurant serves dinner Wednesday through Saturday from 5 to 9 pm.

Is the food farm-to-table?

Yes — three on-site gardens supply fresh ingredients for seasonal dishes across the kitchens.

Are pets allowed?

Pets are generally not accommodated in the hotel buildings. Certain rental homes — including the pet-friendly Wickford Seaside Home — can welcome dogs by prior arrangement, with cleaning fees set out in the rental agreement. Service animals are always welcome and should be declared at check-in.

What is the cancellation policy?

For guest rooms, cancellations more than 14 days before arrival are refunded minus a $75 fee; within 14 days, the reservation is non-refundable. Rental homes and townhomes are non-cancellable with non-refundable deposits. Travel insurance is encouraged — Block Island travel depends on the ferry. Call 401-466-5844 with questions.

How do I book a room?

Book direct through the hotel's online booking engine — every Book button on this site goes straight there — or call 401-466-5844.

What are check-in and check-out times?

Hotel rooms: check-in from 4 pm, check-out by 11 am. Rental homes and townhomes: check-in from 5 pm, check-out by 10 am.

Can I host a wedding or private event there?

Yes — oceanfront ceremonies on the Great Lawn and receptions in four venues, from intimate gatherings to tented celebrations. Molly and the events team handle every detail; inquire through the wedding form.

What is there to do at the hotel?

Sweeping ocean views from the lawn and veranda, landscaped grounds with outdoor seating, live music throughout the week in season, three gardens to wander, and Town Beach an hourly shuttle ride away.

Does the hotel sell gift cards?

Yes — digital gift cards are available online through the hotel's Givex store, usable at the restaurants and toward hotel rooms.

Can I see the island before I arrive?

Yes — the Cupola Cam streams the view from the hotel's cupola on the Webcam page.
